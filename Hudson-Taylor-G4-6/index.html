Develop Your Details

Turn thin details into full D+E

Hudson, your Q11 paragraph has the right frame. The issue is that your supporting details state a fact but do not explain why it matters. Here is how to develop them.

Tap to compare thin vs. developed details
Side by side: what yours looks like vs. what earns full points
THIN (what yours looks like)
"Inventors are important because they make things that help people. They are important s..."
DEVELOPED (full D+E)
"Inventors are important because they notice problems that hurt people and create solutions. For example, Garrett Morgan saw that cars crashed at intersections because there were no signals, so he invented the traffic signal. This shows that one inventor paying attention can save thousands of lives for years to come."
THE D+E FORMULA
Detail: State the example or reason (what happened, who did it).

Explanation: Add "This shows..." or "This matters because..." and explain WHY the detail is important or what it proves about the topic.

Every supporting detail in your paragraph needs both parts. If you only have the Detail, you earn partial credit. With both, you earn full credit.
