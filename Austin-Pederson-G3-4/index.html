Question 11 — Building a Full Paragraph

Turn your one sentence into a complete paragraph
Which is the best topic sentence for a paragraph about how modern sports developed?
A Sports are popular.
B Modern sports developed from simple activities into organized competitions that people all over the world enjoy.

Here is a model paragraph that shows how to expand your one sentence into a full response:

Paragraph Blueprint
TOPIC Modern sports developed from simple activities into organized competitions that people all over the world enjoy.
DETAIL 1 According to the passage, early sports began as games that people played for exercise and fun in their communities.
DETAIL 2 Over time, rules were created so that everyone played the same way, and teams began to compete against each other.
DETAIL 3 The passage also explains that as more people became interested, sports events grew bigger and attracted large audiences.
CLOSING This shows that modern sports became popular because they brought people together through competition and excitement.
THE EVIDENCE SANDWICH
1. Set it up: Write a sentence that introduces your evidence.
2. Evidence: Use specific details from the passage (quoted or paraphrased).
3. Explain: Tell the reader why this evidence matters or what it shows.
YOUR ACTION PLAN FOR NEXT TIME
When you see Question 11, follow these steps:
1. Write a topic sentence that answers the question.
2. Write 2–3 detail sentences using information from the passage.
3. Write a closing sentence that wraps up your main point.
4. Check: Do I have at least 4 sentences? Did I use evidence from the passage?
SO WHAT?
You already showed you understand the main idea when you wrote your one sentence. Now you just need to stretch it into a full paragraph. That one change could add 10+ points to your next test.
