Spelling and Run-on Sentences

Q6 and Q10 lost points to mechanics
Q6 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"The porcupine's defence mechanisim is their quills which protect them."

Two problems:

  • Spelling: "Defence" should be "defense" (American spelling) and "mechanisim" should be "mechanism"
  • Circular reasoning: The question asks what the defense mechanism is, and you answer "their defense mechanism is their quills which protect them." This repeats the question without adding new information.
STRONGER ANSWER
"Porcupines protect themselves with sharp quills that cover their backs and tails. When a predator gets too close, the quills stick into the animal and cause it to back away. This shows that porcupines do not need to be fast because their quills do the work for them."
Q10 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"Porcupines live in forests and they eat bark and plants and they sleep during the day and they come out at night to find food."

The problem: This is a run-on sentence. You connected four ideas with "and...and...and..." which makes it hard to follow. Break it into shorter sentences.

FIXED VERSION
"Porcupines live in forests where they eat bark and plants. They sleep during the day and come out at night to find food. This shows that they are nocturnal animals that are active when predators are less likely to see them."
THE RUN-ON FIX
If your sentence has more than two "and" connections, break it up. Use a period after the first complete idea, then start a new sentence. This makes your writing clearer and earns more points.
