Read It, Close It, Type It

Twenty of your answers still carry the marks of a paste
THE RULE
Read it, close it, type it. Read the prompt sentence. Type the new sentence yourself, from the first word. Never paste. Why: a pasted sentence keeps its old capital, old end mark and old words, and your eye slides past them.

The Read-Back Five

Five checks, ten seconds, before every submit
THE RULE
The read-back five. Before you submit, read your answer once and check:
1. Capital I.
2. The end mark matches: a question gets ?, a command or statement gets ., an exclamation gets !
3. After a comma, a small letter, unless it is a name or I.
4. The pairs: where / wear, its / it’s, to / too.
5. Two thoughts, two sentences.

You know every one of these rules. You read fast and type fast, so the hand moves before the rule gets a turn. The read-back is where the rule gets its turn. One of yours for each check:

You are rushing on quiz items. How to take a quiz item without rushing:

  1. Read the prompt twice. Find the job word: question, command, appositive, combine.
  2. Type the answer from the first word.
  3. Run the read-back five.
  4. Submit.

Aim to spend at least 45 seconds on a quiz item, and at least 15 minutes on an outline.
