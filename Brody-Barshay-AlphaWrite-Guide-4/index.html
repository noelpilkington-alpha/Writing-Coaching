Count the Parts

Set 4 of 5. The paragraph that answers a question about a passage
THE RULE
Count the parts. Answer every part. And, each, why, what happens: each one adds a part. One sentence per part at least. Then the topic sentence does two jobs. It answers, it does not ask: a statement in the question’s words. That is the point. And it names what your details will be about, one supporting detail for each thing it names. That is the how.

A Detail You Can Point To

The passage already has the words. Your job is to find them
THE RULE
A detail is a word you can point to in the passage. Find the noun in the passage that answers the part. Put it in your sentence. If you cannot put your finger on it, it is a gist, not a detail.

Fact, Then the Link

A fact alone is a list. A fact with its link is an answer
THE RULE
Fact, then the link: so, because, which. Every supporting detail has a fact from the passage and a link that says what the fact does for the question.
