THE "ONE IDEA" RULE
When the prompt says "Write one sentence," plan your sentence this way:

1. Pick the one idea you want to say.
2. Say it clearly with a main clause and ONE "because" or "so" clause.
3. Aim for 20–30 words. If you are pushing 40+ words, you are packing in a second idea.

If you have two great ideas, pick the stronger one. The other can wait.

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20/20 level)

Many business experts believe a recognizable logo is valuable because of gist memory and familiarity. According to the passage, the feeling of familiarity makes people feel good about buying or using a product. This shows that customers do not need to recall every detail of a logo to trust it — they only need to recognize its key shapes and colors. Most people could not draw a brand's logo perfectly from memory, but they can still pick it out of a lineup instantly. This shows that a logo is doing its job even when a customer is not paying close attention. For these reasons, the trust a logo builds is exactly why so many business experts see real value in a strong, simple design.
