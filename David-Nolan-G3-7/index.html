THE THREE-PART SENTENCE
Every Q6–Q10 answer is one sentence worth 3 points. The grader looks for three pieces:

1. The fact or opinion (what you think or what happens).
2. The reason word: because, so, or since.
3. The specific reason from the passage that backs it up.

Miss the reason word and you land at 2 of 3 — which is exactly what happened five times on this test.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Q6–Q10 is worth 15 points total. You scored 10 of 15. Adding one "because + reason from the passage" to each answer is worth up to 5 full points — that alone is the move from 83.7% to 90%+.

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20 OF 20)

Learning to cook can help children in many useful ways. One reason cooking is useful is that it teaches kids how to feed themselves when they grow up. This shows that cooking is a life skill they will use forever, not just a fun hobby. Cooking also teaches safety, like how to stay away from hot pans and how to use knives carefully. This matters because these habits keep kids from getting hurt in the kitchen. Another way cooking helps is by teaching kids to understand recipes, which also helps them in school with reading directions and math. This is important because the same skill works in science class and at home. Not everyone will be a chef, but learning to cook helps kids grow up smart, safe, and creative.

DETAIL 2 A second specific reason
SHOWS "This matters because..." — explain detail 2
CLOSE Wrap the central idea in a new way (yours is already strong)
