The Outline Shape

Unit 3 of 4. Your facts are the best part. The shape is the gap
THE RULE, THREE LINES
The topic sentence answers, it does not ask. It is a statement that answers the question in the prompt. The best ones also say what the notes will be about: Roman swords were sharp weapons that soldiers kept safe in a holder promises notes on sharp, and on kept safe.
Notes are key words, not sentences. If it could stand on its own with a period, it is a sentence, not a note.
One sentence, no new fact. The concluding sentence names what the notes were about and says what they add up to. If it adds a fact, the fact moves up into the notes.

The Sentence Test

Three questions, then the count
THE RULE
Find the action word. Find the doer. Is the thought finished? All three there: a sentence. Any one missing: a fragment. Long does not mean finished, and short does not mean unfinished.
WHEN YOU EXPAND A FRAGMENT
One thing: is. More than one: are. After did: the plain verb. Point at the doer and count it before you pick the verb.
