A few answers stopped before they were full sentences

Hope, here is one of your answers. The idea is good — it just is not a full sentence yet:

YOU WROTE (G3.4, Question 6)
"That she does the right thing."

Read it out loud. It feels like it is waiting for something, right? That is because a complete sentence needs two parts:

THE TWO PARTS OF A COMPLETE SENTENCE
1. A naming part — who or what the sentence is about (like Layla).
2. A doing part — what they do (like does the right thing).

Put them together and it can stand on its own: "Layla does the right thing."
Which one is a complete sentence that can stand on its own?
A That she does the right thing.
B Layla does the right thing because she gave the crystal to the guide.

TOPIC Say what your paragraph is about (Sports became popular over time.)
DETAIL 1 One fact from the passage (people wrote down rules)
DETAIL 2 One more fact from the passage (teams formed, fans watched)
CLOSING One sentence to finish your idea
