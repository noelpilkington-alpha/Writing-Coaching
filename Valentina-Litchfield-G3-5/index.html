THE SPELLING — LOCK IT IN
BE + CAUSE = BECAUSE

Think of it as two small words glued together: BE (like "be nice") + CAUSE (like "the cause of it").

✓ BE-CAUSE = BECAUSE
✗ BE-CUSE = not a word
Pick the correct spelling.
A I'm happy becuse the sun is shining.
B I'm happy because the sun is shining.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
You used "becuse" 10+ times across 5 tests. Each one is a half-point partial. On your proofread pass, search every answer for the letters "becuse" and fix every single one. This is the fastest single upgrade you can make. BE + CAUSE = BECAUSE.

FROM G3.4 Q6 — SCORED 0.33

(General claim, no evidence from the passage. This is exactly why the score was only 0.33.)
The Full-Credit Opinion Answer Formula
PART 1 Answer the question (yes/no, who, what)
PART 2 "Because..." + one specific reason from the passage
Prompt: "If you met a friendly dog, how would you feel? Why?" Which answer earns full credit?
A I would be happy, and pet it!
B I would be happy because the dog looks friendly and is wagging its tail.
YOUR TEST ANSWERS, FULL-CREDIT VERSIONS
• G3.1 Q6: "I would be happy because the dog looks friendly, and I would pet it."
• G3.2 Q7: "Nothing, because the story already has a clear beginning, middle, and end."
• G3.4 Q6: "Layla seems happy because the passage says she smiled at every flower and skipped through the garden."
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Every opinion question on Q6–Q10 has two parts: the answer and the reason. If your answer doesn't have "because..." followed by a detail from the passage, you lose half credit. One word — "because" — turns a 0.33 into a 1.0. Every single time.
