Q4 (1/2): The question asked you to add an appositive. You wrote "to see the pretty state flower," which is a purpose phrase (it tells WHY), not an appositive (which renames or describes a noun).

YOUR ANSWER
"...to see the pretty state flower"
AN APPOSITIVE
"...the bluebonnet, the official state flower of Texas, blooms every spring."

An appositive renames or describes the noun right next to it. It is set off by commas. "The official state flower of Texas" renames "bluebonnet."
Which phrase is an appositive?
A"...to see the beautiful wildflowers in the field"
B"The bluebonnet, a wildflower famous for its bright blue petals, grows across Texas."
APPOSITIVE SHORTCUT
An appositive answers "What is it?" or "Who is this?" right after the noun. If your phrase answers "Why?" or "Where?" or "How?" — it is not an appositive.

Q9 (1/3): Your answer has a run-on sentence AND does not connect back to the question being asked. Two separate problems:

FIX 2: CONNECT TO THE QUESTION

Q10 (2/3): Your answer is correct but too brief for 3 points. You gave one sentence when the question needs 2 to 3.

THE FIX FOR BRIEF ANSWERS
For any 3-point question: answer + explain WHY + give a detail. That is 2 to 3 sentences and earns full marks.
A 3-point question asks about robots in the workplace. Which answer earns full points?
A"Robots can help people at work."
B"Robots can help people at work by doing boring or dangerous tasks that humans do not want to do. This means workers can focus on the parts of their job that need creativity and problem-solving."
YOUR RULE
3 points = 2+ sentences. Always. Answer the question, then explain or give a detail from the passage.
