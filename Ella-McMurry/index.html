Q8 — Which sentence best adds elaboration?

YOU CHOSE

This just defines immersion — it doesn't add new information. Elaboration means adding specific detail that develops the idea further.

CORRECT ANSWER
In immersion classes, students must use the new language all day, which helps them learn vocabulary faster.

See the difference? This tells us what happens in immersion classes and what it leads to. That's elaboration.

Q9 — Which transition best connects contrasting sentences?

YOU CHOSE
Although some experts think older students learn better...
CORRECT ANSWER
While some experts think older students learn better...
ALTHOUGH vs WHILE
Both words can show contrast, but they work differently:
"While" = two views exist side by side (balanced contrast)
"Although" = one view is a concession that leads to a stronger point

When presenting two equal but opposing opinions ("some experts say X, others say Y"), "While" is the better fit because it treats both sides equally.

Capitalization & Tricky Words

Small errors that add up fast

Capitalization — proper nouns always get a capital letter:

spanish → Spanish
french → French
americans → Americans

Languages and nationalities are always capitalized because they come from the names of countries.

Homophones — words that sound the same but mean different things:

You wrote Should be Why
...pratice there language their language "their" = belonging to them
...speak they language they are learning ...speak the language they are learning "they" is a pronoun, not an article

Two more quick fixes:

everyday → every day (two words when it means "each day")
childrens' → children's ("children" is already plural — add 's)
QUICK CHECK
Before you submit, scan for: language names (capitalize), there/their/they're (pick the right one), and possessives (children's, not childrens').

Sharpen Your Thesis

Your intro has a great hook — now it needs a roadmap
Your Introduction
At a busy local café, people speak a variety of languages. At one table a family talk in spanish. At the coffee counter, a worker was singing along to a french song on the radio. Scenes like this happen everyday all over the United States, a country built by immigrants. English may be the most common school language, millions of americans grew up using two languages at home.

Your opening scene is excellent — it's vivid and draws the reader in. But the highlighted sentence has two problems:

  • Comma splice — two complete ideas joined by just a comma. You need a period, semicolon, or conjunction ("but" or "yet").
  • No thesis — this sentence doesn't tell the reader what your essay will be about. A strong thesis previews your three body paragraph topics.
STRONGER ENDING TO YOUR INTRO
Although English is the most common school language, millions of Americans grow up using two at home. Children can develop strong language skills through an early start, immersion classes, and real-life communication with native speakers.
THE THESIS FORMULA
[Main idea] + [Point 1], [Point 2], and [Point 3].

This gives the reader a roadmap. They know exactly what's coming in each body paragraph. It also keeps you organized while writing.

The Body Paragraph Pattern

You're close — here's the full structure

Your body paragraphs already have topic sentences and evidence. To earn full points, make the explanation layer more explicit. Here's your Paragraph 3 rewritten with the full pattern:

STRONGER BODY PARAGRAPH (IMMERSION)

TS: A good way to help children learn a new language is through immersion classes.

E1: According to the passage, in immersion schools, teachers use the chosen language all day during lessons, conversations, and even simple requests like asking to go to the bathroom.

X1: This shows that students aren't just learning the language in a textbook — they're hearing and using it constantly, which makes it feel natural.

E2: The passage also explains that because students use the language daily, they pick up phrases and grammar quickly.

X2: This means that immersion works because practice is built into every part of the school day, not just one class period.

CS: Because of this constant exposure, immersion is one of the most effective ways to learn a new language.

MEMORIZE THIS STRUCTURE
TS → Topic Sentence
E1 → Evidence from the passage
X1 → Explanation (This shows that...)
E2 → More evidence from the passage
X2 → Explanation (This means that...)
CS → Concluding Sentence

TS → E1 → X1 → E2 → X2 → CS — use it for all three body paragraphs.
