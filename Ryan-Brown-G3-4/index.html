MODEL PARAGRAPH (what 18/20 looks like)

Modern sports developed because people had more free time to play, and they became popular as schools and communities created places to play them. According to the article, wealthy people in the 1800s had time to invent games and make rules for them. Later, schools began building fields and organizing teams so students could play too. As more people watched and joined in, sports grew into something almost everyone knew. That is how modern sports went from a rich-person hobby to something the whole society shares.

The 4-Part Paragraph Shape (same as last time)
TOPIC State your main idea. Turn the prompt into a sentence. (You nailed this.)
DETAIL 1 ONE specific thing from the article on its OWN line
DETAIL 2 A second specific thing from the article on its OWN line
CLOSING Wrap back to your main idea in new words
Your topic sentence is strong. What is the ONE thing it still needs?
A Stuff one more reason into the topic sentence.

THE "ONE IDEA PER SENTENCE" TRICK
If a sentence has the word "and" joining two big reasons, break it into two sentences. Your original had "rich people AND schools making fields" — that is two ideas fighting for one sentence. Let each idea have its own sentence and give it room to breathe.
