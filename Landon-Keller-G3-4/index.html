The Proofread Pass

A slow read-back catches spelling and missing words

Landon, you already know the content. The points you lost to spelling and missing words are points you can get back by doing one simple thing: reading your answer back to yourself slowly before moving on.

HOW TO DO A PROOFREAD PASS
After you finish writing an answer, do not move to the next question yet. Instead:

Step 1: Point at the first word of your answer (with your finger or your eyes).

Step 2: Read each word one at a time. Say it in your head. Does it look right? Does it sound complete?

Step 3: If something looks strange (like "decsion" or "develpoded"), try rewriting it. If a sentence sounds choppy (like "feel like perfect souvenir"), you probably skipped a small word.
