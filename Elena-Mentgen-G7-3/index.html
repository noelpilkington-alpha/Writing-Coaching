Fix 1: Five Paragraphs, One Job Each

Your facts, sorted into the essay the prompt asked for
THE RULE: FIVE PARAGRAPHS, ONE JOB EACH
Introduction: a general statement about the topic, a specific statement that narrows to this passage, then the thesis, which answers the question in the question’s own words. Body 1, 2, 3: one connection each, opened by a topic sentence, then two supporting details (a fact from the passage, then This shows). Conclusion: the same road back: the thesis in new words, a specific statement, a general statement. The check: count your paragraphs before you submit. Fewer than five, and five points are gone before a word is graded.
