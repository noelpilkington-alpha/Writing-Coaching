Step 4: Your Answers State Facts but Don't Explain WHY

Q6, Q7, and Q9 all lost the same point

James, three questions on this test lost a point for the same reason: you stated a correct fact but did not explain why it matters or how it works.

Q6 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"Pumpkins need warm soil and that is why they get planted in spring."

The problem: "That is why they get planted" is circular. It restates the fact without explaining WHY warm soil is needed. What does warm soil actually do for pumpkins?

A STRONGER ANSWER
"Pumpkins need warm soil because their seeds will not sprout in cold ground. Warm soil helps the roots grow quickly so the plant can get strong before summer."
Q7 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"Pollen helps pumpkins grow."

The problem: This is true but it does not explain HOW pollen helps. What does pollen actually do? The passage tells you.

A STRONGER ANSWER
"Pollen helps pumpkins grow because bees carry it from one flower to another. This allows the plant to make fruit, which is how pumpkins form."

If your answer does not have a "because" or "This shows," it is probably missing the WHY.
