Four complete ideas in one sentence.
FROM G3.4 Q11 (Sports and Society)
"Sports are very inportant they let you have energy and fun."
Two ideas, no period between them.
Which fix turns this into a strong pair of sentences?
A Sports are very important they let you have energy and fun.
B Sports are very important. They let people have energy and fun.
THE RULE
Every sentence should have one complete idea. When you read your sentence back, if it feels like you need to take a breath in the middle, split it.

Two ways to fix a run-on:
1. Add a period and start a new sentence.
2. Add a joining word: and, but, so, because.
THE "PAUSE TEST"
Read each sentence out loud quietly to yourself. If you naturally pause in the middle, that is where a period belongs. Your ear knows where sentences end — listen to it.
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q11 is worth 20 of your 45 total points. Splitting the run-ons into clean sentences is worth 2–3 points every single test, and it makes the whole paragraph easier to read.
