Question 11 — Spelling & Grammar

Small errors that cost you points

Jaya, your ideas are strong, but there are a few spelling and grammar mistakes that are easy to fix. Here are the ones to watch for:

SPELLING ERRORS

1. "a teacher soley uses the new language"

2. "and therefor push up the number"

3. "people can speak in multible languages"

CORRECTED

1. "a teacher solely uses the new language"

2. "and therefore push up the number"

3. "people can speak in multiple languages"

MISSING APOSTROPHES

1. "this can help students brains" → "this can help students' brains"

2. "beneficial to a students brain" → "beneficial to a student's brain"

3. "improve a students experience" → "improve a student's experience"

APOSTROPHE RULE
When something belongs to someone, add an apostrophe:
• One student → a student's brain (the brain of one student)
• Many students → students' brains (the brains of many students)
Without the apostrophe, it reads like a different word entirely.
PROOFREADING TIP
Before you submit, read your essay backward — start with the last sentence and work up. This forces you to see each sentence on its own, which makes spelling errors jump out. On this essay, catching these 6 errors could have earned you the full 20/20.
