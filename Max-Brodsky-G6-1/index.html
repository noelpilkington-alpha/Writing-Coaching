You list great facts — now say what each one shows

YOUR ESSAY, POLISHED (spelling, capitals, and the final sentence fixed)

Wilma Rudolph persevered through hard times to become a champion. As a child she had polio and had to wear a leg brace, and she could not walk on her own until she was 12. This shows how much she had to overcome before she ever reached a track.

Once she could walk, she joined the track team and trained for years. In 1960 she competed in the Olympic Games in Rome, winning three gold medals in track and field. This proves that years of hard work can overcome even a serious disability.

After her racing career, this famous athlete became a coach and a speaker, and she went to college. Wilma told her story to remind people that they can reach their dreams if they try hard enough.
