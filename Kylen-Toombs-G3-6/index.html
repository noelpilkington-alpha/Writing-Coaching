From G3.6 Q2 — the prompt said to combine the sentences using "because." Which answer follows the directions?
A They have strong claws and rough paw pads so they can hold on to tree branches tightly.
B They can hold on to tree branches tightly because they have strong claws and rough paw pads.
THE RULE
When a prompt tells you exactly what to do — use this word, make it about this topic, write a complete sentence — your answer must do that exact thing. The prompt is not a suggestion. It is the grading rubric.

Three checks before you submit an answer:
1. Did I use the exact word the prompt named (because, since, or)?
2. Is my answer about the right topic from the passage?
3. Is my answer a complete sentence (not starting with "Because" or "Since" alone)?
WHY THIS IS THE FIX FOR RUSHING
All three of these errors happened because you didn't re-read the prompt. You got the idea right but skipped the directions. A slow re-read of the prompt takes 10 seconds per question. That is one minute across the whole test — and it saves you 3–4 points every time.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Q1, Q2, and Q6 alone cost you 3.67 points on G3.6 — and on a 45-point test, that is the whole difference between 71% and 80%. These aren't writing errors. They are reading errors. Slow down, re-read the prompt, and these points come right back.

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20/20 level)

A porcupine's body is equipped with several physical features that allow it to remain safe from predators in the wild. The most famous defense is its coat of roughly 30,000 sharp quills, which are actually modified hairs with microscopic barbs at the tips. This shows that a porcupine can hurt any predator that gets too close, because once the barbs stick, they are very hard to pull out. Porcupines also have strong claws and rough paw pads that help them climb trees quickly to escape danger. This shows that a porcupine's body is built for both fighting back and running away. In the end, the porcupine's quills, claws, and climbing skills work together to keep it safe in the wild.
