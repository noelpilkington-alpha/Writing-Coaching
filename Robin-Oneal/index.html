Use the Specific Conjunction Asked For

Q5 — The question said "because, but, or so"

YOU WROTE

"That" is a relative pronoun, not one of the three conjunctions the question asked for. The question specifically listed because, but, and so. Also: "pumkin" should be pumpkin and "bakes" should be baked.

READ THE QUESTION CAREFULLY
When a question says "use because, but, or so," you MUST use one of those three words. Before you write, underline the key instruction in the question. Then check your answer — did you actually use one of those specific words?

Because = gives a reason
But = shows contrast or surprise
So = shows a result
See examples of each
"I was hungry because I skipped lunch." (reason)
"The test was hard, but I did my best." (contrast)
"It started raining, so we went inside." (result)

Develop Your Paragraph

Q11 — Good start, but needs more evidence from the passage

Your paragraph scored 16/20. You have a strong topic sentence with an appositive, and you gave 2 reasons. But your paragraph needs more evidence from the passage and has some spelling errors.

Your Paragraph
Learning to cook, an important life skill, is usefull to learn at a young age. One reason is that if you are left home alone and you are hungry then you can cook your own food. Another reason is that after college you can take care of your self food wise. Overall it is very immportant to know how to cook.

Great job including an appositive in your topic sentence! But notice: your reasons are general — they don't reference specific facts from the passage. Also: "usefull" → useful, "immportant" → important, "your self" → yourself.

Here's the G3 paragraph plan you should follow:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Learning to cook, an important life skill, is useful to learn at a young age. (Your TS is already strong!)
D1 According to the passage, cooking teaches kids to follow directions and measure ingredients carefully.
W This is important because it helps kids practice math and reading skills in a fun way.
D2 The article also explains that kids who cook learn to make healthier food choices.
W This matters because eating well helps kids grow strong and do better in school.
D3 Finally, the passage says cooking is a skill kids will use for the rest of their lives.
W This shows that starting early gives kids an advantage when they grow up.
CS Overall, learning to cook at a young age gives children skills that help them now and in the future.
USE EVIDENCE FROM THE PASSAGE
Your reasons are good ideas, but they're your own thoughts — not facts from the article. When the prompt says "use evidence from the selection," you need to mention specific things the passage says.

Try starting detail sentences with:
"According to the passage..."
"The article explains that..."
"The passage states..."

This signals to the reader (and grader) that you're using evidence.
