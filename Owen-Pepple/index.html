You got all the ideas in, but removing "ran to" made the sentence awkward. When combining, keep the verbs from both sentences.

Tap to see the improved version
Read the explanation above first
SMOOTHLY COMBINED

TWO EDITING SKILLS
Appositives: A short description tucked right after a noun, surrounded by commas.
Formula: Noun, description, rest of sentence.
Example: Sally, the baby seal, swam to the other seals.

Combining sentences: Use a comma + conjunction (and, but, so) to join two sentences. Keep the verbs from both!
Example: Anna ran to the beach, and she found a seal.

Build Your Paragraph

Q11 — Let's learn the paragraph structure from scratch

Your paragraph scored 3/20 because you wrote only one sentence:

YOUR PARAGRAPH
Pokemone is a game that is fun.

You wrote something, which is better than leaving it blank! But this sentence doesn't answer the prompt. The prompt asked: "Explain how Satoshi Tajiri's childhood influenced the creation of Pokémon." Your sentence talks about Pokémon being fun, not about Tajiri's childhood.

Here's the G3 paragraph plan. It tells you exactly what to write, sentence by sentence:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Satoshi Tajiri's childhood love of catching bugs led him to create Pokémon.
D1 As a child, Tajiri spent hours outside catching bugs — his friends called him "Dr. Bug."
W This shows how much he loved collecting creatures, which became the main idea behind Pokémon.
D2 When he grew up, he remembered his bug-catching joy and turned it into a video game idea.
W This shows that without that childhood memory, the game might never have been created.
D3 Pokémon became huge — over 150 characters, TV shows, movies, apps, and more.
W This shows that one kid's hobby turned into something millions of people enjoy.
CS Tajiri's childhood hobby of catching bugs turned into one of the most popular games in the world.
HOW TO START WHEN YOU'RE STUCK
Step 1: Read the prompt carefully. It tells you exactly what to explain. This one says: "Explain how Tajiri's childhood influenced Pokémon."
Step 2: Write one sentence that answers the prompt. That's your Topic Sentence (TS). Example: "Satoshi Tajiri's childhood love of catching bugs led him to create Pokémon."
Step 3: Find 3 details from the article. Those are D1, D2, D3.
Step 4: After each detail, write why it matters. Ask: "So what?"
Step 5: End with a Concluding Sentence that restates your main point.

Also, check your spelling: "Pokemone" should be Pokémon. Reading your writing back to yourself helps catch errors like this.
