THE MEMORY TRICK
Say it out loud: "be · CAUSE". The word has "cause" inside it (as in, "the cause of something"). Spell it like: be + cause = because.

If you are ever unsure, ask yourself: "Does it have CAUSE in it?" If yes, you have it right.

Your #2 Repeated Error: Missing "?"

When the prompt says "rewrite as a question," your answer must end with ?

Lukas, on both G3.5 and G3.7, you got a "rewrite as a question" prompt — and both times you reworded the sentence correctly but forgot the question mark. That is worth half-credit instead of full credit. Easy fix.

FROM G3.7 Q4 (scored 0.5/2)
The prompt: "Rewrite sentence 3 as a question: These sharp quills protect them from danger."

You wrote: "Do sharp quills protect them from danger."
The wording is perfect — but it ends with a period instead of a question mark.
FROM G3.5 Q1 (scored 0.5/2)
The prompt: "Rewrite this sentence as a question: The books were sold in department stores."

You wrote: "Why were the book sold in department stores."
Same pattern — great rewrite, missing "?" at the end.
THE RULE
If the prompt contains the word "question", your answer MUST end with ? — no exceptions.

Look for these prompt phrases:
• "Rewrite... as a question"
• "Turn this into a question"
• "Change this sentence into a question"

Before you submit, look at the last character of your answer. Is it a ? If not, fix it.

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20/20 level)

A porcupine's body helps it stay safe in the wild in several ways. First, porcupines are covered in more than 30,000 quills that protect them from predators. Next, they make a sound with their teeth as a warning to animals that come too close. Then, they have strong claws so they can climb trees to get away from danger. Lastly, porcupines explore the forest at a young age, so they know how to get around and find safe places to hide. In all of these ways, a porcupine's body helps it stay safe in the wild.
