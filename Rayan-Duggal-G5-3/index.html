Fix 1: Answer the Question Asked

“How does the author show...” is about the author, not about VR
THE RULE: ECHO THE QUESTION, THEN “THIS SHOWS THE AUTHOR...”
When the prompt asks how the author shows something, the first sentence echoes it: The author shows ... by .... Then, after every fact you pull from the article, one sentence that starts This shows the author... and names what the fact does for the argument. The check: does your paragraph mention the author? If not, you answered a different question.
