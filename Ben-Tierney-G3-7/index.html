HOW TO FIND PASSAGE DETAILS
When a question says "add a detail from the passage," follow these steps:

1. Go back to the passage and re-read the section about the topic.
2. Look for specific facts, descriptions, or numbers the author wrote.
3. Use those words (or close to them) in your answer.

Key rule: If you can't point to where in the passage your detail comes from, it's your own idea — not a passage detail.

FIXED

THEIR / THERE / THEY'RE
Their = belonging to them ("their seeds," "their house")
There = a place ("over there," "there are many")
They're = they are ("they're coming")

Then vs. Than:
Then = time ("first this, then that")
Than = comparison ("better than," "more than")
See more examples

"First mix the ingredients, then bake." (time order)

Develop Your Paragraph

Q11 — Add a topic sentence and concluding sentence

Your paragraph scored 16/20. You had good ideas about why cooking helps kids, but your paragraph is missing a clear topic sentence and a concluding sentence.

Your Paragraph
Cooking can help kids express them selves by the food. For exsample if it is raining outside a kid might make some hot foods to gain warmth. Also cooking is a good way to show creativity. Kids can make new types of food combining 2 different foods. Last kids will need to learn how to cook when they leave the house and it is good for them to learn now rather then later.

You have 3 good reasons! But your first sentence doesn't clearly state your central idea, and you don't have a concluding sentence. Also: "exsample" → example, "them selves" → themselves, "then" → than.

Here's the G3 paragraph plan you should follow:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Learning to cook can help children in many important ways.
D1 According to the passage, cooking lets kids be creative by trying new recipes and combining ingredients.
W This is important because it teaches kids to experiment and think for themselves.
D2 The passage also explains that kids who cook learn to make healthy meals.
W This matters because eating well helps kids stay strong and focused in school.
D3 Finally, the article says that cooking is a skill kids will need when they grow up and live on their own.
W This shows that learning to cook early prepares kids for the future.
CS Overall, cooking is a valuable skill that helps children be creative, stay healthy, and get ready for adult life.
TWO KEY FIXES FOR YOUR PARAGRAPH
1. Start with a clear topic sentence: Your first sentence should directly answer the prompt. "Learning to cook can help children in many important ways" tells the reader exactly what the paragraph is about.

2. End with a concluding sentence: Your last sentence should wrap up your main point. It should NOT introduce a new idea — it should summarize what you've already said.
