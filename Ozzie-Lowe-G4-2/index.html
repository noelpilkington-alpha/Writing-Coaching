Your #1 Error: Comma Splices

Q1, Q7, Q8 — joining two sentences with just a comma

Ozzie, a comma splice happens when you put two complete sentences together with only a comma between them. You did this on three different questions:

Q1 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"Bluebonnets bloom in the sprig, they cover the fields in blue."
Q7 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"Robots can help people, they can do dangerous jobs."
Q8 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"Robots are tiney and can fit in small places, that helps them do their work."

The rule: If both sides of a comma can stand alone as complete sentences, you have a comma splice. Fix it by using a period or the word "because."

FIXED VERSIONS
Q1: "Bluebonnets bloom in the spring. They cover the fields in blue."
Q7: "Robots can help people. They can do dangerous jobs."
Q8: "Robots are tiny and can fit in small places. That helps them do their work."
Which sentence is correct?
A Bluebonnets grow in fields, they are the state flower of Texas.
B Bluebonnets grow in fields. They are the state flower of Texas.
THE COMMA SPLICE CHECK
After you write a sentence with a comma, cover up everything before the comma. Does what comes after still make sense as its own sentence? If yes, you need a period instead of a comma.

Fix #2: Appositives

Q4 — 0 out of 2 weighted points

Ozzie, Q4 asked you to add an appositive to a sentence. You did not add one. Here is what an appositive is and how to use it:

WHAT IS AN APPOSITIVE?
An appositive is a noun phrase placed next to a noun to rename or describe it. It has no verb and is set off by commas.

Example: "Bluebonnets, the state flower of Texas, bloom every spring."

The bold part renames "Bluebonnets." It tells you what they are.
HOW TO ADD AN APPOSITIVE
1. Pick the noun you want to describe.
2. Write a short phrase that answers "What is it?" (no verb allowed).
3. Put commas around it, right after the noun.

"Robots, machines designed to do tasks automatically, are becoming more common."
Which sentence correctly uses an appositive?
A The bluebonnet, a small purple wildflower, grows all over Texas in the spring.
B The bluebonnet grows all over Texas because it likes warm weather.
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q4 was worth 2 weighted points and you earned 0. Learning this one structure gives you those 2 points back on the next test. An appositive = noun phrase + commas + no verb.

The fix: Before you write, read the question twice and underline the key words. Then make sure your answer directly responds to those key words.

THE KEY WORD METHOD
1. Read the question.
2. Underline the key words (what exactly is it asking?).
3. Start your answer by using some of those same words.
4. Then give your reason + a detail from the passage + what it shows.

The Detail + Explanation Pattern
