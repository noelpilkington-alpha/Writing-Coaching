Fix 1: Find the Is A. The Other Sentence Stays Whole

31 attempts kept the is. Your own second tries show the move
THE RULE: FIND THE IS A. THE OTHER SENTENCE STAYS WHOLE
One of the two sentences says what the thing is: It is a flexible wooden tool. Balance is a crucial skill for gymnasts. The words after is a are the naming phrase. It is, she was, they are get cut, the verb too, not just the pronoun. The other sentence stays whole, every word, and if it starts with It or She, that word becomes the thing being named. The check: the phrase does not start with it is, she was, they are or a bare is. It names the thing, and words like that protect them may follow the name. The rest still reads as a complete sentence on its own.

Fix 2: The Slot Moves the Commas, Not the Words

The end slot: 13 asked, none clean, 6 left blank. You have written it once
THE RULE: THE SLOT MOVES THE COMMAS, NOT THE WORDS
The phrase and the whole sentence are the same in every slot. The instruction names the slot, and the slot sets the commas. Beginning: the phrase, one comma, then the whole sentence with the name first: A dangerous storm hazard, hail can damage cars and plant leaves. The phrase keeps its own first word: the lesson wrote a dangerous storm hazard, so it starts with a. Middle: the name, comma, the phrase, comma, the rest: Hail, a dangerous storm hazard, can damage cars and plant leaves. Two commas, right after the name. End: the whole sentence, one comma, the phrase: The storm brought a blizzard, a dangerous snowstorm with powerful winds. The phrase renames the noun right before the comma, which is the last noun of the sentence. The check: count the appositive’s commas: one, two, one. An opener like In the spring, keeps its own comma.

Fix 3: A Naming Phrase, Not an Example

In the fill-in quiz, the Requiem for Mozart and the Mario Brothers for the flute
THE RULE: AN APPOSITIVE IS A NAMING PHRASE. IT IS ANOTHER NAME FOR THE SAME THING
Mozart, a famous composer: a famous composer is Mozart. The Requiem is something Mozart wrote, not another name for him. The phrase can be a category, a famous composer, a wooden instrument, or the thing’s own name, the band, the Union Grove High School Band. It cannot be a thing connected to it, and it cannot start with is or it is. The check: swap the phrase in for the name. A famous composer wrote beautiful music passes. The Requiem wrote beautiful music fails.
