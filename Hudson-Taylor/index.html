What Is an Appositive?

Q3 — You added an adverb, not an appositive

Q3 asked you to rewrite the sentence using an appositive. You added the word "quickly" — but that's an adverb (it describes how she swam), not an appositive.

YOU WROTE
Sally swam quickly to the other seals.

An appositive is a description that renames or explains a noun. It goes right after the noun and is set off by commas:

Adverb (describes how) → Sally swam quickly
Appositive (renames the noun) → Sally, the baby seal, swam
Tap to see the improved version
Read the explanation above first
FIXED WITH APPOSITIVE
Sally, the baby seal, swam to the other seals.
HOW TO WRITE AN APPOSITIVE
Step 1: Find the noun you want to describe (Sally).
Step 2: Write a short description that renames it (the baby seal).
Step 3: Place it right after the noun with commas on both sides.

Pattern: Noun, description, rest of sentence.
"My teacher, Mrs. Smith, gave us homework."
"The dog, a golden retriever, wagged its tail."
See more examples
"Jupiter, the largest planet, has many moons."
"My friend, a talented artist, drew a picture."
"Pizza, my favorite food, was on the menu."

Here's the G3 paragraph plan you should follow:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Satoshi Tajiri's love for catching bugs as a kid led him to create Pokémon.
D1 As a child, Tajiri spent hours catching bugs — his friends called him "Dr. Bug."
W This shows how much he loved collecting creatures, which became the main idea behind Pokémon.
D2 When he grew up, he started playing video games but thought they were not fun enough.
W This explains why he decided to create his own game — he wanted something better.
D3 He combined his love of bugs with video games, and Pokémon became huge with cards, movies, and apps.
W This shows that his childhood hobby turned into something millions of people enjoy.
CS Tajiri's childhood love of nature led him to create one of the most popular games in the world.
KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR YOUR PARAGRAPH
1. Start with a topic sentence that answers the prompt directly.
2. After each detail, explain why it matters to the main point.
3. Keep everything in one paragraph — no line breaks in the middle.
4. Double-check spelling, especially names like Pokémon.
