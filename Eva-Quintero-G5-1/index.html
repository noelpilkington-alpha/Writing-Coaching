Step 5: Proofread — Tense and "Would Have"

Q4 tense error and Q8 "wouldn't of" error
Q4 — TENSE ERROR (1 out of 2 weighted points)
You wrote "walk" when the passage was in past tense. It should be "walked."

The fix: Check what tense the passage uses. If the story happened in the past, your answer should use past tense too. "Walk" is present tense. "Walked" is past tense.

Q8 — "WOULDN'T OF" (2 out of 3 weighted points)
You wrote "wouldn't of" — the correct phrase is "wouldn't have."

Why this happens: When we say "wouldn't have" out loud, it sounds like "wouldn't of." But "of" is not a verb. "Have" is the verb. In writing, it must always be "would have," "could have," "should have."

THE RULE
"Would have" — always.
"Could have" — always.
"Should have" — always.

Never "would of," "could of," "should of." Those are not real phrases in written English.
