Your G3.4 Paragraph

14 out of 20 — needs more detail and explanation

Conan, your paragraph on Q11 earned 14 out of 20. You have the right structure (a topic sentence and some details), but your supporting details need more D+E (Detail + Explanation). Right now the paragraph is thin because each point is stated but never explained.

STEP 6: SINGLE PARAGRAPH OUTLINE (use the scratchpad)
Before writing your paragraph, plan it in the AlphaTest scratchpad:

T.S. — Topic Sentence (answers the question directly)
S.D. — Supporting Detail (a reason or example + explain it)
S.D. — Supporting Detail (a second reason or example + explain it)
C.S. — Closing Sentence (wraps up your paragraph)
Example Single Paragraph Outline for a Q11 prompt
T.S.Outdoor parks are important because they give people a place to explore nature.
S.D.For example, visitors can walk on trails and discover interesting rocks and animals. This matters because it helps people learn about the world outside of their homes.
S.D.Another reason parks are important is that they give families a place to spend time together. This is important because spending time outdoors together creates good memories.
C.S.That is why outdoor parks are important places for people to visit.

Notice: Each supporting detail has two parts: the detail itself ("visitors can walk on trails") and the explanation ("This matters because..."). That second part is what earns full D+E points.
