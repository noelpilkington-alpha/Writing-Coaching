Error #1: Appositives Need Commas

Step 2 skill: the question is testing appositives

Caroline, Q3 asked you to add an appositive to a sentence about Sally. You put the right information in ("the seal pup"), but you left out the commas that make it an appositive.

Q3 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"Sally the seal pup was found on the beach."

The problem: An appositive is a renaming phrase that is set off by commas. Without the commas, "Sally the seal pup" reads as one long name rather than a noun followed by a description.

CORRECT: WITH COMMAS
"Sally, the seal pup, was found on the beach."
THE APPOSITIVE PATTERN
Noun, [renaming phrase], rest of sentence.

• Sally, the seal pup, was found on the beach.
• The family, a group of tourists, spotted her by the rocks.

Two commas. Always. The renaming phrase is surrounded by commas like it is in parentheses.
Which sentence correctly uses an appositive?
A"Rex the family dog ran to the beach."
B"Rex, the family dog, ran to the beach."
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q3 is worth 2 weighted points. You earned 1 because you had the right idea but the wrong punctuation. Two commas would have earned you full credit.

Error #2: Use the Right Connector

Step 2 skill: the question is testing cause-and-effect connectors

Caroline, Q5 asked you to combine two ideas using a connector that shows a cause-and-effect relationship. You used "Later," which shows time order, not cause and effect.

Q5 — YOUR ANSWER (0 out of 2 weighted points)
"Later, the family called the rescue center."

The problem: "Later" tells us WHEN something happened (time order). The question wanted you to show WHY the family called. The correct connector is "Since" because it shows a reason.

CORRECT: CAUSE-AND-EFFECT CONNECTOR
"Since Sally was injured, the family called the rescue center."
CONNECTOR TYPES
Time order: Later, Then, After, Before, Next
Cause and effect: Since, Because, So, Therefore

Before you write, ask: "Is the question asking me to show when or why?"
• When = time connector (Later, Then)
• Why = cause connector (Since, Because)
The question asks you to show WHY the rescue center came. Which connector is correct?
A"After that, the rescue center came to help Sally."
B"Because Sally was hurt and alone, the rescue center came to help her."
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q5 is worth 2 weighted points and you earned 0. This was not a knowledge gap. You know the word "Since." The fix is Step 2: read the question, identify that it is asking for a cause-and-effect relationship, then choose a connector that shows a reason.

Error #3: Explain WHY It Matters

Q7 lost 1 point because the explanation stopped too soon

Caroline, on Q7 you wrote a good answer about a meaningful moment, but you did not explain WHY it was meaningful. You described what happened but left out the "so what" part.

Q7 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"A meaningful moment was when the family found Sally on the beach and decided to help her."

What is good: You identified the moment correctly and described it clearly.

What is missing: The question asks WHY this moment is meaningful. You need one more sentence explaining what it shows or why it matters.

STRONGER: WITH THE "WHY" SENTENCE
"A meaningful moment was when the family found Sally on the beach and decided to help her. This moment is meaningful because it shows that the family cared about animals and was willing to give up their day to help a stranger creature in need."
THE DETAIL + EXPLANATION PATTERN
Detail: What happened? (Name the moment, describe it.)
Explanation: Why does it matter? What does it show?

Think of it as two sentences:
1. The moment was [describe it].
2. This is meaningful because [explain what it shows about the character or story].
The question asks: "Describe a meaningful moment and explain why it is meaningful." Which answer earns full points?
A"The family found Sally on the beach and took her to the rescue center."
B"The family found Sally on the beach and decided to help her. This moment is meaningful because it shows that ordinary people can make a big difference for an animal in need."
THE PATTERN FOR Q11 TOO
This same detail + explanation pattern applies to your paragraph (Q11). Every supporting detail needs a "This shows..." or "This is important because..." sentence after it. That is how you move from 16 to 18+ on the paragraph.
