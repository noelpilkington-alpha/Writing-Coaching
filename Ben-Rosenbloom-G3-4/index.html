Step 2: Identify the Skill — Fix the Fragment

Q5 still has a fragment after your rewrite

Ben, on Q5 you were asked to rewrite a fragment into a complete sentence. Your rewrite is still a fragment. Here is what Step 2 catches:

Q5 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
Your rewrite is still not a complete sentence — it does not express a complete thought with a subject doing an action.
STEP 2: WHAT SKILL IS THIS?
The question says "rewrite this fragment as a complete sentence." That means your answer needs:

• A subject (who or what)
• A verb (what they do)
• A complete thought (it makes sense on its own)

If your sentence does not have all three, it is still a fragment.
A COMPLETE SENTENCE VERSION
"Layla discovered a beautiful geode on the nature trail near her house."

Subject = Layla. Verb = discovered. Complete thought = yes, it tells a full idea.
Which of these is a complete sentence (not a fragment)?
A The beautiful geode on the nature trail near Layla's house.
B Layla found a beautiful geode on the nature trail near her house.
YOUR CHECK
After you rewrite a fragment, ask: "Does this have a subject DOING something?" If the answer is no, add a subject or a verb. This one check is worth 2 weighted points every time it appears.
