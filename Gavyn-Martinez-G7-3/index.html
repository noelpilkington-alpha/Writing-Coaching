THE FIX: WHISPER IT AS YOU WRITE IT
Every one of your spelling errors — across both tests — is a word where you skipped a sound. You know these words. You use them in speech. The problem is that your hand writes faster than your brain spells.

The fix: whisper each tricky word as you type it, syllable by syllable.
• "dif-fer-ent" — you hear the second "f" and the "e"
• "whis-tles" — you hear the "h" after the "w"
• "lan-guage" — you hear "gua" not "gau"
• "je-op-ar-dy" — you hear "eo" not "ea"

This is not about memorizing a spelling list. It is about slowing down for one second on words that have more than two syllables.

THE ONE-QUESTION TEST
Before you write a body paragraph, write down the one question it answers:
• Body 1: How does El Silbo work?
• Body 2: How did El Silbo connect people in daily life?
• Body 3: What happened when it almost disappeared, and how was it saved?

If a sentence does not answer that paragraph's question, it belongs in a different paragraph. Move it or start a new one.
