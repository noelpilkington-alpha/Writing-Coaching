THE TWO RULES
Apostrophe rule: An apostrophe marks where a letter was dropped.
• should not → shouldn't (apostrophe where the "o" was)
• it is → it's (apostrophe where the "i" was)
• that is → that's (apostrophe where the "i" was)

Capital letter rule: Capitals go on the FIRST letter of:
• A sentence (always)
• Proper nouns (names of people, places, brands): Kenya, Satoshi, Pokemon
• Nothing else in the middle of a sentence

Modern sports developed and became popular because people found new ways to organize them and share them with big audiences. Another reason is that modern sports became popular through television and the internet, which let fans watch games from anywhere. This shows that technology turned sports into something millions of people could enjoy at the same time. That is how modern sports developed and became popular in society.
