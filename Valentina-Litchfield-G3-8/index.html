YOUR 3-MINUTE PROOFREAD PASS
Minute 1 — Hunt the double letters. Go through every long word. Ask: "does this end in -full or -ful?" "-ment or -mint?" Your pattern is adding an extra letter. Cut the extras.

Minute 2 — Hunt the missing letters. Words like different, remembered, measuring need every letter. If a word looks short, spell it out in your head: d-i-f-f-e-r-e-n-t. Add what's missing.

Minute 3 — Read it backwards. Start with the last word and go right-to-left. This breaks the auto-complete habit and makes misspellings jump off the page.

Quick-Answer Typos (Q1–Q5)

The tiny slips in the sentence questions that cost half a point each

Valentina, your Q1–Q5 answers are almost always right on the idea. The points you lose here are single-letter typos — places where one letter is wrong or missing. Graders take off half a point for each one.

"recored" should be record. Also, "that day" is repeated at the start and end of the sentence — the prompt asked you to rewrite it clearly and concisely, meaning to cut the repetition. Dropped from 2 to 1 point.

FROM G3.5 Q1
"My family has two dogs, there names are Lucy and Rocket."
"there" should be their (showing possession). Also, the prompt asked for an appositive like "Lucy and Rocket, our two dogs,…"
THE THERE / THEIR / THEY'RE RULE
This one shows up often. Use this quick check:
• there = a place ("over there")
• their = belongs to them ("their names")
• they're = they are ("they're happy")
If you can swap the word for "they are" and it still makes sense, it's "they're." Otherwise, if it shows ownership, it's "their."
Which sentence earns the full 2 points?
A My family has two dogs, there names are Lucy and Rocket.
B My family has two dogs, Lucy and Rocket, and their favorite game is fetch.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Q1–Q5 are each worth 2 points — 10 points total. If you lose half a point on two of them per test because of a typo, that's 1 point gone, which is the difference between 88% and 90%. Proofreading the short answers matters just as much as proofreading Q11.
