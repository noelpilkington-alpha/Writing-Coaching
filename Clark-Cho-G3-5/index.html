Make Your First Sentence Match the Prompt

Echo the question word so your paragraph lands on target
🔍 Pattern alert: On G3.5 your opening sentence announced the topic ("his art is under the ocean") but did not answer the question. Echoing the question word in your first sentence is the fix — and it works on every prompt.

Clark, here is a simple habit that will help on every paragraph: make your first sentence echo the question word. If the prompt says why, your first sentence should have the word because or so that in it. If it says how, your first sentence should describe the way something happens.

THE ECHO TRICK
Look at the question word, then build your first sentence around it:

Prompt says WHY → first sentence: "[Subject] did this because..."
Prompt says HOW → first sentence: "[Subject] did this by..."
Prompt says WHAT → first sentence: "The most important thing about [subject] is..."
The prompt asks WHY Jason placed statues under the sea. Which first sentence matches the prompt?
A Jason placed his statues under the sea because he wanted nature to grow over them.
B This paragraph is about Jason's underwater statues.
