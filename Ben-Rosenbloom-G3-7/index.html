THE RULE: THE "BECAUSE" PART MUST ADD NEW INFORMATION
After you write "because," ask yourself: "Did I just say the same thing again, or did I explain WHY?"

✗ Circle: "Bees help grow because they help them grow." (repeats the claim)
✗ Off-topic: "…because of their big size." (a reason that doesn't fit)
✓ Real reason: "…because they carry pollen between flowers." (new fact that explains why)

This is the same skill that makes your paragraph reasons strong — every good reason adds a fact that explains the point.

Appositives & Complete Sentences

Questions 4 and 10 — do exactly what's asked
🔍 Pattern alert: Q4 and Q10 each lost a point for the same kind of reason — not finishing the exact task. Q4 asked you to ADD an appositive but you rewrote the sentence; Q10 started with "Because" and never became a full sentence.

Ben, first up — appositives. An appositive is a short phrase that renames the noun, set off with commas. Q4 asked you to add one to a sentence about bees. Here is what you wrote:

YOU WROTE (G3.7 Q4, earned 1 of 2 points)
"Bees make noise as they move from flower to flower quickly."

That is a fine sentence — but it does not have an appositive. You rewrote the sentence instead of adding a phrase that renames the bees. An appositive would look like: "Bees, busy pollinators, buzz from flower to flower."

Which sentence actually ADDS an appositive?
A Bees make noise as they move from flower to flower quickly.
B Bees, busy pollinators, buzz from flower to flower.

Now the other half — complete sentences. Q10 asked you to answer in a full sentence, and here is what you wrote:

YOU WROTE (G3.7 Q10, earned 2 of 3 points)
"Because that's when the soil is warm."

This starts with "Because…" but it never finishes the thought — it is a fragment, not a complete sentence. A sentence that starts with "Because" still needs a main clause (a who and a what that can stand on its own).

Which one is a COMPLETE sentence?
A Because that's when the soil is warm.
B People plant pumpkin seeds in late spring because that's when the soil is warm.
Tap to see both answers fixed
An appositive, and a complete sentence
FIXED
Q4 (appositive added): Bees, busy pollinators, buzz from flower to flower.

Q10 (complete sentence): People plant pumpkin seeds in late spring because that's when the soil is warm.
TWO QUICK RULES
1. Appositive = rename with commas. If a question says "add an appositive," drop a phrase that renames the noun, set off with commas: "Bees, busy pollinators, buzz…" Don't rewrite the whole sentence.

2. "Because" needs a main part. If your answer starts with "Because," it is not done yet. Add the who-and-what first: "People save seeds because…"
