"Then" vs "Than"

Q8 — Two words that sound alike but mean different things

In Q8, you used the wrong word for a comparison:

FIXED
I would rather listen to a book than read it, because that is faster.
THEN = time / sequence "First this, then that."
THAN = comparison "Bigger than, rather than"
MEMORY TRICK
THAN has an A — just like compArison.
Whenever you're comparing two things (rather X than Y, bigger than, more than), use than.

Capitalize "I" + "Too" vs "To"

Q10 — Two quick fixes

Your Q10 answer has two small errors that are easy to fix once you know the rules:

YOU WROTE
I don't think I would like reading a Little Golden Book, because i'm to old for them.

Fix 1: Always capitalize "I"

i'm → I'm

The letter "I" is always capitalized when it refers to yourself — even inside contractions like I'm, I'll, I've, and I'd.

Fix 2: "To" vs "Too"

to old → too old
TO vs TOO vs TWO
To = direction or purpose ("go to the store," "want to read")
Too = also OR excessively ("too old," "me too")
Two = the number 2

Quick test: If you can replace it with "excessively" or "also," use too.

Remove Extra Commas

Q2 & Q9 — Don't add commas where they don't belong

You added commas in two places where they aren't needed:

Q2 — No comma between subject and verb

YOU WROTE
The Poky Little Puppy, was very popular.
FIXED
The Poky Little Puppy was very popular.

Never put a comma between the subject ("The Poky Little Puppy") and its verb ("was"). They belong together.

Q9 — No comma before "because" here

YOU WROTE
One reason the Little Golden Books became so popular is, because they were so cheap.
FIXED
One reason the Little Golden Books became so popular is because they were so cheap.

When "because" directly explains the main idea, you don't need a comma before it.

COMMA QUICK RULES
1. Never put a comma between the subject and its verb.
2. Don't put a comma right before "because" when it explains the main idea directly.
3. When in doubt, read the sentence out loud. If you don't naturally pause there, skip the comma.

BETTER

D1 When people swim near reefs, they may accidentally break the coral.
W This is a problem because coral takes a very long time to grow back.

KEY TAKEAWAY
Your paragraph had the right idea but needed more sentences. After every detail (D), write a sentence explaining why it matters (W). Also: always proofread for leftover words from editing — "went would go" should be just "would go."
