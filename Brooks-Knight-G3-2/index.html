THE COMPLETE SENTENCE RULE
A complete sentence needs three things:
1. A subject (who or what)
2. A verb (what they do or think)
3. A detail or reason (why, how, or what happened)

Complete: "I think the Teddy Bear Toss is the best part because fans get to help kids and have fun at the same time."

Detail + Explanation for Q11

How to turn one idea into a full paragraph

Brooks, a strong Q11 paragraph uses a simple pattern: give a detail from the article, then explain why it matters. Do that twice and you have a full paragraph.

THE DETAIL + EXPLANATION PATTERN
Detail: A specific fact or event from the article.
Explanation: Why that fact matters or what it shows.

Think of it like this: the detail is what happened, and the explanation is why it is important.

The G3.2 passages were about the Hershey Bears Teddy Bear Toss and "One Book, One Camel." Here is how the pattern works with the camel library:

Detail + Explanation Example (Camel Library)
DETAIL In some parts of the world, a camel carries books to children who live far from any library.
EXPLAIN This matters because those children cannot walk to a library, so the camel is their only way to read new books.

Now stack that pattern into a full paragraph about the Teddy Bear Toss:

Topic + detail/explain + detail/explain + closing
MODEL PARAGRAPH (what 14-18 out of 20 looks like)

The Teddy Bear Toss shows that hockey fans care about helping others. Every year, fans bring stuffed animals to the Hershey Bears game and throw them onto the ice after the first goal. This is important because all those bears are donated to children in need during the holidays. The event also shows that fans want to make games about more than just winning. In the end, the Teddy Bear Toss proves that fans can have fun and help their community at the same time.

Which sentence pair uses the detail + explanation pattern correctly?
