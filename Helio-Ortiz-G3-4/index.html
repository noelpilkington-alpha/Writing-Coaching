Your Paragraph — The One Thing to Fix

13 out of 20 — underdeveloped. Here is the structure that earns 18+.

Helio, your paragraph has the right idea but needs more development. You state a point but do not explain it enough. The fix: after every example, add a sentence that says why it matters or what it shows.

Single Paragraph Outline — the 19–20/20 shape
TOPIC State your main idea clearly
DETAIL First example from the passage
EXPLAIN "This shows..." — why the example matters
DETAIL Second example from the passage
EXPLAIN "This shows..." — why the second example matters
CLOSE Wrap up your main idea in a new way
THE RULE: DETAIL + EXPLANATION, ALWAYS
Every time you give an example from the passage, follow it with a sentence that explains why it matters or what it shows. This is the single move that separates a 13/20 paragraph from an 18/20 paragraph.

Example:
Detail: "Sports bring communities together when fans cheer for the same team."
Explanation: "This shows that sports create connections between people who might not know each other otherwise."
Which paragraph excerpt includes proper Detail + Explanation?
ASports are popular. People play them every day. Teams compete in leagues around the world.
BSports bring communities together when fans cheer for the same team. This shows that sports create connections between people who might not know each other otherwise.
