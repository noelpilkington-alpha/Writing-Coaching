The G7 rubric rewards the next sentence, the one that answers "and so what?" That is the same move for multiple choice and for essay.
Which sentence best elaborates on the topic "benefits for the medical clinic"?
A The medical clinic is a place where people in the village get care.
B The clinic now stores vaccines safely overnight because the refrigerator no longer loses power at sunset.
THE TOPIC-MATCHING TEST
When a question asks you to match a topic to the best sentence, ask these three questions in order:

1. Does this sentence name the benefit/cause/change? Not just the setup — the actual change.
2. Does it add a specific detail (who, when, how much, how long)?
3. Does it answer "so what?" — does it show why this matters?

The right answer usually says more with fewer words — it is precise, not general. "The extra hours gave customers more time to shop" beats "Businesses could stay open for longer" because it names the effect, not just the fact.
