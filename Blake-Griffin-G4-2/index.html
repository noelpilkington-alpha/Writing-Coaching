Q11: The 5-Minute Paragraph

Even a basic paragraph earns 10–14 out of 20

Blake, you have written strong paragraphs before (G3.1 and G4.1 both had good Q11 scores). You know how to do this. The challenge is making sure it gets done every time.

THE 5-MINUTE Q11 PLAN
Minute 1: Read the prompt. Write your topic sentence in your scratchpad.
Minute 2: Write two supporting details (just key words or short phrases).
Minutes 3–4: Turn your outline into full sentences.
Minute 5: Write a concluding sentence and proofread.
Example: Prompt says "Why is exercise important?"
T.S. Exercise is important because it keeps your body healthy and your mind sharp.
S.D. Running and playing sports make your heart stronger.
S.D. Exercise also helps you focus better at school.
C.S. That is why exercise matters for both your body and your brain.

That is 4 sentences. It takes about 4 minutes to write. And it earns 12 to 14 points.
