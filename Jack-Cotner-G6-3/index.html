Q9 — "However" shows contrast; "Unless" shows a condition.

The two sentences were:

The Sentences
"Fewer butterflies are surviving their trips." → "People are taking action to protect them."

You chose Unless, but the second sentence contrasts with the first — it's not a condition. The correct transition is "However" because the sentences show a problem and then a response:

Tap to see the correct usage
Think: problem vs. response
CORRECT TRANSITION
"Fewer butterflies are surviving their trips. However, people are taking action to protect them."
QUICK GUIDE
However = "but" — the second sentence contrasts with the first.
Unless = "if not" — the second sentence is a condition for the first.

Test it: "Fewer butterflies are surviving. If not, people are taking action" — that doesn't make sense. "Fewer butterflies are surviving. But people are taking action" — that works.

The Body Paragraph Pattern

How to build a strong body paragraph every time

Every body paragraph in a G6+ essay should follow this pattern:

Body Paragraph Formula
TS Topic Sentence — states the paragraph's main point
E1 Evidence 1 — a specific fact, quote, or example from the passage
X1 Explanation 1 — explain why that evidence matters
E2 Evidence 2 — a second fact or example from the passage
X2 Explanation 2 — explain why that evidence matters
CS Concluding Sentence — wraps up the paragraph's main idea

Here's what your citizen scientists paragraph could look like with this pattern:

MODEL BODY PARAGRAPH — CITIZEN SCIENTISTS
[TS] One important way people help monarch butterflies is by becoming citizen scientists who collect valuable data. [E1] According to the passage, volunteers tag butterflies and record where they travel so scientists can track migration patterns. [X1] This information is important because it helps researchers figure out which migration routes are declining and where monarchs need the most protection. [E2] The passage also explains that these citizen scientists report sightings across the country, building a picture of monarch populations over time. [X2] Without this data, scientists would have no way of knowing whether conservation efforts are actually working or where to focus their resources next. [CS] By turning everyday people into data collectors, citizen science programs are playing a key role in the effort to save monarch butterflies.
THE KEY RULE
Every E (evidence) must be followed by an X (explanation). Evidence without explanation is just a list of facts. The explanation is where you show the reader why that evidence matters. Ask yourself: "So what? Why does this matter?"
