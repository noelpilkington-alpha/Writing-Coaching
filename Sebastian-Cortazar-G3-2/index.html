THE "WHAT + WHY" SENTENCE
For Questions 6–10, write one complete sentence that does two jobs:

1. WHAT — answer the question directly.
2. WHY — add the reason or example, joined with because.

✗ "It is fun and helpful." (answer only)
✓ "It is helpful because it gives stuffed animals to children in need." (answer + reason)
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

Your Spelling Sweep

A few small slips per test — every one is fixable
🔍 Pattern alert: Your ideas are right, but a few words slip past on the first write. A 1-minute spelling sweep at the end catches almost all of them.

Sebastian, here are the real spelling slips from your G3.2 test. You know all of these words — they just need a second look:

FROM Q3
"Does this fin event sell put every year?"
Should be: fun, out — your question form was right, the spelling just slipped.
FROM Q2 AND Q9
"...becuase..." and "...beucase..."
Should be: because — the trick is be-CAUSE: b-e-c-a-u-s-e.
SOUND IT OUT
When a word looks a little off, say it slowly in your head and listen for each part:

fun = f · u · n (not "fin")
out = ou · t (not "put")
because = be · cause (b-e-c-a-u-s-e)
Which sentence is spelled correctly?
A The fans cheer becuase the event is fun.
B The fans cheer because the event is fun.
YOUR TOP "WATCH OUT" WORDS
These are the exact words to double-check on your sweep:

because (not becuase / beucase) · fun (not fin) · out (not put)
