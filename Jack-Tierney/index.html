Complete Sentences, Not Fragments

Q7 and Q8 — Your biggest chance to earn more points

Some of your answers started a thought but didn't finish it. That's called a fragment.

Q7 — YOU WROTE
"When the seals thanked Anna."

This starts with "When..." but never finishes. When the seals thanked Anna... what happened?

Q8 — YOU WROTE
"Only if they knew the gender of the seal."

This starts with "Only if..." but never finishes. Only if they knew... then what?

HOW TO CHECK: DOES IT HAVE BOTH PARTS?
A complete sentence needs:
1. WHO or WHAT (the person or thing)
2. DID or IS (what they did or what happened)

For Q6–10, use this pattern every time:
[My answer] because [my reason].
Q7 FIXED
"The most meaningful moment was when Anna and Jim saw the three seals because it showed Sally had found her family."
Q8 FIXED
"It was a good idea to name the seal Sally because giving her a name showed that Anna cared about her."

Q5: Reason Words vs. Feeling Words

Skill: Fill in the blank with the right connecting word

The sentence: "___ there was no sign of the seals, the family placed Sally in a fish box and motored into the bay."

YOU WROTE
"Sadly"

That's a feeling word — it tells how someone felt. But this sentence needs a reason word — it tells why something happened.

CORRECT ANSWER
"Because there was no sign of the seals, the family placed Sally in a fish box and motored into the bay."
QUICK TEST
Ask yourself: Does the rest of the sentence tell a REASON?

"There was no sign of the seals" → That's why they put Sally in the box.
So the blank needs a reason word: Because.

"Because" = tells WHY something happened.
"Sadly" = tells how someone FELT.

Q11: What a Paragraph Looks Like

Topic: How Satoshi Tajiri's childhood led to Pokémon
YOUR PARAGRAPH (7/20)
"Satoshi Tajiri's love for collecting bugs became somthing millions of people all over the globe enjoy."

That's only one sentence. A paragraph needs multiple sentences! Also: somthing → something.

Here is the plan for a paragraph:

PARAGRAPH PLAN (SPO)
First sentence: Answer the question (Topic Sentence)
Middle sentences: Give details from the article + explain why they matter
Last sentence: Wrap it up (Closing Sentence)
EXAMPLE PARAGRAPH
Satoshi Tajiri's childhood love of collecting bugs inspired him to create Pokémon. The article says that as a boy, Tajiri loved exploring nature and catching insects. This gave him the idea for a game where players could collect and trade creatures. The article also explains that Tajiri turned his hobby into a video game that became popular around the world. Because of his childhood love of bugs, millions of people now enjoy Pokémon.

See how that has 5 sentences? Each one has a job. Your sentence was a great start — next time, keep going!
