Step 5: Proofread — Q8 Incomplete Answer

Your answer was on the right track but did not finish the thought
🔍
What happened on Q8: You started a good answer but it was incomplete or unclear. The grader could not tell what point you were making because the thought was not finished. You earned 1 out of 3 weighted points.
Q8 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 3 weighted points)
Your answer started with the right idea but trailed off without completing the thought or connecting it clearly to the passage.

The fix: Every answer to an open-response question needs three parts:

  • Claim: State your answer directly (what happened, what it means, how it would change)
  • Evidence: Name a specific detail from the passage
  • Explanation: Connect the evidence to your claim with "because" or "this shows"
A COMPLETE ANSWER STRUCTURE
"[Claim] The ending would change because [Evidence] in the story, the character learns the lesson only after being caught. [Explanation] If the character had confessed first, the teacher would have praised honesty instead of teaching the lesson about consequences."
STEP 5: THE COMPLETENESS CHECK
After writing, read your answer and ask:

1. Did I state my answer clearly? (Claim)
2. Did I name something specific from the passage? (Evidence)
3. Did I explain how it connects? (Explanation)

If any piece is missing, add it before moving on.

WHY THIS MATTERS
Q8 was worth 3 weighted points and you earned 1. A complete answer with claim + evidence + explanation would bring that to 3/3. That alone moves your short-answer score from 86% to 94%.
