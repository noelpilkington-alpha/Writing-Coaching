Fix 3: Do the Job, Keep the Meaning

The two editing points from G3.1, so they stay fixed
THE RULE: DO THE JOB THE INSTRUCTION NAMES, AND SAY EXACTLY WHAT THE ORIGINAL SAID
Each editing question is two points: one for the skill named (appositive, question, combine) and one for a clean sentence. An appositive is a noun phrase that renames the noun beside it, inside commas: two dogs, Lucy and Rocket. Joining with and is not one. A rewrite as a question keeps the original meaning; it does not add an opinion. The check: is the named skill actually in my sentence? Does my sentence say what the original said, no more and no less?
YOU WROTE (G3.1 Q1, 1 OUT OF 2)
“My family has two dogs and their names are Lucy and Rocket.” JOINED WITH AND; NO APPOSITIVE
Both facts, one sentence, clean. The skill named was an appositive, and and is not one.
THE APPOSITIVE
My family has two dogs, Lucy and Rocket.
The names rename two dogs, inside commas. Swap test: My family has Lucy and Rocket. Still a sentence, so it passes.
YOU WROTE (G3.1 Q2, 1 OUT OF 2)
