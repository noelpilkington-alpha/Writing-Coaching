YOUR UPDATED TOP "WATCH OUT" WORDS
Old list (G5.2/G5.3): author, efficiency, field, than vs then, probably, solution, business, believe

New from G5.4:
• necessary (not neccesary) — one c, two s's
• beneficial / benefit (not benificial / benifit) — "bene" like "benefactor"
• efficient (not efficiant) — ends in -cient, not -ciant
• segregation (not segragation) — three e's: seg-RE-gation
• knowledge (not knoledge) — don't forget the "w"
• practicality (not pracitcallity)
PATTERN: THE -CIENT / -CIAL ENDING
Several of your slips are the same ending — efficient, beneficial. Both end in a "-shent" or "-shul" sound but are spelled with c + i + e/a.

• efficient = effi-ci-ent
• beneficial = benefi-ci-al
• necessary = nece-ss-ary (double s, one c)
Which sentence is spelled correctly throughout?

B High-speed trains are necessary and beneficial for urban transportation.

Read your paragraph one word at a time, from the last word to the first. Your brain stops auto-filling what it expects and actually looks at each word. Misspellings jump out. Takes under a minute per paragraph.
