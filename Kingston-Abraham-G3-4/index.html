Detail + Explanation

Q11 — how to develop your paragraph for more points

Q11 — 10 out of 20. You attempted the paragraph about the "From Sports and Society" passage, which is great. But to earn more points, each detail from the passage needs an explanation of why it matters.

Here is the pattern:

Detail + Explanation Pattern
D Detail: A specific fact or event from the passage.
E Explanation: Why that detail matters or what it shows about the topic.

For example, if the passage says sports became popular in the 1800s:

DETAIL
"By the late 1800s, men started to compete in organized sports."
EXPLANATION (WHY IT MATTERS)
"This was important because it gave ordinary people a way to earn respect, not just the wealthy."

Right now your paragraph lists facts but does not explain why each one matters. Adding even one explanation sentence after each detail can move you from 10 to 14 or higher.

THE "SO WHAT?" TEST
After you write a detail from the passage, ask yourself: "So what? Why does this matter?" Then write that answer as your next sentence. That is your explanation.

Paragraph Review

Q11 — 10 out of 20 — "From Sports and Society"

Here is what you wrote. Notice how the details are listed but not explained:

YOUR PARAGRAPH (Q11)
Moder sports has became a huge hit over the past years. Sports was only played by rich people back then. Then rules started to change. After that sports groups became leagues. Now sports have spread to many countries.

You have a topic sentence and several details — that is a solid start. Here is how to build on it:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D + E → D + E → D + E → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail  •  E = Explanation  •  CS = Closing Sentence
TS Modern sports have become a huge hit over the past years.
D Back then, sports were only played by rich people who had enough free time.
E This means that regular people did not have a chance to play or compete.
D Then, rules started to change and each game got a set of official rules.
E This was important because it made games fair for everyone.
D After that, sports groups became leagues so teams could compete regularly.
E Leagues helped sports grow because fans could follow their favorite teams.
CS Now, sports have spread to many countries and bring people together.

See how each E sentence answers "why does this matter?" That is what moves a paragraph from 10 to 16+.
