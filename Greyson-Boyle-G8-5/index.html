Fix 1: Answer the Why, Then This Shows

The prompt asked why readers need to understand both sides. The essay explained what the sides are
THE RULE: THE THESIS ANSWERS THE QUESTION ASKED; EVERY FACT GETS A SENTENCE THAT SAYS WHAT IT SHOWS
Read the prompt’s question word. Why is it important for readers to understand is a why question, so the thesis has to give reasons, and the body paragraphs prove them. After each fact from the passage comes a sentence that starts This shows and ties the fact back to the thesis. The last sentence of a body paragraph is one of these, not a summary of the paragraph. The check: could a reader list your reasons from the thesis alone? After each number or example, is the next sentence about what it proves, or the next fact?

Fix 4: The Conjunction Matches the Relationship

The one multiple-choice point, on G8.4 and again on G8.5
THE RULE: NAME THE RELATIONSHIP FIRST, THEN PICK THE WORD
A subordinating conjunction states how two ideas connect. Because gives the cause. If and unless give a condition that may or may not happen. Although gives a contrast. When, after, before give the time. The check: say the relationship in plain words before you look at the options. Is the first idea the reason for the second, a condition for it, a contrast to it, or the time it happens?
YOU CHOSE (G8.5 Q6)
“Because the technology fails, difficult questions arise about safety and trust.” A CAUSE WORD FOR A CONDITION
The passage does not say the technology fails; it asks what happens if it does. Because turns a possibility into a fact. If the technology fails, difficult questions arise.
YOU CHOSE (G8.4 Q5)
“Astronauts exercise daily because they must stay strong in microgravity.” A GOAL WHERE THE CAUSE GOES
The question asked which sentence best uses a subordinating conjunction. Because they must stay strong gives the goal, not the cause. The cause is in the passage: Because their muscles weaken without gravity, astronauts exercise daily. Your own G8.4 essay had it right: Exercise is very important on the ISS because muscles weaken faster in microgravity.
