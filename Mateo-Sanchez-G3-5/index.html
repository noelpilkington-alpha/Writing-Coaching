FULL PARAGRAPH (19-20/20)

"Little Golden Books, a popular series of children's books, changed how families enjoyed reading together. The passage says these books cost only 25 cents, which meant that almost any family could afford to buy them. This matters because before Little Golden Books, many children did not have books at home and could only read at libraries or school. Another way these books changed reading is that they had colorful pictures on every page. The passage explains that the bright illustrations made kids excited to turn the page and keep reading. This shows that Little Golden Books made reading feel fun instead of like a chore. These examples prove that Little Golden Books changed reading by making it affordable and enjoyable for all families."

COUNT THE PARTS
The full paragraph has:

1. Topic Sentence (with appositive — you already do this)
2. Detail 1: "cost only 25 cents"
3. Explain 1: "This matters because..."
4. Detail 2: "colorful pictures on every page"
5. Explain 2: "This shows that..."
6. Conclusion: "These examples prove..."

You wrote parts 1 and 2. You need to add parts 3, 4, 5, and 6. That is the whole fix.
