Error #1: Restructure the Sentence

Step 2 skill: Q1 asked for a question, not just adding "?"

Dei'Undre, Q1 asked you to turn a statement into a question. You added a question mark to the end, but you did not restructure the sentence into an interrogative form.

Q1 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"Little Golden Books were popular with families?"

The problem: Adding "?" to a statement does not make it a real question. A real question restructures the word order (starts with a question word or flips the subject and verb).

CORRECT: A REAL QUESTION
"Why were Little Golden Books so popular with families?"
THE QUESTION TEST
A real question:
• Starts with a question word (Who, What, Why, How, Did, Were, Is)
• Flips the subject and verb ("They were" becomes "Were they")
• Ends with a question mark

Just adding "?" to a statement does not count.
Which is a properly restructured question?
A"The books had colorful pictures?"
B"What made the books so popular with young readers?"
