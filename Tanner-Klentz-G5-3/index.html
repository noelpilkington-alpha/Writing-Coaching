THE 2-LAYER HABIT

Layer 2 — Why or how: add "because...", "for example...", or a specific detail.

Your #2 Error: Wrong Connecting Word

Picking "but" when the ideas need "so," or "because" when they need "but"

Tanner, the combining questions (Q2, Q4) give you a choice of because, but, so. You pick one, but sometimes the one you pick does not match how the ideas actually connect. Here are two real examples:

FROM G5.1 Q2 — scored 0.5 of 2
Original sentences: "I had always been curious about what she does at her desk. I wandered over to take a peek."

Your answer: "I had always been curious about what she does at her desk in the mornings because I wandered over to take a peek."
"Because" means the second action causes the first. Here the curiosity caused the peeking, so the right word is "so": "...curious about what she does, so I wandered over to take a peek."
FROM G5.2 Q4 — scored 1 of 2
Your answer: "On our last Sunday, we took our time and followed every step carefully, but the pie came out looking perfect, and we wondered how it would taste."
"But" signals contrast — something unexpected. Here, taking your time causing a perfect pie is expected, not a surprise. The right word is "so" or "and."
THE 3 CONNECTORS — WHAT EACH ONE MEANS
because → shows a reason. "I wore a jacket because it was cold."

but → shows a contrast or surprise. "I wore a jacket, but I was still cold."

so → shows a result or cause-and-effect. "It was cold, so I wore a jacket."

Quick test: ask yourself — does idea 2 happen because of idea 1 (use so), or does idea 1 happen because of idea 2 (use because), or do the two ideas surprise each other (use but)?
Which version uses the right connecting word?
A The camping trip was canceled, because Jaylen was disappointed.
B The camping trip was canceled, so Jaylen was disappointed.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Q2 and Q4 are worth 2 points each. Picking the right connector is worth 1–2 points per test. The skill is not new — you already know these words. The skill is pausing for 5 seconds to check which direction the ideas actually flow.
