Cause vs Effect

Q7 — 0 out of 1 — picking the enabler instead of the result
🔍 The error: The question asked about benefits for local businesses after solar panels were installed. You chose "Businesses could stay open for longer" — that is the cause (what the panels enabled). The correct answer was "The extra hours gave customers more time to shop" — that is the result (the actual benefit).
CAUSE vs EFFECT: WHICH ONE IS THE QUESTION ASKING FOR?
When a question says "What was the benefit?" it is asking for the end result, not the thing that made the result possible.

• Enabler (cause): Businesses could stay open for longer
• Result (effect/benefit): The extra hours gave customers more time to shop

The enabler is why the benefit happened. The benefit is what actually changed for the businesses. The question asks for the benefit — so pick the result.
A new passage says: "After the well was dug, villagers no longer had to walk 3 miles for water. Families gained an extra two hours each day, which parents used to start small vegetable gardens." The question asks: "How did the well benefit families?"

THE FIX: ASK "AND THEN WHAT?"
When a question asks about a benefit or effect, and you find an answer that sounds right, ask yourself: "And then what happened because of that?"

• "Businesses could stay open longer" → and then what? → Customers had more time to shop (that is the benefit)
• "Villagers did not have to walk 3 miles" → and then what? → Parents started gardens (that is the benefit)

If you can ask "and then what?" and get a further result, you are looking at the cause, not the effect. Go one step further to find the actual answer.

FILLED-IN OUTLINE FOR THIS PROMPT
Intro: A rural village installed solar panels. Before the panels, villagers relied on kerosene lamps and had limited hours of productivity. Solar panels benefited the village by improving education, boosting local businesses, and protecting health.

Body 1 (Education): Children can now study after sunset without straining their eyes. The passage states that academic progress increased because students had reliable light for homework. This matters because education is the path out of poverty for rural children.

Body 2 (Businesses): Local shops can stay open after dark, giving customers more time to shop. The passage notes that the extra hours led to increased sales. This matters because stronger businesses create jobs and keep money in the village.

Body 3 (Health): Families no longer breathe kerosene fumes from lamps. The passage explains that respiratory problems decreased after the switch to solar. This matters because healthier families can work and attend school more consistently.

Conclusion: Solar panels transformed the village by lighting homes, shops, and schools. What began as an energy project became a catalyst for education, economic growth, and better health.

Detail + Explanation

Each body paragraph needs a passage detail + why it matters
📚 What separates 14/20 from 18/20: A basic essay states the benefits. A strong essay uses specific details from the passage and then explains why those details matter. This is the Detail + Explanation pattern.
YOUR ATTEMPT (DETAIL WITHOUT EXPLANATION)
"Children can now study in the events without training their eyes, allowing for academic progress after sunset..."

You had a detail (children studying after sunset) and a half-formed explanation (academic progress). But it is crammed into one sentence with no development. Here is how to expand that into a full body paragraph:

THE DETAIL + EXPLANATION PATTERN
Topic sentence: "One of the biggest benefits of solar panels was the improvement in education."

Detail from passage: "The passage states that children can now study after sunset without straining their eyes, because solar-powered lights replaced dim kerosene lamps."

Explanation (why it matters): "This matters because students who can read and do homework in the evening make more academic progress, which gives them a better chance at opportunities beyond the village."
Which body paragraph uses the Detail + Explanation pattern correctly?
A "Solar panels helped local businesses. Shops can stay open longer because they have light. The businesses benefit from the solar panels because they can stay open."
B "Solar panels also boosted local businesses. The passage explains that shops can now stay open after dark, giving customers extra hours to browse and buy. This matters because increased sales allow shop owners to hire more workers and invest back into the village economy."
THE THREE-SENTENCE BODY PARAGRAPH
Every body paragraph you write should have at least these three sentences:

1. Topic sentence: State the point this paragraph will prove
2. Detail: "The passage states/explains/shows that [specific fact]."
3. Explanation: "This matters because [why it is important]."

That is only 3 sentences and you already have a solid paragraph. Add a fourth sentence with another detail or a connection, and you are at a strong 18/20 paragraph.
