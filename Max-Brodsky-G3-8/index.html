Question 1 Fix — Writing Appositives

Let's review this together.

You scored 1.0/2 on this question — close, but you lost 1.0 point.

The question asked:

"Rewrite the following sentence to include an appositive to give more information about Ms. Bright: Ms. Bright thanked Jordan for his help."

YOU WROTE
"Ms. Bright thatnked Jordan for his kindness, after he helped her put away the books."
HOW TO ADD AN APPOSITIVE
An appositive is a short phrase that renames or describes a noun. It goes right after the noun and is set off by commas: "Sally, the baby seal, swam away." Ask yourself: "Who or what is this?" and tuck the answer between commas.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Appositives make your writing richer because they pack extra information into one sentence. Using them well shows strong sentence construction skills.

WRITING SENTENCES FROM A PROMPT
Read the prompt carefully and answer exactly what it asks. Start your sentence by restating the key words from the prompt. Then add a reason or detail to support your answer. Make sure your sentence is complete with a subject, verb, and full thought.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Writing clear, on-topic sentences is the foundation of good writing. Every paragraph you write is made up of sentences like these, so mastering this skill helps everything.

WHAT YOU DID WELL
