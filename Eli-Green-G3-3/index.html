Fix 1: Echo the Question, then the Reason

Q7 and Q10. A full sentence first, then a because or an example from the story
THE RULE: ECHO THE QUESTION, THEN THE REASON
Turn the question into a full sentence that gives your answer. Then add because and a reason, or an example from the story. Two checks: cover the opening word (When, Yes). A full sentence must be left, with someone doing something. Then find the because or the example.

Q7 asked: What do you think was the most meaningful moment in the story? Explain why.

YOU WROTE (1 OUT OF 3)
NO ECHO“When the family helped sally get back to her family, because this was the right thing to do.”
“When the family helped Sally...” is a when-clause. It tells when, but nothing happens in the main part. There is no sentence here.
IN THE ECHO SHAPE
ECHOThe most meaningful moment was when the family took Sally back to her own family BECAUSEbecause it was the right thing to do and she finally got to go home.
