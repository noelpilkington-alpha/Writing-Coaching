Appositives vs. Conjunctions

Q4 — These are different tools

Q4 asked you to combine ideas using an appositive. You used "because" instead — that's a conjunction, not an appositive.

YOU WROTE
The books are still popular because they are known for their golden spines.

This is a good sentence! But it uses a conjunction ("because"), not an appositive. An appositive is a description placed right next to a noun, set off by commas.

Tap to see the improved version
Read the explanation above first
FIXED — WITH AN APPOSITIVE
The books, known for their golden spines, are still popular today.

Here's the difference:

Conjunction: "because," "but," "so" → Connects two ideas with a linking word
Appositive: noun, description, verb → Adds a description right next to the noun
HOW TO SPOT AN APPOSITIVE
An appositive renames or describes a noun and sits right next to it, surrounded by commas:

The Poky Little Puppy, a best-selling story, became famous.
Jason, an underwater artist, builds sculptures in the sea.

The interesting thing is — you already identified an appositive perfectly on Q5! Now you need to write them, not just find them.
More appositive examples
"My dog, a golden retriever, loves to swim."
"Pizza, my favorite food, is always good on Friday."
"Mrs. Johnson, our science teacher, showed us a cool experiment."

You have 4 sentences with good ideas. But notice: "build" should be builds, "stastues" and "ststues" should be statues, "visting" should be visiting. Your paragraph also needs a clear topic sentence that answers the prompt directly and more evidence from the passage.

Here's the G3 paragraph plan you should follow:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Jason deCaires Taylor chose to place his sculptures under the sea to protect marine life and attract visitors away from coral reefs.
D1 According to the article, Taylor noticed that coral reefs were being damaged by too many tourists.
W This is important because the reefs are home to many sea creatures and need to be protected.
D2 Taylor created underwater statues in places like the Caribbean and Cancun so people would visit those instead.
W This shows that his art serves a purpose — it draws tourists away from the fragile reefs.
D3 The passage explains that the statues also become new habitats as coral and sea life grow on them over time.
W This means the sculptures don't just protect the ocean — they actually help it grow.
CS Overall, Jason deCaires Taylor placed his sculptures underwater because they protect coral reefs while creating new homes for marine life.
TWO KEY FIXES FOR YOUR PARAGRAPH
1. Start with a topic sentence that answers the prompt: The prompt asks why Jason chose to place sculptures under the sea. Your first sentence should answer that question directly.

2. Add more details from the passage with "why it matters": You mentioned protecting marine life (great!) but didn't explain how or why in enough detail. After each fact, add a sentence explaining why it matters.
