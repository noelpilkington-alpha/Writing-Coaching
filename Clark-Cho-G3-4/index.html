Q1 and Q3: Answer the Exact Question

Step 1 catches these errors

Clark, Q1 asked for a stronger verb and Q3 asked you to change a sentence into an interrogative without changing the meaning. Both lost points because the answer did not match exactly what was asked.

Q1 (1/2) — "LOOKED" IS NOT STRONGER
The question asked you to replace a weak verb with a stronger one. "Looked" is a common verb, not a strong one. A stronger verb would be something like "examined," "studied," or "inspected."
STRONGER VERBS
A "stronger verb" means a more specific and vivid verb. Ask yourself: "Does this new verb paint a clearer picture?"

• "looked" → "examined" (more specific)
• "said" → "exclaimed" (more vivid)
• "went" → "rushed" (more specific)
Q3 (1.5/2) — CHANGED THE MEANING
You rewrote the sentence as a question (correct!) but changed what it was asking about. The original meaning must stay the same — only the sentence type changes.
The sentence is: "The students cleaned the classroom." Which is a stronger verb replacement for "cleaned"?
A "The students did the classroom."
B "The students scrubbed the classroom."
THE RULE
"Stronger" means more specific and vivid. If your replacement verb is common (looked, said, went, did), try again with a more descriptive one.
