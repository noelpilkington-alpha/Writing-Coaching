Develop Your Body Paragraphs

This is a Step 3 skill: plan each paragraph with evidence AND explanation

Andrew, your body paragraphs include good evidence from the passage, but they often stop after stating the fact. To earn full idea points, each piece of evidence needs an explanation sentence that tells the reader what the evidence means or why it matters.

📊 The pattern: You state evidence from the passage but do not explain what it proves. The grading feedback rewards the sequence: Evidence → Explanation → Connection to thesis. Without the explanation, you earn partial credit on ideas.
THE DETAIL + EXPLANATION FORMULA
For every piece of evidence you include, add a sentence that starts with one of these:

• "This shows that..."
• "This means that..."
• "This is significant because..."
• "In other words..."

Evidence alone = partial credit.
Evidence + Explanation = full credit.
Tap to see evidence vs. evidence + explanation
The difference between 15/20 and 18+/20
EVIDENCE ONLY (WHAT YOU WROTE)
"The government started teaching El Silbo in schools in 1999."
EVIDENCE + EXPLANATION (FULL CREDIT)
"The government started teaching El Silbo in schools in 1999. This shows that the community values El Silbo not just as a historical artifact, but as a living tradition worth passing to the next generation."
Which body paragraph earns full idea points?
A "El Silbo allows people to whistle across deep valleys. The whistles can travel up to six kilometers. People on La Gomera have used this for hundreds of years."
B "El Silbo allows people to whistle messages across valleys that stretch up to six kilometers. This means that even in the most remote parts of La Gomera, no one is truly isolated. The language bridges the physical landscape and connects people who would otherwise have to hike for hours to share a single message."
YOUR ESSAY PLANNING TEMPLATE
Before you write, plan each body paragraph with three parts:

1. Topic sentence: What is this paragraph about?
2. Evidence: What specific fact or quote from the passage supports this?
3. Explanation: Why does this evidence matter? How does it connect to your thesis?

If every body paragraph has all three, your essay will score 18+ out of 20.
