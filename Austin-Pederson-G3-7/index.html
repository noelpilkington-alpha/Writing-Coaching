Fix 1: A Paragraph in the Box, Every Time

Six sentences. You had every one of them in the passage
THE RULE: THE SIX-SENTENCE RECIPE
TOPIC SENTENCE answers the question in the question’s own words. Then two supporting details, each in two halves: SHOW a fact you can point to in the passage, then SO WHAT This shows… what the fact proves. Then a CONCLUDING SENTENCE that says the answer again in new words. The check: six sentences, two of them starting This shows. An empty box is 0. Six plain sentences are usually 12 or more.

Fix 2: Whole Sentence, Right Question

Q6 and Q10: three points, two habits
THE RULE: ECHO THE QUESTION, THEN BECAUSE
Start the answer with the question’s own words, then give the reason from the passage. The check: two questions to ask your sentence. Does it have a main part before the because? Does it use the key words of the question that was asked?

Q6 asked why pumpkin seeds need warm soil to sprout:

YOU WROTE (1 OUT OF 3)
“Because they are used to no water.” NO MAIN PART
Starts with Because and stops. The reason is not in the passage either: it says seeds sprout in spring when the soil warms up.
ECHO, THEN BECAUSE
Pumpkin seeds need warm soil to sprout BECAUSEbecause the passage says gardeners plant them in spring, when the soil warms up.

Q10 asked why someone might save pumpkin seeds to plant in spring:

YOU WROTE (2 OUT OF 3)
“People might save seed for a snack.” DIFFERENT QUESTION
A whole sentence, and true, but the question said plant in spring, not snack.
ECHO, THEN BECAUSE
Someone might save pumpkin seeds to plant in spring BECAUSEbecause dried seeds can grow a whole new pumpkin plant the next year.
