Detail + Explanation

The pattern to use when you write your Q11 paragraph

When you attempt Q11 next time, use this pattern to earn strong points. Each sentence pair uses a detail from the passage followed by an explanation of what it shows.

Detail + Explanation Pattern
D Detail: A specific fact or event from the passage.
E Explanation: Why that detail matters or what it shows about the topic.

Here is an example using the "Dive into a Sunken Museum" passage:

DETAIL
"The underwater museum has over 500 sculptures sitting on the ocean floor."
EXPLANATION (WHY IT MATTERS)
"This shows that the museum is very large and gives divers many things to explore, which makes it a special place to visit."

A simple paragraph plan for Q11:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D + E → D + E → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail  •  E = Explanation  •  CS = Closing Sentence
TS State the main idea the passage teaches you.
D Pick one interesting fact from the passage.
E Explain why that fact matters or what it shows.
D Pick a second fact from the passage.
E Explain why that one matters too.
CS Wrap up by restating the main idea in different words.
THE "SO WHAT?" TEST
After you write a detail from the passage, ask yourself: "So what? Why does this matter?" Then write that answer as your next sentence. That is your explanation.
