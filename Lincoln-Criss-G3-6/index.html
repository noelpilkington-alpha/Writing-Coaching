The prompt asked how the porcupine's body helps it stay safe. You gave 4 facts about porcupines, but only one is about staying safe. The paragraph needs a clear central idea and analysis sentences.
THE FIX: "FACT + SO WHAT?"
Every time you write a fact from the passage, the very next sentence should be a "this shows..." sentence. Example:

Fact: Porcupines have more than 30,000 stiff quills.
Partner: This shows that their main defense is passive — they don't need to fight or run, just flex their quills.

The fact is the evidence. The "this shows" is the analysis. Top paragraphs always have both.

B Porcupines have 30,000 quills that stick into predators. This shows that they can stop an attacker without fighting back. They also climb trees with strong claws, which keeps them out of reach of hungry animals on the ground.

Q3–Q4 trip you up about half the time

Lincoln, this is a smaller but recurring pattern. Questions that ask you to use an appositive or combine sentences in a specific way sometimes lose you half a point because you combine the ideas but don't use the exact structure the prompt asked for.

FROM G3.4 Q8 (short answer — scored 0.67/3)
Prompt: What do you think was the most important moment in the story? Explain in one sentence.
You wrote: "I think the most important moment in the story was when Layla took the geode to the guide."
This tells what happened but does not explain why it was important. The question asked you to explain.
FROM G3.6 Q4 (rewrite as a question — scored 0.75/2)
Prompt: Rewrite sentence 3 as a question: These sharp quills protect them from danger.
You wrote: "Do the porcupines sharp quills protect them from danger?"
Close! The missing piece is the apostrophe: porcupines' or porcupine's. An apostrophe shows possession.
THE RULE FOR "EXPLAIN" QUESTIONS
When a prompt says "explain" or asks "why", your answer MUST include a because clause or a sentence that gives the reason.

✗ "The most important moment was when Layla took the geode to the guide." (tells what)
✓ "The most important moment was when Layla took the geode to the guide because it showed she chose honesty over keeping something beautiful." (tells why)

B Do the porcupine's sharp quills protect them from danger?

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20/20 level)

A porcupine's body helps it stay safe in the wild through its quills, its claws, and its warning signals. First, its quills are actually long, stiff hairs, and porcupines have more than 30,000 of them. This shows that their body is designed to keep them high up where most hungry animals cannot reach them. In the end, porcupines survive not because they are fast or strong, but because their body is built for passive defense.
