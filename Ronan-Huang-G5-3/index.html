THE RULE: TOPIC SENTENCE, THEN SHOW, THEN SO WHAT

Fix 3: Because Carries the Reason

THE RULE: WHEN THE INSTRUCTION SAYS BECAUSE, THE WORD IS IN YOUR ANSWER
Some Q1 to Q5 instructions name the exact word: expand using because, combine using since. The word has to appear, followed by a reason in your own words. The check: find because in your sentence. Then cover everything in front of it and read the rest. Is it a why, or is it a description of how someone looked or what they could not do?

WITH BECAUSE

Fix 4: A Question the Sentence Answers

Q5: rewrite sentence 5 as an interrogative. Yours asked something new
THE RULE: HELPING VERB TO THE FRONT, EVERY FACT KEPT
Interrogative means a question. To turn a statement into one, put Did, Do, Does, Is, Was, Were or Had at the front and keep the rest: The Thompsons camped at Willow State Park every summer becomes Did the Thompsons camp at Willow State Park every summer? The check: answer your own question. If the answer is the original sentence, you did the job. If your question starts with how, why or when, it asks for something the sentence never said.
YOU WROTE (Q5, 1 OUT OF 2)
“Every summer, how did the Thompsons camp at Willow State Park?” ASKS SOMETHING NEW
Answer it: in tents? by car? Sentence 5 never said how. A question mark, correct grammar, camped correctly turned into did…camp, and a different question.
A QUESTION THAT SENTENCE 5 ANSWERS
Did the Thompsons camp at Willow State Park every summer?
Answer: yes, every summer the Thompsons camped at Willow State Park. That is sentence 5, so the job is done.
