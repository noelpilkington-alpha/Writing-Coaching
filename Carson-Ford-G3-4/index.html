Step 2: Identify the Skill — Q3

The question asked for a question. You wrote a statement.

What happened on Q3: The question asked you to rewrite a sentence as an interrogative (a question). You rewrote it as a declarative (a statement). The answer was scored 0 out of 2 because it did not do what was asked.
Q3 — YOUR ANSWER (0 out of 2 weighted points)
You rewrote the sentence as a statement instead of turning it into a question.

The question asked: Rewrite the sentence as an interrogative sentence.

The problem: An interrogative sentence ends with a question mark. It asks something. Your answer was still a statement — it tells something. That is a declarative sentence, not an interrogative one.

WHAT AN INTERROGATIVE LOOKS LIKE
Original: "The geode felt like a perfect souvenir."
Interrogative: "Did the geode feel like a perfect souvenir?"

The fix: flip the sentence so it asks instead of tells. Start with "Did," "Was," "Is," "Can," or "Does" and end with a question mark.
STEP 2 IN ACTION
Before writing your answer, ask: "What type of sentence does the question want?"

• Interrogative = a question (ends with ?)
• Declarative = a statement (ends with .)
• Exclamatory = shows strong feeling (ends with !)

If it says "interrogative," your answer must end with a question mark. If it does not, stop and rewrite it.
The directions say: "Rewrite as an interrogative." Which answer follows the direction?

B Did the dog run quickly across the yard?
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q3 was worth 2 weighted points and you earned 0. That is the single biggest point loss on the test. And the fix is simple: read the direction, identify the skill (make a question), write a question, check that it ends with "?". Two points back.
