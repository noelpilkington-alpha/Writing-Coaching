The fix that wins back the most points
🔍 Pattern alert: On both Q1 and Q3 you wrote a good sentence — but it answered a slightly different task than the one asked. The trick is to make the exact change to the given sentence and keep its meaning.

EXACT-TASK ANSWER (2 of 2 points)

THE EXACT-TASK RULE
Before you answer, underline in your mind the action word in the question: concise, question, combine, declarative.

✗ Wrong = you write a new sentence or change the meaning.
✓ Right = you do that one exact change to the GIVEN sentence and keep its meaning.
