Error #1: Answer the Exact Question

Q4 — 1 out of 2 — wrote explanation instead of sentence type

On Q4, the question asked you to identify the sentence type. You wrote an explanation of what the sentence does instead of naming the type.

Q4 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2)
"This sentence tells us about what happened at the park."

The problem: The question wants the NAME of the sentence type (declarative, interrogative, imperative, or exclamatory). You explained the content instead of identifying the type.

THE CORRECT ANSWER
"This is a declarative sentence."
THE FIX
When a question says "identify the type," it wants a label. Read the question twice: "identify" = name it. Do not explain what it does. Just name the category.
The question says: "Identify the sentence type." Which answer is correct?
A"It is telling us something important about the story."
B"This is a declarative sentence."
YOUR RULE
When you see "identify," "name," or "what type," the answer is a label. Keep it short and direct.

Error #2: Catch Spelling Errors

Q7 ("thier") and Q11 ("herd") — common words misspelled
Q7 — SPELLING ERROR
"...because thier ideas are important..."

Should be: their (t-h-e-i-r). "I before E" in this word.

Q11 — SPELLING ERROR
"She herd the news and was surprised."

Should be: heard (h-e-a-r-d). "Herd" is a group of animals. "Heard" is the past tense of "hear."

THE FIX
"Their": Remember the pattern: t-h-e-i-r. The "e" comes before the "i." Think: "the" is hiding inside "their."

"Heard": Think of the word "ear" inside "heard." You hear with your ear. H-ear-d.
Which sentence uses "their" and "heard" correctly?
A"Thier friends herd the good news yesterday."
B"Their friends heard the good news yesterday."
MEMORY TRICKS
• Their: "The" is hiding inside (t-h-e-i-r)
• Heard: "Ear" is hiding inside (h-ear-d)
