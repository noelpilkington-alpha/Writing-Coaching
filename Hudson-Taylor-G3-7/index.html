Your #2 Error: Sentence Expansion on Q1–Q5

When the prompt says "add detail," you need MORE words, not just different ones

Hudson, on G3.7 Q3 the prompt asked you to expand a sentence with another detail from the passage. You changed the sentence but did not really add anything new. This also showed up on Q1 and Q2.

🔍 Pattern alert: On editing questions that ask to expand or combine, you lose partial credit when the new sentence has the same amount of information as the old one.
FROM G3.7 Q3 — THE PROMPT
"Sentence 5 tells us what a pumpkin seed looks like on the outside. Add another detail from the passage to expand sentence 5 into a complete sentence with more information."
Original: "A pumpkin seed is flat and white."
WHAT YOU WROTE (scored 1 of 2)
"A pumpkin seed is flat, white, and small."
You added "small," but that is just another surface trait — not a new detail from the passage. The grader counted it as a light expansion, not a full one.
Which version earns the full 2 of 2 points?
A A pumpkin seed is flat, white, and small.
B A pumpkin seed is flat and white, and inside it holds a tiny plant that can grow into a new pumpkin vine.
WHEN THE PROMPT SAYS "EXPAND" OR "ADD DETAIL"
Look back at the passage. Pull a fact the original sentence did NOT mention — a new action, a new purpose, a new number or name. Then join it with and, because, or which.

✗ Adding a second adjective (flat + white + small) — the grader reads this as restating, not expanding.
✓ Adding a new fact (what is inside, what it does, where it comes from).
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
On Q1–Q5 you have been landing at half credit (1 of 2) on the "combine" and "expand" prompts instead of full credit (2 of 2). That is up to 5 points on every test. Pull a real detail from the passage and that gap closes fast.
