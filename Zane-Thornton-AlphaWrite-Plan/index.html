Fix 3: The Ending Says What the Details Add Up To

Ten of nineteen real tries gave one detail or the topic sentence again. Your five passes all did the same thing
THE RULE: NOT A DETAIL, NOT THE TOPIC SENTENCE AGAIN. WHAT THE DETAILS ADD UP TO
The topic sentence is already on the page and the details are the evidence. The ending names what the details are together and says what they add up to, in one short sentence the topic sentence did not already say. Two of the lesson’s own examples have this shape: Good equipment helps baseball players avoid injuries. These steps help players make great catches. A group word for the details, then what that group does. Others give a final thought the topic sentence did not say (Fans will always remember this famous slugger.). Adding the details up is the surer way to pass. A final thought that says something new also passes. Two checks: can you point to the one detail your sentence is about? Then it is a detail. Cover the topic sentence and read yours: does it say anything new? If not, it is the topic sentence again.
