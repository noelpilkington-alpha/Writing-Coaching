Fix 1: Show, then So What

After every reason: a fact you could point to, then what it proves
THE RULE: SHOW, THEN SO WHAT
After every reason, add one fact from the passage, a thing you could point to in the text. Then one sentence that starts This shows. The check: could a reader find your fact in the passage? If not, it is not a fact yet.
