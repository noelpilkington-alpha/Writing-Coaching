THE RULE: POINT TO THE FACT, THEN SO WHAT
Every supporting detail starts with a fact from the passage, then a This shows sentence that says what the fact proves. Fix 2: One Sentence Means One Sentence

Q7, Q8, Q9: three points, one habit
THE RULE: ONE MAIN PART, ONE REASON, ONE PERIOD
A one-sentence answer has a main part, a because or an example, and one period. The check: read it aloud. Could you cut it into two or three sentences with periods? Then it is two or three sentences, and the grading counts that against it. Does it start with Because and never get a main part? Then it is half a sentence.

Q8 asked for one sentence about something that changes as the pumpkin grows, and why:

YOU WROTE (2 OUT OF 3)

Three sentences in one, 38 words, and the why is missing.
ONE SENTENCE
As pumpkins grow, they change from small and green to big and orange BECAUSEbecause they stop growing and ripen in the fall.
