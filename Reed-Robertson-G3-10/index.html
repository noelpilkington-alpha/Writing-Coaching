Q1 Appositive + Q6 Verb Tense

Two more fixable errors

Q1 (1/2): You tried an appositive ("a wood bicycle") but the sentence structure got tangled. The appositive idea is right. The execution needs work.

APPOSITIVE STRUCTURE
An appositive goes right after the noun it renames, between commas:
"The draisine, a wooden bicycle without pedals, was invented in 1817."

Keep the rest of the sentence the same. Just insert the renaming phrase.

Q6 (2/3): You wrote "push" (present tense) but the passage is about history, so the correct tense is "pushed" (past tense).

TENSE CHECK
If the passage is telling a story about the past, your answer should use past tense. Before submitting, ask: "Is this passage about now or about the past?" If it is about the past, check that your verbs end in "-ed" where needed.
The passage describes how early riders moved their bikes in the 1800s. Which verb tense is correct?
A"Riders push their feet along the ground to move forward."
B"Riders pushed their feet along the ground to move forward."
TENSE RULE
Historical passage = past tense verbs. Check: does my verb match the time period?
