Build an Appositive From Scratch

You can spot them. Now let's build them.

FROM G3.6 Q3 (Apr 2026)
Prompt: "Combine using an appositive."
You wrote: "Porcupines are rodents, and they are slow movers..."
(Used “and” — that’s a conjunction, not an appositive.)
FROM G3.1 Q1 (Nov 2025)
You wrote: "My family has 2 dogs, named Lucy and Rocket"
(A reduced clause. The appositive would be: “My family has two dogs, Lucy and Rocket.”)
WHAT IS AN APPOSITIVE?
An appositive is a noun phrase that renames or describes another noun. It sits right next to the noun, set off by commas (or dashes).

Formula: Noun, [appositive phrase], rest of sentence.

✓ My dog, a scruffy terrier, loves the beach.
✓ Porcupines, slow-moving rodents, protect themselves with sharp quills.
✓ Anna, the girl who found the seal, called her mom.
Prompt: "Combine using an appositive: Porcupines are rodents. Porcupines are slow movers." Which rewrite is a correct appositive?
A Porcupines are rodents, and they are slow movers.
B Porcupines, slow-moving rodents, use their quills for protection.
CORRECT BUILDS FOR YOUR 3 PROMPTS
• "My family has two dogs, Lucy and Rocket." — names ARE the appositive, no word “named” needed
• "Porcupines, slow-moving rodents, protect themselves with sharp quills."
• "Anna, a caring girl from the beach, found the baby seal."
THE 3-STEP BUILD
1. Find the main noun in your sentence.
2. Write a short noun phrase that renames it (no verb, no “and”).
3. Drop it right after the noun, between two commas.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Question 3 on most tests asks you to BUILD an appositive — that’s worth about 2 points per test. You’ve been losing those points for 6 tests. Learn the 3-step build, practice 3 times, and you lock those points in forever.

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (18–19/20 level)

Britain is an exciting place to visit because of its famous landmarks and rich history. For example, Big Ben is one of the most recognized clock towers in the world, and it has kept time over London for more than 160 years. In addition, Britain is home to ancient castles where kings and queens once lived, which gives visitors a glimpse of the past. These landmarks make Britain a country that is both fun and full of history.

Your Paragraph Upgrade Formula
THESIS Name the claim: "X is Y because of [specific reason 1] and [specific reason 2]."
DETAIL 1 Use "For example," + one fact from the passage
DETAIL 2 Use "In addition," + a second fact from the passage
CLOSE Restate the thesis in different words
