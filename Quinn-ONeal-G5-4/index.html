Step 4: Preserve Meaning When Rewriting

Q5 (1/2) — your rewrite changed the meaning

Quinn, on Q5 the question asked you to rewrite a sentence using different words while keeping the same meaning. Your version changed what the sentence actually says.

THE MEANING-CHECK
After rewriting a sentence, ask: "Does my new version say the same thing as the original?"

• Same people doing the same actions?
• Same cause leading to the same effect?
• Same time and place?

If anything changed, revise until the meaning matches.
Original: "Claudette refused to move because she believed the law was unjust." Which rewrite preserves the meaning?
ABecause Claudette thought the rule was unfair, she stayed in her seat.
BClaudette was too scared to stand up on the bus.
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q5 is worth 2 points. You earned 1 instead of 2 because the meaning shifted. A quick meaning-check during your proofread catches this every time.
