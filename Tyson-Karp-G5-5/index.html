Choosing the Right Conjunction

because / but / so — matching the word to the relationship

Tyson, your second most frequent error is swapping conjunctions — using "but" when you need "so," or "so" when you need "because." Each word means something specific:

Conjunction Reference
because Gives a REASON. Answers "why?" — "Jaylen smiled because Kim brought him soup." (Why did he smile? Kim brought soup.)
but Shows CONTRAST. Two parts are opposite or unexpected. "It rained but we still played outside." (Rain usually stops play — but not here.)
so Shows a RESULT. Something happened as a result. "It rained so we went inside." (Rain caused us to go inside.)
FROM G5.3 Q2 — wrong conjunction
You used "but" where "so" was needed (cause-and-result, not contrast).
FROM G5.5 Q1 — wrong conjunction
You swapped "so" for "because" — two different relationships, two different words.
Combine: "It rained hard. We went inside." Which conjunction fits?
A It rained hard but we went inside.
B It rained hard so we went inside.
THE 3-QUESTION TEST
Before you write a conjunction, ask yourself one question:

• Am I giving a REASON (answering why)? → use because
• Am I showing an OPPOSITE or UNEXPECTED idea? → use but
• Am I showing what HAPPENED AS A RESULT? → use so
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Combine-sentence questions appear on every test. Using the wrong conjunction changes the meaning of the sentence, which earns only partial credit. A 3-second check (reason? contrast? result?) is usually all it takes to pick right.

What's the best FIRST move before writing Q11?

YOUR Q11 ROUTINE
1. On scratch paper, write one declarative sentence that answers the prompt (your thesis).
2. Check: does it use the correct character names? Does it make a claim (not ask a question)?
3. Scan the passage for 2 pieces of evidence that support your thesis.
4. Write your paragraph: Topic → Evidence 1 → Explain → Evidence 2 → Explain → Conclusion.
5. Re-read: does every sentence end with . ? or !? No rhetorical questions inside your argument.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
