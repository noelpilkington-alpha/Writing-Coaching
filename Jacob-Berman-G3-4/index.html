MODEL PARAGRAPH USING SPECIFIC DETAILS

Modern sports developed step by step as people made them more organized. First, people wrote down clear rules for each sport, so the games were fair and everyone played the same way. Next, towns built large stadiums where big crowds could gather to watch. Selling tickets gave teams money to keep playing and to pay their players. Finally, teams joined leagues so they could compete season after season, which gave fans a reason to follow their favorite team. Little by little, these changes turned simple games into the popular sports we cheer for today.

Do the Exact Edit the Question Asks

Question 1 — one small habit worth a full point

Jacob, Question 1 asked you to make the verb stronger and more descriptive. You added a good reason — but you kept the weak verb "looked." Here is what you wrote:

YOU WROTE (G3.4 Q1 — scored 1 of 2)
"Layla looked at the flower because she knew butterflies loved it."

The reason ("because she knew butterflies loved it") is a nice touch — but the question wanted a stronger verb. "Looked" is still a plain, everyday word. The fix is to swap it for a vivid one.

The question says "change the verb to make it stronger." Which answer does that?
A Layla looked at the flower very carefully for a long time.
B Layla studied the flower closely.
THE RULE: MATCH THE EXACT TASK
When a question asks you to change the verb, your main job is to swap the weak verb for a vivid one. Adding a reason is fine, but it does not replace the edit that was asked for.

Stronger verbs for "looked": studied, admired, gazed at, examined, watched.

✗ "Layla looked at the flower because..." (kept the weak verb)
✓ "Layla admired the flower because she knew butterflies loved it." (stronger verb AND a reason)
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
This was the only short-answer point you missed all test — the difference between 24/25 and a perfect 25/25 on Questions 1–10. Reading the task closely and doing the exact edit asked is a one-second habit that locks in those points.

Modern sports developed slowly as people made them more organized and fun to watch. First, people invented sports by brainstorming ideas because they wanted more active things to do.
