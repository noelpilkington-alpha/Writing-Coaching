Step 2: Identify the Skill — Stronger Verb

Q1 — 1 out of 2 weighted points

Mateo, Q1 asked you to replace a verb with a stronger verb. You added adjectives instead of changing the verb itself.

Q1 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"The brave explorer looked carefully at the deep, dark cave."

The question asked: Replace "looked" with a stronger verb.

The problem: You added "carefully" and extra adjectives, but the verb is still "looked." The question wants a different, more vivid verb in place of "looked."

CORRECT — STRONGER VERB
"The explorer peered into the cave." or "The explorer gazed into the cave."
THE VERB SWAP RULE
When the question says "stronger verb," it means:

• Find the original verb (like "looked")
• Replace it with a more specific verb (like "peered," "gazed," "squinted," "stared")
• Do NOT add adjectives or adverbs. Change the verb itself.
The sentence says "The cat walked across the room." Which uses a stronger verb?
AThe fluffy cat walked slowly across the big room.
BThe cat crept across the room.
THE PAYOFF
This one fix is worth 1 point. Small change, clear rule: when it says "stronger verb," swap the verb. Do not add words around it.

Step 2: Identify the Skill — Actions Reveal Character

Q6 — 1 out of 3 weighted points

Mateo, Q6 asked what a character's actions tell us about them. You listed personality traits but did not name a specific action from the passage.

Q6 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 3 weighted points)
"He is brave, kind, and helpful to his friends."

The question asked: What do the character's actions tell us about him?

The problem: Your answer lists traits (brave, kind, helpful) but never names a specific action from the story. The question wants: action first, then what it reveals.

FULL-CREDIT ANSWER
"When the character shares his last piece of bread with the younger boy, this action shows he is kind and puts others before himself."
THE ACTION-TO-TRAIT PATTERN
Step 1: Name the specific action (what the character DID)
Step 2: Say what that action REVEALS about the character

Think of it as: "When [character] [does X], this shows [trait]."
The question asks: "What do Maria's actions tell us about her?" Which earns full points?

BWhen Maria stays after school to help her classmate understand the math problem, this shows she is patient and cares about helping others succeed.
