How This Guide Works

Four steps for every word, one try per word today
THE ROUTINE: HEAR IT, SAY IT, SEE ITS PARTS, TYPE IT
1. Hear it. Tap Hear it. If your device has no voice, tap Show it for 3 seconds and read it while it is there. 2. Say it back out loud, in beats: pro-tec-tion. 3. Look at the marked part and the rule above it. That is the part your ear cannot hear. 4. Type it and tap Check, or press Enter. One try per word today. A miss shows you the word with the hard part marked. You type it once more to fix it. Twelve of these words come back in the return set two days from now. On the sound-alike cards there is no Hear it button: the sentence is the clue, so read it, say the word, then type.

Why typing and not picking. Picking a spelling from a list only tests you. Building the word from its sound, its parts and its pattern is what makes it stay. So nothing on these cards is tap-to-choose except a few warm-ups before the typing.

THE RULE: EACH PAIR HAS ONE HOOK. LEARN THE HOOK, NOT THE WORD
Eight pairs below, each with the hook that decides. Most hooks are a letter you can see but not hear.
too / to
Too means also or too much: the extra o is the extra. To points somewhere or sits before a verb.
Yours: it was to wet, it was to late. Too much, so too.
would / wood
Would is a helping word, spelled like could and should. Wood comes from trees, oo like look.
Yours: it wood not be fun, three drafts. A helping word, so would.
which / witch
Which asks or chooses, wh like what, when, where. A witch has a broom and a t.
Yours: witch is supper bright light. Choosing, so which.
know / no
Know is in your head, with a silent k like knee and knife. No is the opposite of yes.
Yours: I don’t no the name. In your head, so know.
by / buy
By means next to, or done by someone. Buy means pay, with a u for the money you hand over.
Yours: helps people buy lifting cars. Done by lifting, so by.
right / write
Right is correct, or the direction, with gh like light and night. Write needs a pencil, w like words.
Yours: if you have the wright things. Correct, so right.
heal / heel
Heal means get better, ea like health. Heel is the back of your foot, ee like feet.

win / when
Win is to come first, one short i. When asks about time, wh like where. Wen is neither.

Endings: Using, Running, Rarities, Lives

Useing, runing, rarity’s, life’s, wolfs: five rules at the join
THE RULE: FIVE THINGS HAPPEN AT THE JOIN
1. Drop the silent e before -ing: use, using. make, making. 2. One short vowel then one consonant: double it before -ing or -ed: run, running. hit, hitting. stop, stopped. Compare hope, hoping with hop, hopping. The double letter shuts the vowel in, the same as super and supper. 3. y turns to i before -es: rarity, rarities. party, parties. 4. f turns to v before -es: life, lives. wolf, wolves. knife, knives. 5. -ly turns a describing word into a how word: different, differently.

The Apostrophe

Thats seven times, Im, cant, wont, dont, and rarity’s where none belongs
THE RULE: THE APOSTROPHE STANDS FOR MISSING LETTERS, OR MARKS AN OWNER. NEVER A PLURAL
Missing letters. that is, that’s (the i went). I am, I’m. cannot, can’t. do not, don’t. what is, what’s. will not, won’t, the odd one. An owner. the dog’s caretaker, ice cream’s cold temperature. Never a plain plural or a plain verb. rarities, lives, does: no apostrophe. Yours put one in rarity’s, life’s, doe’s and left it out of thats seven times, Im, cant, wont, dont.
