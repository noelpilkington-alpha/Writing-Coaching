HOW TO CHANGE A SENTENCE TYPE
To turn a statement into a question, move the helping verb to the front (or add one like "Did" or "Does"), and end with a question mark. Keep all names capitalized and make sure every word is spelled correctly.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
On writing tests, Changing Sentence Types questions check whether you can restructure a sentence without losing any details. Careful attention to punctuation and capitals is the difference between full credit and partial.

WRITING COMPLETE SENTENCE ANSWERS
A complete answer has three parts: (1) Restate the question in your answer, (2) State your opinion or answer, (3) Give a reason or explanation using "because." A one-word answer or a sentence without explanation will lose points.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Most sentence-writing questions on tests are worth 3 points. Adding a clear explanation with 'because' is often the difference between partial credit and full credit.
