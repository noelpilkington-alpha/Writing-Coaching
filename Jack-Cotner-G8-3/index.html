Your Recurring Pattern: Evidence Without Explanation

The same gap on G8.1, G8.2, and G8.3 — 14/20 each time
📊 Three tests, same score, same reason: Your essay body paragraphs consistently include a claim and evidence from the passage, but they stop short of explaining what the evidence proves. This is the "Detail + Explanation" gap. You state facts from the text but do not connect them back to your thesis.

Here is what happens in your body paragraphs:

TYPICAL BODY PARAGRAPH PATTERN (FROM G8.3)
"[Claim]. The passage states that [quote or paraphrase from text]." ← stops here

What is missing is the explanation sentence that says: "This shows that..." or "This matters because..." or "This connects to the thesis by proving that..."

THE PATTERN THAT EARNS 18-20/20
"[Claim]. The passage states that [evidence]. This demonstrates that [explanation connecting evidence to thesis]."
THE D+E FORMULA
Every body paragraph needs this sequence:

D (Detail): A specific quote or paraphrase from the passage.
E (Explanation): One sentence explaining HOW that detail supports your claim.

Think of it this way: the Detail answers "What does the text say?" The Explanation answers "So what? Why does that matter?"
Which body paragraph includes proper explanation after the evidence?
A "The author uses imagery to describe the setting. The passage states that the hills were covered in mist and the river ran silently through the valley. The author also mentions birds flying overhead."
B "The author uses imagery to describe the setting. The passage states that the hills were covered in mist and the river ran silently through the valley. This creates a mood of isolation that mirrors the main character's loneliness after losing his companion."
YOUR PROOFREADING CHECK
After you finish your essay, go through each body paragraph and look for the explanation sentence. If a paragraph ends with evidence but no "This shows..." or "This demonstrates..." or "This matters because..." sentence, add one. That is the single highest-value revision you can make.

Building Stronger Explanations

Three sentence starters that turn 14/20 into 18/20

Jack, your evidence selection is good. You pull relevant details from the passage. The problem is not what you choose to cite. It is what you do after you cite it. Here are three ways to turn evidence into explanation:

Explanation Sentence Starters
WHY "This demonstrates that..." or "This reveals that..." — connects the evidence to a larger claim.
HOW "By doing this, the author shows..." — explains the technique and its effect on the reader.
SO WHAT "This matters because..." or "This connects to the thesis by..." — ties it directly back to your argument.
Tap to see a before/after example
Same evidence, with and without explanation
BEFORE (EVIDENCE ONLY — 14/20 LEVEL)
"The author describes how the community worked together to rebuild the bridge. According to the text, 'every family in the village contributed materials and labor to the project.'"
AFTER (EVIDENCE + EXPLANATION — 18/20 LEVEL)
"The author describes how the community worked together to rebuild the bridge. According to the text, 'every family in the village contributed materials and labor to the project.' This demonstrates that the bridge was not merely a physical structure but a symbol of collective identity, since its reconstruction required the same unity that originally defined the community."

The bolded sentence is the explanation. It takes the evidence ("every family contributed") and explains WHY that matters to the argument (the bridge symbolizes unity). That one sentence is worth 2 to 3 points.

You just wrote: "The passage states that the main character refused to leave." Which follow-up sentence adds proper explanation?
A "This refusal reveals that his loyalty to his family outweighed his instinct for self-preservation, which is the central tension the author builds throughout the story."
B "He stayed in the house even though the storm was getting worse and everyone else had already left for higher ground."
THE PAYOFF
Each body paragraph that adds a proper explanation sentence is worth roughly 1 to 2 points. With three body paragraphs in your essay, adding explanation to each one could move you from 14/20 to 18 or 19/20. That takes your overall score from 83% to 90%+.
