Answer the EXACT Question Asked

The biggest reason your paragraph dipped to 11/20

Jacob, this is the most important card in your review. On Question 11, the prompt asked: WHY did Jason place his sculptures under the sea? Here is what you wrote:

YOU WROTE (G3.5 Q11)
"Artist Jason deCaires Taylor has made more than 500 life sized statues built in the Carribbean. He built 500 statues because he really enjoyed making it... The first statue is near Grenada and the second statue is in Cancun, Mexico."

Your sentences are clear and you used real facts from the passage. The trouble is what they answer. You told the reader how many statues there are and where they sit — but the question asked WHY he put them under the sea. The facts are correct, but they answer a different question.

💡 The pattern: When a prompt asks WHY, the reader is looking for a reason. "There are 500 statues" and "they are near Grenada" are facts about how many and where — not reasons why he placed them underwater.
The prompt asks: WHY did Jason place his sculptures under the sea? Which sentence actually answers that?
A The first statue is near Grenada and the second is in Cancun, Mexico.
B He placed them under the sea because the ocean slowly covers them with seaweed and sponges, turning the art into a living reef.
HOW TO LOCK ONTO THE QUESTION
1. Find the question word: WHY, HOW, or WHAT.
2. Say it back to yourself: "The question wants a REASON he put them underwater."
3. Make your first sentence start with the answer: "Jason placed them under the sea because..."
4. Check each sentence: does this help explain WHY? If it only tells how many or where, it does not count.

MODEL PARAGRAPH (answers WHY)

Jason placed his sculptures under the sea because the ocean turns his art into something alive. Over time, sponges and seaweed grow on the statues, and they slowly become a living reef. This shows that he wanted the art to change and become a home for ocean creatures. Fish and other animals move in, so the sculptures become a real museum under the water. In the end, Jason placed his art beneath the sea so nature could finish it and give sea life a new home.

Finish Questions & Give Real Reasons

Two small fixes from Q1 and Q7 worth easy points

RULE 1 — QUESTIONS END WITH "?"
If the prompt asks you to write a question, your answer must end with a question mark. A question that ends with a period is not finished. Check the very last character before you move on.
RULE 2 — A REASON ADDS NEW INFORMATION
A real reason explains why with new facts. It does not just repeat the word.

✗ "It felt special because it teaches them something very special." (goes in a circle)
✓ "It felt special because the puppy learned to follow the rules and still got dessert." (gives a real reason)
THE TEST FOR A GOOD REASON
After you write "because...", read it back and ask: did I add a new fact, or did I just say the same word again? If you repeated the word, swap in a real detail from the passage.
