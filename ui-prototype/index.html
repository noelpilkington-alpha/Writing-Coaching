New evidence belongs in a body paragraph, not the conclusion. The conclusion should restate your main points and leave the reader with a final thought — not introduce new facts.

G6 Essay Plan
INTRO Hook about monarch migration → Thesis: People help through citizen science, individual efforts, and habitat restoration.
BP1 Citizen scientists — E1: tracking migration data → X1: why this data helps → E2: reporting sightings → X2: how scientists use the info
BP2 Carter Steadman — E1: 9th birthday donations + way station → X1: direct impact → E2: "Humans may be the problem..." quote → X2: inspires others
BP3 Habitat restoration — E1: planting milkweed and native plants → X1: why monarchs need milkweed → E2: 43% more eggs → X2: shows restoration works
CONC Restate 3 points → broader message about why conservation matters

BP = Body Paragraph • E = Evidence • X = Explanation • TS = Topic Sentence • CS = Closing Sentence

THE FIX

The Body Paragraph Pattern

How to build a strong body paragraph every time

Every body paragraph in a G6+ essay should follow this pattern:

Body Paragraph Formula
TS Topic Sentence — states the paragraph's main point
E1 Evidence 1 — a specific fact, quote, or example from the passage
X1 Explanation 1 — explain why that evidence matters
E2 Evidence 2 — a second fact or example from the passage
X2 Explanation 2 — explain why that evidence matters
CS Concluding Sentence — wraps up the paragraph's main idea

Here's what your P2 (citizen scientists) would look like with this pattern:

Tap to see a full body paragraph
Study the formula above first
EXAMPLE BODY PARAGRAPH
[TS] Citizen scientists play a key role in helping monarchs survive by collecting important data. [E1] According to the passage, thousands of people across North America tag butterflies, count caterpillars, and record monarch sightings online. [X1] This matters because without this data, scientists would not know where monarchs are struggling or where they need the most protection. [E2] The passage also explains that these reports help experts understand how the butterflies are doing and where they need help. [X2] As a result, citizen science turns everyday people into part of the solution, making monarch conservation a shared effort rather than something only researchers can do. [CS] By collecting and sharing data, ordinary citizens are helping ensure that monarchs get the protection they need.
THE KEY RULE
Every E (evidence) must be followed by an X (explanation). Evidence without explanation is just a list of facts. The explanation is where you show the reader why that evidence supports your point. Ask yourself: "So what? Why does this matter?"
