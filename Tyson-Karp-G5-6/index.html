THE "TWO-PART PROMPT" RULE
Before writing a single word, underline every AND and every question word in the prompt. Most Q11 prompts have two parts:

• "Explain X AND Y" — two parts.
• "Describe why X and how Y" — two parts.
• "What does X do, and what does it mean?" — two parts.

Then plan your paragraph with equal space for each part. At minimum: 2 sentences for part 1, 2 sentences for part 2, plus a topic and a closing.
MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (18–20/20 level)

Sea turtles are in danger, but people are working hard to protect them. First, the danger: global warming is warming up the beaches where turtles lay their eggs, and the sand temperature controls whether the babies hatch as male or female. When beaches get too hot, almost all the babies are female, which means there are not enough males to reproduce. Second, how people help: scientists and volunteers protect nests on beaches, relocate eggs to cooler spots, and rescue turtles caught in plastic. They also shade the nests to keep more male turtles hatching. In the end, sea turtles are in trouble, but careful people around the world are giving them a fighting chance.

SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

The 7-Part Two-Sided Paragraph

SHOWS "This shows..." — explain what part 1 means
PART 2 DETAIL Specific details from the passage on the second half of the prompt
SHOWS "This shows..." — explain what part 2 means
CLOSING Wrap both halves back to the central idea
