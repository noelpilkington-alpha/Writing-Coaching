Question 7 Fix — Subject-Verb Agreement

Let's review this together.

You scored 2.0/3 on this question — close, but you lost 1.0 point.

The question asked:

"Why is honey important to the bees? Write one complete sentence."

Which sentence has correct grammar?
A Honey is important to bees because it feed them and helps them make their home.
B Honey is important to bees because it feeds them and helps them make their home.
YOU WROTE
"Honey is important to bees, because it feed them and helps them make their home."

Two issues here:

  • Subject-verb agreement: "it feed" should be "it feeds." When the subject is "it," the verb needs an "s."
  • Unnecessary comma: You do not need a comma before "because" when the reason comes after the main idea.
STRONGER VERSION
"Honey is important to bees because it feeds them during the winter and helps them build their hive."
QUICK CHECK
Before you finish a sentence, find the subject (who or what is doing something) and check: does the verb match? If the subject is he, she, it, or one thing, the verb usually needs an "s."
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Subject-verb agreement is one of the grammar rules that grading feedback checks for. Getting it right means your writing sounds polished and you keep your full points.

Question 3 Fix — Combining Sentences

Let's review this together.

You scored 1.0/2 on this question — you lost 1.0 point.

The question asked:

"Combine Sentence 8 and Sentence 9 using the word and or then."
Sentence 8: The bees bring the nectar back to the hive.
Sentence 9: They turn the nectar into honey.

Which combination is strongest?
A The bees bring the nectar back to the hive, and turn it into honey.
B The bees bring the nectar back to the hive, and then they turn it into honey.
YOU WROTE
"The bees bring the nectar back to the hive, and turn it into honey."

You used "and," which is what the question asked for. But the combination dropped the subject of the second part. A stronger version keeps both ideas complete.

STRONGER VERSION
"The bees bring the nectar back to the hive, and then they turn it into honey."
COMBINING SENTENCES
When you combine two sentences with "and" or "then," make sure both parts still sound like complete ideas. A quick test: could each half stand on its own as a sentence? If not, add back the missing subject.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Combining sentences is a skill you will see on every writing test. When you keep both ideas complete and clear, you show strong control of your writing.

Question 11 — Your Paragraph

"The Mighty Oak Tree" — 17.0/20 points
Which central idea sentence is stronger?
A There are many ways an oak tree is important to other animals.
B Oak trees are important to animals because they provide fresh air, shelter, and food.
Strong: 17.0/20 5 sentences Spelling errors

Max, this is well-organized writing. You used transitions ("One reason," "Another reason," "The final reason") and gave clear details. Let's look at the structure.

TOPIC (could preview your reasons) There are many ways an oak tree is important to other animals.

REASON 1 One reason is that it provides fresh air for animals to breath, so then if there was bad air the tree would get rid of it.

REASON 2 Another reason is it provides a home for some animals, this lets the animal be safe from preditors.

REASON 3 The final reason is that it can provide food for some animals like squirrels, because squirrels eat the acorns of an oak tree.

CLOSING These are just some of the reasons how an oak tree helps other animals.
WHAT YOU DID WELL
Excellent transitions: "One reason," "Another reason," "The final reason" — this makes your paragraph very easy to follow. You also tied each reason back to animals, which matches the prompt perfectly.
TWO THINGS TO IMPROVE
1. Make your topic sentence more specific. Instead of "There are many ways," try previewing your reasons: "Oak trees are important to animals because they provide fresh air, shelter, and food."

2. Fix spelling. breath → breathe and preditors → predators. These small fixes would push your paragraph score even higher.

Question 11 — Stronger Writing

How to push from 17/20 to 20/20
Which paragraph uses stronger evidence?
A Oak trees help animals. They give them air and a home. Animals like oak trees.
B According to the passage, oak trees produce acorns that squirrels and other animals rely on for food. This shows that oak trees are not just big plants — they are a food source that keeps forest animals alive.

Your paragraph already has solid evidence — you mentioned fresh air, shelter, and food with real details. The 3 points you lost come from spelling errors and a topic sentence that could be more specific.

YOUR REASON 2 — MADE EVEN STRONGER WITH THE EVIDENCE SANDWICH

Set it up: "Another reason oak trees are important is that they provide shelter for animals."

Evidence: "The passage explains that birds, squirrels, and other animals build their homes in the branches and trunk of oak trees."

Explain: "This means that without oak trees, many forest animals would have nowhere safe to live."

THE EVIDENCE SANDWICH
1. Set it up: Write a sentence that introduces your evidence.
2. Evidence: Use specific details from the passage (quoted or paraphrased).
3. Explain: Tell the reader why this evidence matters or what it shows.
