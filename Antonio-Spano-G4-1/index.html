Sentence Structure: Q5

"and because" creates an awkward connection

Antonio, on Q5 you used "and because" to connect two ideas. This creates a slightly awkward sentence because "and" and "because" do different jobs.

Q5 (1.5/2) — YOUR ANSWER
You connected two clauses with "and because," which made the sentence structure unclear.

The fix: Choose one connector. If the second idea explains why, use "because" alone. If it adds another equal idea, use "and" alone.

USING "BECAUSE" ALONE
"Students enjoy group projects because they can share ideas and learn from each other."
USING "AND" ALONE
"Students enjoy group projects and they learn better when they work together."
QUICK RULE
If you write "and because," stop and ask: "Am I explaining why or adding another idea?" Pick the one connector that matches. You do not need both.

THE D+E PATTERN (DETAIL + EXPLANATION)
Detail: "A good friend helps you when you are having a bad day."

+ Explanation: "This matters because knowing someone cares about you makes the hard times feel easier to get through."

That second sentence is the "E" in D+E. It takes your detail and explains why it matters or what it shows. Adding one of these after each detail is what separates an 18 from a 20.
