THE RULE: SHOW, THEN SO WHAT
After every reason, one fact from the passage, a thing you could point to in the text. Then one sentence that starts This shows. The check: could a reader find your fact in the passage? If not, it is not a fact yet. Two reasons means two facts and two This shows.
