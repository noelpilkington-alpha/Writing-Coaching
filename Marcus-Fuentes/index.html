TWO RULES FOR FULL CREDIT
1. Always finish your sentence. Even if you're unsure, write your best complete thought. An incomplete answer gets zero; a finished one can earn partial credit.

Build Your Paragraph

Q11 — Good structure, but use your own words

Your paragraph scored 17/20. You had a clear topic sentence and a concluding sentence. But you relied too heavily on copying quotes from the passage instead of putting the ideas in your own words.

YOUR PARAGRAPH
Jason deCaries Taylor, a famous sculpture, put his statues/artwork underwater for many reasons. According to the passage, it says "Jason wants to protect coral reefs." and "Without meaning to, they hurt the coral reefs and sea animals. if people swim around the statues instead the reefs can rest." which gives us the purpose of the statues under water. In conclusion, Jason's purpose to protect the sea is helpful to the earth and the enviorment itself.

Issues:

  • "sculpture" should be "sculptor" (a sculpture is the art; a sculptor is the person)
  • Too much direct quoting — put the ideas in your own words
  • "if" needs a capital I (it starts after a period in the quote)
  • "enviorment" should be "environment"

Here's the G3 paragraph plan — notice how every detail is paraphrased, not quoted:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Jason deCaires Taylor, a famous sculptor, placed his artwork underwater to help protect ocean life.
D1 According to the passage, Taylor wanted to protect coral reefs that were being damaged by swimmers and tourists.
W This shows that his underwater sculptures were not just art — they had the important purpose of keeping reefs safe.
D2 By putting statues in the water, Taylor gave tourists something else to look at so they would stay away from the reefs.
W This shows that Taylor found a creative way to solve a real problem — he used art to redirect people.
D3 Over time, the underwater statues even became new homes for coral and sea creatures.
W This shows that his plan worked even better than expected — the sculptures actually helped the ocean grow back.
CS In conclusion, Taylor's underwater sculptures show that art can do more than look beautiful — it can help protect the environment.
PARAPHRASE, DON'T QUOTE
Instead of copying sentences from the passage with quotation marks, put the idea in your own words. Here's how:

Quote: "Jason wants to protect coral reefs."
Paraphrase: Taylor wanted to protect coral reefs that were being damaged by tourists.

You still use the same information — you just say it your way. This shows the reader you understand the passage, not just that you can copy it.
