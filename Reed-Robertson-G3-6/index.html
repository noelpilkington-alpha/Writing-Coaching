THE 4-STEP RULE FOR REWRITES
1. Start with a question word: Did, Do, Does, Is, Are, Was, Were, Can, Why, How, What.
2. Flip the subject and helper verb. "The dog ran" becomes "Did the dog run?"
3. Keep ALL the original content. Don't drop words or add new ones.
4. End with "?" — always. Every single interrogative ends with a question mark.
Prompt: "Rewrite as an interrogative: The geode felt like a perfect souvenir." Which is correct?
A The geode that felt like a perfect souvenir.
B Did the geode feel like a perfect souvenir?
CORRECT REWRITES FOR YOUR 4 PROMPTS
• "You do not take your dogs to the pond." → "Do you take your dogs to the pond?"
• "This fun place sells out every year." → "Does this fun place sell out every year?"
• "Anna named the seal Sally." → "Did Anna name the seal Sally?"
• "The geode felt like a perfect souvenir." → "Did the geode feel like a perfect souvenir?"
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Question 1 or 2 on most tests is a rewrite prompt worth about 1–2 points. You’ve been losing that point on 4 of 6 tests. The formula is simple. Memorize it, apply it, keep the point forever.

Two-Part Questions — Answer Every Part

When the prompt asks two things, you only answer one

Reed, here's a pattern across your tests: when a question asks "What rule AND why is it important?" — you often write only the rule. The "why" half goes missing.

FROM G3.4 Q10 (Mar 2026)
Prompt: "What is an important rule to follow AND why is it important?"
You wrote: "Always follow the rules when your in the nature."
(You gave a rule. You never said WHY it matters. Half credit.)
FROM G3.5 Q9 (Apr 2026)
Prompt: "Do you enjoy reading and why?"
You wrote: "Yes I do enjoy reading."
(Yes, but no reason. Half credit again.)
THE TWO-PART RULE
When a prompt uses the word "and," it is asking for TWO things. Both must appear in your answer.

✗ "Always follow the rules when your in the nature."
✓ "Always follow the rules in nature because some animals can be dangerous and some plants can hurt you."

✗ "Yes I do enjoy reading."
✓ "Yes, I enjoy reading because stories can take me to places I've never been."
Prompt: "What is your favorite animal AND why?" Which answer earns FULL credit?
A My favorite animal is a dog.
B My favorite animal is a dog because dogs are loyal and fun to play with.
YOUR 2-STEP CHECK
1. Before writing, underline the word "and" or count how many question marks are in the prompt.
2. Before submitting, re-read your answer and ask: "Does this have ALL the parts the prompt asked for?"
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
You have had at least 3 half-credit answers across your recent tests just because you forgot the second half. That's 1.5–3 easy points per test. Add a “because” or “so that” clause to every Q6–Q10 answer and you lock those points in.

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20/20)

Porcupines are interesting animals because they have clever ways to protect themselves. For example, their sharp quills can puncture a predator if it ignores the porcupine's warning. In addition, their tail helps alert other animals to stay away before anyone gets hurt. Because of these natural defenses, porcupines can live safely in the forest without needing to be fast or strong.

The Passage-Fact Formula
TOPIC Your claim about the passage
FACT 1 For example, + a SPECIFIC detail from the passage
FACT 2 In addition, + a SECOND specific detail
CLOSE Tie back to the claim, no new facts
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
