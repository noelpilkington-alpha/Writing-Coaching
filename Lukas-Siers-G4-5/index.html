THE RULE: FOR EXAMPLE, THEN THIS SHOWS
After each point, For example is followed by a fact you can point to in the article. Then one sentence that starts This shows and says what the fact proves about the question: here, how sharing stories keeps a culture alive. The check: if your example starts with if or when you, it is a situation you imagined. Imagined situations earn no evidence points. Only the article does.
