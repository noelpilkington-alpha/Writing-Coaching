Q3: What's an Appositive?

A short description tucked inside the sentence

The question asked you to rewrite the sentence and add an appositive:

Original
Sally swam to the other seals.
YOU WROTE
Sally curiously swam to the other seals.

You added an adverb ("curiously"), which is a good instinct — but that's not an appositive. An appositive is a short description placed right after a noun, set off by commas. It tells the reader who or what something is.

Tap to see the improved version
Read the explanation above first
WITH AN APPOSITIVE
Sally, the baby seal, swam to the other seals.
THE APPOSITIVE PATTERN
Name, [short description], rest of sentence.

Think of it like a whisper to the reader: "Sally — oh, by the way, she's the baby seal — swam to the other seals."

More examples:
• Anna, the oldest child, spotted the seal first.
• The fishnet, a tangled mess, was wrapped around the pup.

2. Use the TS → D → W → CS plan.

Paragraph Plan — "How Tajiri's childhood influenced Pokémon"
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Satoshi Tajiri's love of catching bugs as a kid led him to create Pokémon.
D1 As a child, Tajiri spent hours catching bugs — friends called him "Dr. Bug."
W This shows how much he loved collecting creatures, which became the main idea behind Pokémon.
D2 When he grew up, he remembered his bug-catching joy and used it as the idea for a game.
W This matters because without that childhood memory, the game might never have been created.
D3 Pokémon became huge — 150+ characters, TV shows, movies, apps.
W This shows that one kid's hobby turned into something millions of people enjoy.
CS Tajiri's childhood hobby turned into one of the most popular games in the world.

You already had all these details in your paragraph — you just need to organize them and add the "why it matters" sentences after each detail.

THE TOPIC SENTENCE TRICK
Read the prompt, then turn it into your first sentence:

Prompt: "Explain how Satoshi Tajiri's childhood influenced the creation of Pokémon."
Topic sentence: "Satoshi Tajiri's childhood love of catching bugs influenced him to create Pokémon."

See? You just flip the prompt into a statement. Then your details and explanations come after.
