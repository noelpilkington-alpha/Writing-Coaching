Fix 2: Cover the Comma

A comma cannot hold two sentences together
THE RULE: COVER THE COMMA
Cover the comma with your finger. Read the words on the left. Read the words on the right. The check: if both sides could stand alone as sentences, a comma is not enough. Use a period and a capital letter, or add a joining word like so or because. If one side cannot stand alone, the comma is fine.

Your second sentence, with the comma covered:

YOU WROTE
“At school, children can read books too, camels are strong so people trust the camels.”
Left of the covered comma: At school, children can read books too. Stands alone. Right: Camels are strong so people trust the camels. Stands alone. Two sentences, one comma.
WITH A PERIOD
At school, children can read books too. Camels are strong, so people trust them.
