POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20 OF 20)

Shared gardens are helpful to towns in many important ways. One reason is that a garden gives people a new spot to grow food, especially when some homes do not have room for plants. This matters because fresh food is easier to get and families can save money on groceries. A garden is also a great place to learn — children can learn about plants and how to use tools like knives safely. This shows that shared gardens teach kids real skills they would not pick up anywhere else in town. Another way gardens help is that people can meet up with their friends and family there. This is important because it brings neighbors together and makes the town feel more like a community. Gardens are a small act, but they really do make a big difference.
