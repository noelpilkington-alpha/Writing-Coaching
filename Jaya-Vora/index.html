The Body Paragraph Pattern

How to build a strong body paragraph every time

Every body paragraph in a G6+ essay should follow this pattern:

Body Paragraph Formula
TS Topic Sentence — states the paragraph's main point
E1 Evidence 1 — a specific fact, quote, or example from the passage
X1 Explanation 1 — explain why that evidence matters
E2 Evidence 2 — a second fact or example from the passage
X2 Explanation 2 — explain why that evidence matters
CS Concluding Sentence — wraps up the paragraph's main idea

THE KEY RULE
Every E (evidence) must be followed by an X (explanation). Evidence without explanation is just a list of facts. The explanation is where you show the reader why that evidence supports your point. Ask yourself: "So what? Why does this matter?"

Q3: Conjunctions — "Because" vs. "But"

Know when ideas contrast vs. when they explain each other

The question asked you to combine these two ideas with a logical conjunction:

The Two Ideas
Idea 1: Animated movies are fun.
Idea 2: They also include valuable lessons.

You chose: "Animated movies are fun because they include valuable lessons."

The correct answer was: "Animated movies are fun but they include valuable lessons."

Why "but"? These two ideas show a contrast — being "fun" and including "valuable lessons" are different (and somewhat surprising) qualities. "But" signals that the second idea adds something unexpected.

Why not "because"? "Because" means the second idea causes the first. Are animated movies fun because they have lessons? Not exactly — they're fun for many reasons (animation, humor, music). The lessons are a bonus, not the reason they're fun.

Conjunction When to use it
but When the second idea contrasts with or adds something unexpected to the first
because When the second idea explains the reason for the first
THE TEST
Ask: "Does the second idea explain why the first is true?" If yes, use because. If the second idea adds something different or surprising, use but. Example: "I was tired but I kept running" (contrast). "I was tired because I ran all day" (reason).
