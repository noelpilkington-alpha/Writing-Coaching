THE RULE: ANSWER, THEN BECAUSE
For Q6 to Q10, one point is for a clear answer in a sentence, one point is for the reason, explanation or example inside it, and one is for spelling and punctuation. The check: find the word because (or so, or for example) in your sentence. If it is not there, the sentence is not finished yet, even when it is a good sentence.

Fix 2: An Appositive Renames with a Noun

Q4 described the books. On G3.3 there was no appositive at all
THE RULE: A NOUN BETWEEN THE COMMAS
An appositive is a noun phrase that renames the noun right before it, set off by commas: The Poky Little Puppy, a best-selling story, became… The check: cover everything outside the commas and read what is left. Does it start with a noun that could stand in for the thing it follows? a series, a story, a pup. If it starts with a verb form like known, sold, covered, it describes but does not rename. Close, and one point.

You know the shape. In Q5 you picked the appositive out of a sentence for full marks:

YOURS, Q5, 2 OUT OF 2
The Poky Little Puppy, APPOSITIVEa best-selling story, became the most popular picture book of all time.
Between the commas: a best-selling story. Starts with a noun. Renames the Puppy.

Q4 asked you to build one, and the phrase you built describes instead:

YOU WROTE (Q4, 1 OUT OF 2)

Cover the outside: known for their golden spines. It starts with known, a verb form. Good commas, right place, no noun.

Two words added. A series renames the books, and the rest of your phrase stays.
