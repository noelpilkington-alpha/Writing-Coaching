Fix 3: An Appositive Renames with a Noun

Q4: you described the books. The question asked you to rename them
THE RULE: A NOUN BETWEEN THE COMMAS
An appositive is a noun phrase that renames the noun right before it, set off by commas: The Poky Little Puppy, a best-selling story, became… The check: cover everything outside the commas and read what is left. Does it start with a noun that could stand in for the thing it follows? a series, a story, an artist. If it starts with a verb form like known, made, sold, or a preposition like with, it describes but does not rename. Close, and one point.

You already know the shape. In Q5 you picked the appositive out of a sentence and got full marks:

YOURS, Q5, 2 OUT OF 2
The Poky Little Puppy, APPOSITIVEa best-selling story, became the most popular picture book of all time.
Between the commas: a best-selling story. Starts with a noun. Renames the Puppy.
