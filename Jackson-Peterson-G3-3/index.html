If the Answer Is a Question, It Ends With ?

One missing punctuation mark cost you half a point on G3.3 Q1

Jackson, on G3.3 Q1 the prompt was "Change this sentence into a question." You wrote the right sentence — but you ended it with a period instead of a question mark. That single slip cost you half a point.

FROM G3.3 Q1 (Change to a question — scored 0.5)
Prompt: Change this sentence into a question: Anna named the seal Sally.
Your answer: "Did Anna name the seal Sally."
Words are right. Word order is right. Just missing the ? at the end.
THE FIX (full credit — 2/2)
"Did Anna name the seal Sally?"

This is a one-character fix and it is worth a full point. The grader checks for ? at the end because a question is defined by its punctuation. Without the "?", it doesn't count as a question — even if the words form a question.

Which answer earns full credit for "Change this to a question"?
A Did the dogs go to the park.
B Did the dogs go to the park?
THE RULE
End mark matches sentence type.

• Statement → period .
• Question → question mark ?
• Strong feeling → exclamation !

If the prompt asks you to write a question, the LAST KEYSTROKE of your answer should be ?. Check it before you submit.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Q1 is a 2-point question. Missing the "?" is 1 full point lost on a single keystroke. You had the answer right — you just needed to end it correctly. On your proofread pass, check every rewrite's last character.
