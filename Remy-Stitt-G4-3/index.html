Fix 1: Name Both Parts of the Question

Q11 asked about ideas and character. Your facts cover both. Your first and last sentences name neither
THE RULE: THE POINT, THEN THE HOWS, STRAIGHT FROM THE QUESTION
When a question has two parts, ideas and character, those two parts are your hows. The first sentence answers the question (POINT) and names both (HOW). Each how gets a DETAIL · SHOW, a fact from the article with its number or its words, and a DETAIL · SO WHAT, This shows plus what the fact proves about that how. The CONCLUDING SENTENCE names both hows and says what they add up to. The check: underline the parts of the question. Find each one in your first sentence and in your last.

Fix 2: A Since-Part Needs a Main Part

Your September card was about sentences that never end. This one never got started
THE RULE: SINCE, BECAUSE, WHEN, IF START A PART THAT CANNOT STAND ALONE
A part that starts with since, because, when or if leans on a main part. If the leaning part comes first, a comma, then the main part: Since the holes held the water, the seeds could grow. The check: if a sentence starts with one of these words, find the comma, then find a main part after it with its own doer and verb. No main part, no sentence.
YOU WROTE (Q11, THE FOURTH SENTENCE)
“Since Yacouba stuck with this method of filling holes with manure so the water would be maintained around the seed.” A SINCE-PART, A SO-PART, NO MAIN PART
Two leaning parts and nothing to lean on. The period arrives before the sentence does. In September you were joining two whole sentences with nothing between them. This is the mirror: a sentence that is only the joining part.
WITH A MAIN PART
Since Yacouba stuck with this method, MAIN PARTthe water stayed around the seed and the seedlings lived.
YOU WROTE (G4.1 Q5, 1 OUT OF 2)
“His dedication has transformed the school, because of his efforts the enrollment has doubled, and more students are now attending college.” TWO WHOLE SENTENCES ON ONE COMMA
The September habit, two weeks ago. Because of is not the joining word because; cover the comma and both sides stand alone. His dedication has transformed the school, so enrollment has doubled does the job.

ONLY THE LEANING PARTSince the tadpoles grew legs after a week or two.

PRACTICE IT IN YOUR ALPHAWRITE LESSONS
Every sentence that starts with since, because, when or if: find the comma, then the main part. Every comma between two whole sentences: add so, but or and, or make two sentences.

THE RULE: NAME THE ONE THING, THEN SAY WHY
For Q6 to Q10, one point is for a clear answer in a sentence, one point is for the reason, explanation or example inside it, and one is for spelling and punctuation. When the question says one way or how a person might feel, the answer is one specific way, one named feeling, then the because. The check: could your sentence be true of almost anything? Then it has not named the thing yet.
YOU WROTE (Q8, 2 OUT OF 3)

The question asked for one way a plant or animal changes. The story has one in front of you: tadpoles grow legs, their tails shrink, and three frogs sit on a rock.
ONE SPECIFIC WAY
A tadpole changes as it grows by sprouting a pair of tiny legs in front of its tail, BECAUSEbecause the tail it swam with shrinks away as the legs it will hop with grow.
