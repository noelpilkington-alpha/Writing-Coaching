Step 5: Connector Logic — Q5

You wrote "That" when the sentence needed "Since"
🔍
What happened on Q5: The question asked you to combine two sentences showing a reason. You used "That" as the connector. The correct choice was "Since" because it shows a reason/cause relationship.
Q5 — YOUR ANSWER (0 out of 2 weighted points)
You used "That" to connect the sentences, but "That" does not show a reason.

The connector logic:

  • "Since" / "Because" = shows a REASON (why something happened)
  • "That" = introduces a fact or description (not a reason)
  • "But" / "Although" = shows a CONTRAST
  • "So" = shows a RESULT
THE FIX
"Since Sally the seal was hungry, she swam to find fish." → "Since" shows the reason (hunger) for the action (swimming).

"That" does not work here because it does not tell us why something happened.
Which connector shows a reason?
AThat
BSince
THE ONE-QUESTION TEST
Before writing your connector, ask: "Am I showing WHY something happened?"

If yes → use "since" or "because"
If showing contrast → use "but" or "although"
If showing result → use "so"

This one-second check catches the Q5 error.
