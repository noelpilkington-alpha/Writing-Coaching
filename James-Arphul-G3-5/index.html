Your #2 Fix: Watch for Stray Commas

Q2 — 1 out of 2 — extra comma in unscramble

On Q2, you were asked to unscramble words into a correct sentence. You got the word order right, but you added an extra comma that should not be there.

Q2 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2)
"The little Puppy, ran across the yard."

The problem: There should be no comma between the subject ("The little Puppy") and the verb ("ran"). That comma breaks the sentence incorrectly.

THE CORRECT VERSION
"The little Puppy ran across the yard."
THE RULE
Never put a comma between the subject and its verb unless there is an appositive or extra phrase in between. If the sentence reads "[Who] [did what]," there is no comma between them.

Your proofread check: After unscrambling, read the sentence out loud. If you pause unnaturally at the comma, it probably should not be there.
Which sentence has correct comma usage?
A "The orange cat, jumped onto the table."
B "The orange cat jumped onto the table."
QUICK CHECK
When you proofread, look at every comma and ask: "Is this comma needed here, or did I just put it in by habit?" If the sentence works fine without it, remove it.
