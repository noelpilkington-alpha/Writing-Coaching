Fix 1: Not the Topic Sentence Again

Eleven of fifteen wrong first tries, and seven second tries, said the topic sentence in new words
THE RULE: THE ENDING SAYS WHAT THE DETAILS ADD UP TO
The topic sentence is already on the page. The ending’s job is to name what the details are together and say what they add up to, in one short sentence the topic sentence did not already say. The lesson’s own examples show the shape: These tools help workers fix cars safely. This equipment keeps players safe on the field. Simple care keeps a car running well. A group word for the details, then what that group does. Some examples just say why the topic matters in fresh words. The sum is the surer way to pass. The check: put your sentence beside the topic sentence you were given. Same idea, same words in a new order? Start over. If yours says what the details add up to, it is the ending, even if it could have opened the paragraph.
