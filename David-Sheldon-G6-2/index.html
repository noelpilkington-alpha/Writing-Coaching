ERROR 1 — FROM G6.2 Q3 (logical conjunctions)
Question: Which sentence combines the ideas using a logical conjunction?
“Animated movies are fun. They also include valuable lessons.”
Your pick: a combined sentence, but the conjunction didn't match the meaning.
THE RULE — MATCH THE CONJUNCTION TO THE MEANING
When you combine two sentences, the joining word has to match the relationship between them:

and = adds a second idea of the same type (fun AND lessons)
but / however = shows a contrast (fun BUT also serious)
because / so = cause and effect (fun BECAUSE of the colors)
or = gives a choice (fun OR boring)

For the G6.2 Q3 stimulus, both ideas are positive — movies are fun AND they also have lessons. That is an additive relationship, so the right conjunction is and, not but or because.
From G6.2 Q3 — which combination uses a LOGICAL conjunction?
A Animated movies are fun, but they also include valuable lessons.
B Animated movies are fun, and they also include valuable lessons.
ERROR 2 — FROM G6.2 Q11 (essay development)
On Q11 you earned 15/20 (up from 11/20 on G6.1 — huge growth). The largest remaining gap is in the rubric line “use evidence AND explain it” (worth 5 points). Evidence was there; explanations were thin.
THE RULE — EVIDENCE NEEDS AN EXPLANATION
A G6 body paragraph is supposed to go: topic sentence → evidence → explanation → evidence → explanation → closing.

Three phrases that start a clean explanation sentence:
• “This shows...” — what the evidence demonstrates
• “This proves...” — what the evidence establishes as true
• “This means...” — what the evidence implies or helps the reader understand

An explanation sentence is usually one or two lines long. It connects the evidence back to your thesis.
Which body paragraph structure earns a higher evidence-and-explanation score?
A Topic sentence + evidence + evidence + done.
B Topic sentence + evidence + “This shows...” + evidence + “This proves...” + closing.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
These two errors cost you 6 points on G6.2. The conjunction rule is a 1-minute fix. The evidence-explanation pattern is a 5-minute habit that — once it is automatic — earns 2 extra points on every essay for the rest of your writing tests. That is the path to 90+.

Second, animated movies help children understand how to work as a team.
