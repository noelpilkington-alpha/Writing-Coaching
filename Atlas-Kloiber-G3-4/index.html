Error #1: Using the Right Conjunction

This is a Step 2 skill: the question is testing conjunctions

Atlas, Q2 asked you to combine two sentences using a conjunction that shows cause and effect. You used "so," but the question needed "since" (or "because").

Q2 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"Layla loves exploring so she found a geode on the trail."

The problem: "So" shows a result ("she loves exploring, so she found..."), but the question asked for a reason word. The sentence should show that finding the geode happened because she loves exploring.

CORRECT CONJUNCTION
"Layla found a geode on the trail since she loves exploring and always looks carefully at the ground."
STEP 2: WHICH CONJUNCTION?
• Because / Since = reason (WHY something happened)
• So = result (WHAT happened because of something)
• But / Although = contrast (two ideas that seem opposite)

Read the question and ask: "Does it want the reason or the result?" Then pick the right conjunction.
The question asks you to show WHY Layla noticed the geode. Which conjunction is correct?
A"Layla is curious so she noticed the geode on the trail."
B"Layla noticed the geode since she is curious and always pays attention to her surroundings."
