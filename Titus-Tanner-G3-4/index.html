Detail + Explanation for Paragraphs

The pattern that turns 13/20 into 18+/20

Titus, the difference between a 13/20 paragraph and an 18+/20 paragraph is not about having different ideas. It is about developing each idea with a specific detail from the passage and then explaining what that detail shows.

The Detail + Explanation Pattern
DETAIL Name something specific from the passage
EXPLAIN Say what that detail shows or why it matters

Here is the difference for the "From Sports and Society" passage:

UNDERDEVELOPED (13/20 level)
"Sports are important because they bring people together and make them happy."
FULLY DEVELOPED (18+/20 level)
"Sports bring people together because fans cheer for the same team and share exciting moments. For example, the passage describes how entire communities gather to watch local games, even if they do not normally spend time together. This shows that sports can create connections between people who would otherwise be strangers."
WHAT CHANGED
The developed version has the same idea ("sports bring people together"). But it adds:
• A specific detail (communities gather to watch local games)
• An explanation (This shows sports create connections between strangers)

That is where the extra 5 to 7 points live.

Building a Full Paragraph

The structure that earns 18+/20

Titus, here is the formula for a paragraph that earns 18 to 20 points:

Paragraph Blueprint
Sentence 1 Topic sentence: Answer the question directly
Sentence 2 Detail: Name a specific thing from the passage
Sentence 3 Explanation: "This shows..." what that detail means
Sentence 4 Second detail: Another example from the passage
Sentence 5 Explanation: "This shows..." what the second detail means
Sentence 6 Closing: Wrap up the main idea
Which paragraph follows the Detail + Explanation pattern?
A "Sports are important to society. They are cool and fun. Everyone likes sports because they are exciting. Sports will always be popular."
B "Sports help young people build friendships. For example, the passage describes how Layla joined a soccer team and met friends she still talks to years later. This shows that being on a team gives people a chance to bond over shared goals and experiences."
THE MAGIC PHRASE
After every detail you mention, write: "This shows..."

That phrase forces you to explain the detail. It is the single most important habit for moving your paragraph from 13/20 to 18+/20.

FULL PARAGRAPH (18–20/20 level)
"Sports play an important role in society because they bring communities together and teach people valuable lessons. For example, the passage describes how Layla's neighborhood comes together every weekend to watch and play local games. This shows that sports give people a reason to gather and support each other, which builds a stronger community. COUNT YOUR D+E CYCLES
A good paragraph needs at least two Detail + Explanation cycles:

• Detail 1 + "This shows..." = one cycle
• Detail 2 + "This shows..." = two cycles

If you only have one cycle, your paragraph will be too short (13 to 15 points). Two cycles gets you to 18+.

B At least two cycles (two details, each followed by "This shows...")
