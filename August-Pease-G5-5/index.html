2. The thing (what object, action, or detail)

Tiny Fixes, Big Points

Four small habits that separate 89% from 90%+

August, you are at the level where the small stuff is what holds you back. Here are four polish habits that have shown up as missed points across your tests:

HABIT 1 — APOSTROPHES & CONTRACTIONS — FROM G5.3 Q9
"Jaylen might of felt really sad whenever he heard that the trip was canceled."
Should be: might have. "Might of" is never correct in writing — it sounds like "might've" but it is always "might have."
HABIT 2 — HOMOPHONE SWAPS — FROM G5.2 Q4
"On are last sunday, we took are time..."
Should be: our. "Are" is the verb ("they are ready"). "Our" shows possession ("our time").
HABIT 3 — CAPITALIZATION — FROM G5.2 Q4
"On our last sunday..."
Should be: Sunday. Days of the week are proper nouns — always capitalized.
HABIT 4 — "THE" VS "A" IN APPOSITIVES — FROM G5.3 Q1
"Every summer, the Thompsons camped at the Willow State Park, a campsite..."
Should be: Willow State Park (no "the"). Named places don't take "the" in front when the name is already specific.
Which version earns full credit on a polish-tier test?
A Jaylen might of felt sad when the trip was canceled, and are family camped on sunday.
B Jaylen might have felt sad when the trip was canceled, and our family camped on Sunday.
YOUR POLISH CHECKLIST (use this during proofreading)
1. Any "of" after could/would/should/might? Check if it should be have.
2. Any "are"? Check if it should be our.
3. Any day of the week, month, or named place? Capital letter.
4. Any "the [place name]"? Often just the place name is enough.
5. Any word over 6 letters? Double-check the spelling.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
These four habits show up on every one of your tests. One slip per test is worth about 1 point. Four slips per test is worth 4–5 points. Add those back and you are at 90%+.
