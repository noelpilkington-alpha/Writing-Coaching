THE DEVELOPMENT RULE FOR 3-POINT QUESTIONS
Any question worth 3 points needs at least 2 sentences:

• Sentence 1: State your answer with a specific detail from the passage
• Sentence 2: Explain WHY it matters or what it shows

One sentence = partial credit. Two sentences with detail + explanation = full credit.
