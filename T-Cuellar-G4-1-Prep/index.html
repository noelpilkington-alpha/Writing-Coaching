Your Game Plan

Four moves, every question
THE GAME PLAN
1. Read the question twice and find the job word: combine, expand, appositive, complete, explain, why. The job word tells you what the points are for. 2. Do the job, then add the reason if the question asks for your opinion or your idea. 3. Read the answer back once before you move on: capitals, commas, a period at the end. 4. Never leave a box empty. A blank is 0. A try is usually 1 or 2.

Lock In: Every Answer Carries Its Because

Q6 to Q10. Two of the three points are the reason
THE RULE: ANSWER, THEN BECAUSE
For Q6 to Q10, one point is for a correct sentence and two points are for the reason, explanation or example inside it. The check: find the word because (or so, or for example) in your sentence. If it is not there, the sentence is not finished yet.

Your Paragraph Recipe for G4.1

Your Pokémon paragraph scored 18. Here is the 20

The G3.3 question: Explain how Satoshi Tajiri’s childhood influenced the creation of Pokémon. Here is what you wrote, labelled:

HOOK“Found it!” If you play Pokémon or catch bugs you will often hear this phrase. TOPIC SENTENCEWhen a man named Satoshi Tajiri was a young boy, he liked to catch bugs. Catching bugs influnced a game he made called Pokémon. DETAIL 1 · SHOWSometimes he was out collecting bugs for hours at a time. DETAIL 2 · SHOWWhen he was older, he played video games; but, his opinion was that they were not very good. That made him want to make a more interesting game. CONCLUDING SENTENCESatoshi hoped that other people would love his game that was based off his childhood hobby.

A hook, a topic sentence that answers the question, two real facts from the article in order, and a concluding sentence that comes back to the childhood hobby. That is an 18, and it is the recipe. The two points left: after each fact, a sentence that says what the fact proves, and a read-back for influenced and the punctuation before but.

THE RECIPE, FOR ANY TEXT
TOPIC SENTENCE answers the question in the question’s own words. Then two or three supporting details, each in two halves: SHOW a fact you can point to in the text, then SO WHAT This shows… what the fact proves about the question. Then a CONCLUDING SENTENCE that says the answer again in new words. The check: count the facts, count the This shows sentences. Same number.
After your fact “Sometimes he was out collecting bugs for hours at a time,” which sentence is the SO WHAT?
AHe also played video games when he was older.
BThis shows that the patience Pokémon asks of its players came straight from his own childhood.
After the fact “He thought the video games he played were not very good,” which sentence is the SO WHAT?
AThis shows that Pokémon began as the game he wished he could have played.
BPokémon became one of the most popular games in the world.
