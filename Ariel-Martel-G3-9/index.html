Error: Appositive Completeness

This is a Step 2 skill: the question is testing appositives

Ariel, Q1 asked you to write a sentence with an appositive about the queen bee. Your answer was close but the appositive phrase was incomplete.

Q1 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"A colony has one leader, queen bee."

The problem: "Queen bee" is meant to be an appositive renaming "one leader," but it is missing an article. It should read "the queen bee" to be a complete noun phrase.

CORRECT APPOSITIVE
"A colony has one leader, the queen bee, who makes all the important decisions."

The appositive "the queen bee" is now a complete noun phrase that renames "one leader," set off by commas.

THE FIX: COMPLETE THE NOUN PHRASE
An appositive must be a complete noun phrase. That means it needs its article ("the," "a," "an") if the noun usually takes one:

• Incomplete: "one leader, queen bee" (missing "the")
• Complete: "one leader, the queen bee"

After writing an appositive, read it alone: "queen bee." Would you say that by itself? Or would you say "the queen bee"? If it needs "the" or "a," add it.
Which sentence has a complete appositive?
A"The oak, tallest tree, provides shade for the whole yard."
B"The oak, the tallest tree in the yard, provides shade for everyone."

Error: Spelling in Your Paragraph

This is a Step 5 skill: proofread before submitting

Ariel, your paragraph had three spelling errors:

SPELLING ERRORS FROM Q11
"Oak trees are inportant because..." → important
"Squirls use the acorns for food." → Squirrels
"Animals would froget where they buried them." → forget

All three are words you know. They are "speed errors" where letters get dropped or swapped because you are writing quickly.

THE FIX: READ BACK SLOWLY
After writing your paragraph, read it back one sentence at a time. Say each word in your head:

• "in-por-tant" sounds wrong → "im-por-tant" sounds right
• "Squirls" looks short → "Squir-rels" has two syllables after "Squir"
• "fro-get" sounds wrong → "for-get" sounds right

This takes less than a minute and catches all three errors.
Which sentence has correct spelling?
A"Oak trees are important because squirrels use the acorns and sometimes forget where they buried them."
B"Oak trees are inportant because squirrels use the acorns and sometimes forget where they buried them."
