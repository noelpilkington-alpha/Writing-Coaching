THE KEY DIFFERENCE
A summary retells facts in order: "This happened, then this happened." An expository essay organizes facts around main points and explains what they show: "El Silbo's origins show how deeply it connects to the island's history because..."
See the difference in action
Summary style: "Historians believe El Silbo started with early inhabitants. It helped people communicate."

Essay style: "El Silbo's origins show how deeply it connects the island's people to their past. Historians believe the language may have started with La Gomera's earliest inhabitants, meaning it has been part of island life for centuries. This long history shows that El Silbo is not just a way to communicate — it is a tradition passed down through generations."

The Body Paragraph Pattern

How to build a strong body paragraph every time

Every body paragraph in a G7+ essay should follow this pattern:

Body Paragraph Formula
TS Topic Sentence — states the paragraph's main point
E1 Evidence 1 — a specific fact, quote, or example from the passage
X1 Explanation 1 — explain why that evidence matters
E2 Evidence 2 — a second fact or example from the passage
X2 Explanation 2 — explain why that evidence matters
CS Concluding Sentence — wraps up the paragraph's main idea

Here's what your P2 (origins of El Silbo) would look like with this pattern:

EXAMPLE BODY PARAGRAPH
[TS] El Silbo's ancient origins show how deeply it connects the people of La Gomera to their past. [E1] According to the passage, historians believe the whistled language may have started with the island's earliest inhabitants, centuries before modern communication existed. [X1] This means El Silbo is not a recent invention — it is a tradition that has been passed down through countless generations, tying today's islanders to their ancestors. [E2] The passage also explains that for centuries, the whistled language helped people communicate across the island's deep ravines and valleys, making daily life much easier. [X2] This shows that El Silbo was not just a novelty — it was essential to survival on a rugged island where walking between villages could take hours. [CS] By preserving El Silbo, the people of La Gomera are keeping alive a language that has defined their island for generations.
THE KEY RULE
Every E (evidence) must be followed by an X (explanation). Evidence without explanation is just a list of facts — which turns your essay into a summary. The explanation is where you show the reader why that evidence supports your point. Ask yourself: "So what? Why does this matter?"
