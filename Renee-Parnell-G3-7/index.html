Fix #1: Appositive, Not "Because"

Q4 — 1 out of 2 weighted points

Renee, Q4 asked for an appositive. You used "because" instead. An appositive is a noun phrase (no verb, no "because") that renames the noun next to it.

Q4 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"Pumpkins are grown in fall because farmers plant them in summer."
CORRECT APPOSITIVE
"Pumpkins, a popular fall crop grown on farms across the country, are harvested in October."
THE APPOSITIVE RULE
An appositive:
• Is a noun phrase (no verb)
• Does not use "because"
• Goes between commas right after the noun
• Answers "What is it?"
Which uses an appositive?
AThe pumpkin, a warm orange vegetable used in pies and soups, is a symbol of autumn.
BThe pumpkin is popular because people use it for Halloween decorations.
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q4 is worth 2 weighted points. You earned 1. Learning this one structure gives you the other point back.

Fix #2: Explain HOW, Not Just THAT

Q6 and Q7 — circular reasoning and missing mechanism

Renee, on Q6 your answer used circular reasoning (restating the question as the answer), and on Q7 you said bees help flowers but did not explain how they do it.

Q6 — (2 out of 3 weighted points) — CIRCULAR
"Warm soil is important for pumpkins because pumpkins need warm soil to grow."

The problem: This just restates the question. "Why is warm soil important?" "Because pumpkins need warm soil." That is a circle, not an explanation.

EXPLAINS WHY
"Warm soil is important for pumpkins because the seeds will not sprout if the ground is too cold. The passage says pumpkins need soil above 60 degrees to germinate. This shows that warmth is what activates the seed to start growing."
Q7 — (2 out of 3 weighted points) — MISSING HOW
"Bees help pumpkin plants because they are important for flowers."

The problem: This says bees help but never explains how. What do bees actually do?

EXPLAINS HOW
"Bees help pumpkin plants by carrying pollen from one flower to another. This matters because without pollen being moved between flowers, the plant cannot produce fruit."
The question asks: "How do bees help pumpkin plants grow?" Which answer explains HOW?
ABees are important for pumpkin plants because they help them grow better.
BBees help pumpkin plants by carrying pollen from one flower to another, which allows the plant to produce pumpkins.
THE "HOW" CHECK
After writing your answer, ask: "Did I explain HOW or WHY this happens? Or did I just say THAT it happens?" If you only said THAT, add one more sentence explaining the mechanism.

Fix #3: Avoid Run-on Lists

Q9 — 2 out of 3 weighted points

Renee, on Q9 your answer listed several ideas in a run-on sentence without separating them properly or developing any single one.

THE FIX: PICK ONE AND DEVELOP IT
Instead of listing four ideas in one long sentence, pick one idea, state it clearly, add a passage detail, and explain what it shows.

Listing (run-on): "You can cook soup and make pie and roast seeds and bake bread."

Developed (full credit): "One way to use a pumpkin is to roast the seeds. The passage says that roasted pumpkin seeds are a healthy snack full of nutrients. This shows that pumpkins are useful for more than just decoration."
Which answer earns more points?
AYou can make pumpkin pie and pumpkin soup and pumpkin bread and roast the seeds too.
BOne way to use a pumpkin is to roast the seeds for a snack. The passage says roasted pumpkin seeds are full of healthy nutrients. This shows that pumpkins are useful for more than just decoration.
THE RULE
One developed point with Detail + Explanation earns more than a list of four undeveloped points. Pick your best idea, support it, and explain it.
