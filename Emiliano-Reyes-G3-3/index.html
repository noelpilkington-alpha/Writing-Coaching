Every Sentence Needs a Complete Verb

A small fix that makes your writing fully correct

Emiliano, on Question 7 your idea was great — but one sentence was missing part of its verb. Here is what you wrote:

YOU WROTE (G3.3 Q7)
"The most meaningful moment was when the three seals bobbing in the water as if to say thank you."

"The three seals bobbing" is missing a helping word. A complete verb here is "were bobbing." With that small change, the sentence is complete and correct.

Which sentence has a complete verb?
A The three seals bobbing in the water.
B The three seals were bobbing in the water.
THE COMPLETE-VERB CHECK
Read each sentence back and ask: "What is the action, and does it have all its words?" Words like is, are, was, were often need to come before words ending in -ing.

✗ "The seals bobbing in the water." (missing a verb word)
✓ "The seals were bobbing in the water." (complete verb)
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Your ideas on Questions 7, 8, and 9 were all correct — you just lost a little credit on the writing. Rereading each sentence for a complete verb turns those near-misses into full points.
