Fix 2: The Six-Sentence Paragraph

The 20-point box, built from the passage you read
THE RULE: THE SIX-SENTENCE RECIPE
TOPIC SENTENCE answers the question in the question’s own words. Then two supporting details, each in two halves: SHOW a fact you can point to in the passage, then SO WHAT This shows… what the fact proves. Then a CONCLUDING SENTENCE that says the answer again in new words. The check: six sentences, two of them starting This shows.
