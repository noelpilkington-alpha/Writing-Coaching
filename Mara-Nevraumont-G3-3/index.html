Your G3.3 Paragraph

13 out of 20 — the story is right; now make it answer the question

The paragraph is worth 20 points — almost half the test. You earned 13. Here is what you wrote:

“Pokemon was made by somone named Tajiri. When Tajiri was little he loved bugs. When he got older he liked video game. But Tajiri wanted something better so he thoght about what he liked and he came up with Pokemon. A game were you catch little creatures and throw balls.”
The whole story is there (bugs → games → Pokemon) Events in the right order Opens with a fact, not an answer Never connects the bugs to the game Ends on a fragment

What is working: you found the right facts and put them in the right order — he loved bugs, then video games, then combined what he loved. That is the hard part and you did it.

Three things unlock the rest. First, the question asked how Tajiri’s childhood interests led to Pokémon — so the first sentence should answer that, not just say who made it. Second, your paragraph never says the magic connection out loud: catching creatures in the game is exactly what he did with bugs as a kid. Third, your last line — “A game were you catch little creatures and throw balls” — is a describing phrase standing alone as a fragment. Here is the fun part: you already know the fix. It is an appositive — the exact skill you aced on Question 3. Attach it to the sentence before it with a comma, the same way you attached “a seal” to Sally.

YOUR PARAGRAPH, REBUILT (closer to 20/20)

“Tajiri’s childhood hobbies led him straight to Pokémon. Pokémon was made by someone named Tajiri. When Tajiri was little he loved bugs. When he got older he liked video games. But Tajiri wanted something better, so he thought about what he liked and came up with Pokémon, a game where you catch little creatures, just like he once caught bugs as a boy. The things he loved as a child became a game that the whole world plays.”

Look at what changed: an opening sentence that answers the question, your fragment tucked into the sentence before it as an appositive, the bugs-to-creatures connection said out loud, and a closing line. All of your facts, none of them moved.
