Fix 1: Answer First, then Show and So What

The passage’s first sentence is not your first sentence
THE RULE: ANSWER FIRST, THEN SHOW, THEN SO WHAT
The TOPIC SENTENCE answers the question in the question’s own words. Never the passage’s opening sentence. Then each fact is a SHOW from the passage, followed by a SO WHAT: This shows… how that part of the body keeps the porcupine safe. Then a CONCLUDING SENTENCE. The check: does your first sentence contain the question’s key words, body and safe? Then count the facts and count the This shows.

Fix 2: Read It Back Once

Plural s, its, and two sentences that lost their words
THE RULE: ONE READ-BACK, THREE QUESTIONS
Before you submit, read the paragraph back aloud in your head, one sentence at a time, and ask three things. Does the subject match its verb? Porcupines are, a porcupine is. Is it its or it’s? Its means belonging to it; it’s means it is. Does the sentence make sense read aloud? If a word is missing, you will hear the gap.
