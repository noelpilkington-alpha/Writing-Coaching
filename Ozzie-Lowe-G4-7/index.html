APOSTROPHE-S IS NOT PLURAL-S
visitor's means something belongs to a visitor (the visitor's ticket). More than one visitor is just visitors — no apostrophe. You clearly know the plural –s now (you got it right everywhere else on this test) — just do not let an apostrophe sneak into it.

The Last Habit: The Comma Before the Verb

The same comma, three answers in a row

Ozzie, you beat the comma splice — but a smaller comma habit took its place. Three times on G4.7, a comma landed between a subject and its own verb, right before “would” or “because”:

THE SAME COMMA, THREE TIMES
“One lesson I could share, would be to try to plant new bamboo forests.” (Q8)
“One way people could help the community, would be trying to build new bamboo forests.” (Q9)
“…giant pandas were in danger, because people would cut down bamboo forests.” (Q5)

In the first two, the comma splits the subject (“One lesson I could share”) from its verb (“would be”). Those two belong together — nothing goes between them. In the third, the comma separates the sentence from its own reason.

THE RULE
A subject and its verb are one unit — never put a single comma between them. And when “because” gives the reason the sentence needs, it runs straight on with no comma in front. Read your sentence out loud: if you would not truly pause there, no comma goes there.
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
AOne lesson I could share would be to protect the places animals live.
BOne lesson I could share, would be to protect the places animals live.
WHY THIS IS WORTH A WHOLE CARD
This comma is now your single most repeated pattern — it showed up three times in one test. It is also the easiest thing on this card to fix, because it always sounds wrong when you read the sentence out loud. One habit, one point, and it is the kind of point that decides an 89 versus a 91.

YOUR PARAGRAPH, REPAIRED (20/20 territory)

“Schools should keep recess because it helps students learn better, not just play. First, recess gives students a chance to exercise and take a little brain break. Second, recess helps students with their social skills. Recess can also improve a student’s mood. The article describes a test that showed students often do better in class when they have recess. This shows that recess is not wasted time. It actually helps learning. In conclusion, recess is very important to students, and that is why every school should keep it.”

SO WHAT?
Two moves for G4.8: Move 1 — open with the reason, not with “this is why” (point forward, not backward). Move 2 — when you cite a study or fact, give it its own clean sentence and follow it with “This shows that…” Your organization is already there. These two moves are the difference between 17 and 20.
