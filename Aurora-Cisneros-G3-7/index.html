The Echo Trick — Your Bridge from Q5 to Q6

The most important strategy on this card

Q1-Q5 asks you to fix sentences that already exist. Q6-Q10 asks you to write sentences from scratch. You already proved you can write perfect sentences — look at your answers on Q1 through Q5. The only difference is that Q6-Q10 need a starting point.

THE ECHO TRICK
Turn the question into the beginning of your answer. Then add "because" and one detail from the passage.

Watch how it works with your blank questions:

Echo + Because = Full-credit answer
Q6 "Why do pumpkin seeds need warm soil?" → "Pumpkin seeds need warm soil because..."
Q7 "Why do bees help pumpkins grow?" → "Bees help pumpkins grow because..."
Q8 "What changes as the pumpkin grows?" → "As the pumpkin grows, [thing] changes because..."
Q9 "What can people use pumpkins for?" → "People can use pumpkins for..."
Q10 "Why might someone save pumpkin seeds?" → "Someone might save pumpkin seeds because..."

All five blank questions follow the exact same pattern. Echo the question, add "because," finish with one detail from the passage. You already write perfect sentences — the Echo Trick just gives you the first few words.
