When a question asks "what does the passage teach," your answer should name:

• The specific technology (3D printing, not "new technology")
• The specific problem it solves (building cheaper homes, not "real-world problems")

Your Paragraph — 16/20

Good structure, needs more development to push higher

Kaiden, your paragraph scored 16/20 on G5.5. That is down from 17/20 on G5.4. The structure is still there — you have a topic sentence, evidence, and a closing. What is missing is development: explaining what your evidence means and why it matters.

WHAT "NEEDS MORE DEVELOPMENT" MEANS
It means: after you drop in a quote or fact from the passage, you need to explain it. Do not just list facts — tell the reader what the fact proves.

Think of it this way:
• Fact: "3D-printed homes can be built in 24 hours."
• Development: "This means families who cannot afford traditional construction can have a home much sooner, which solves the problem of long building timelines."

On G5.4 you used "This shows..." sentences well. On G5.5 they were shorter or missing. That is the exact move that pushes 16 to 18+.

How to turn 16/20 into 18+/20
THE PATTERN THAT PUSHES YOU HIGHER

Step 1 (Fact): "According to the passage, 3D-printed homes can be built in just 24 hours."

Step 2 (This shows...): "This shows that 3D printing solves the biggest problem with traditional housing — it takes too long and costs too much. Families can move in faster and spend less money."

The "This shows" sentence is where your score jumps. It is YOU explaining what the fact means. Without it, you are just listing. With it, you are analyzing.
