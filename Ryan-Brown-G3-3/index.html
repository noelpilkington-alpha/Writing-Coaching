MODEL PARAGRAPH (what 18/20 looks like)

Satoshi Tajiri's childhood obsession influenced his creation of Pokemon by combining his love of bugs with making video games. When Tajiri was young, he loved to collect bugs in the woods and keep them organized. He also spent hours playing early video games like Space Invaders at the arcade. Because he grew up doing both of these things, he built Pokemon so players could catch and collect creatures inside a video game. His childhood hobbies became the heart of a game millions of people play today.

The 4-Part Paragraph Shape
TOPIC State your main idea. Turn the prompt into a sentence. (You already do this well.)
DETAIL 1 One specific thing from the article that supports your idea
DETAIL 2 A second specific thing from the article
CLOSING Wrap back to your main idea in new words
Your topic sentence is great. What comes next in a paragraph?
A A brand new opinion about a different topic.
B Two specific details from the article, then a closing sentence.
THE "AND THEN" TRICK
When you have your topic sentence, ask yourself "what does the article say that proves this?" Write one detail. Then ask "and what else does it say?" Write a second detail. Then write one closing sentence that says the main idea again in new words. That is a paragraph.

Add a Reason to Every Opinion

Q6, Q7, and Q8 all earned partial credit — one small fix gets you full credit

Ryan, look at three of your opinion answers from G3.3. The prompts all asked you to explain why. You stated the opinion clearly, but you left out the "why."

Prompt: Would you have helped the seal? Explain your answer in one sentence.

FROM G3.3 Q8 (0.33 / 1)
Prompt: Was it a good idea to name the seal Sally? Explain your opinion in one sentence.
You wrote: "yes it was a good idea."
Same shape — opinion, no reason, and "yes" needs a capital Y.

Good news — this is the easiest fix in this whole review. When you see the word "explain," your answer needs a reason. The trick is to use the word "because."

THE "BECAUSE" TRICK FOR OPINIONS
Any opinion sentence gets full credit when you add "because" and a reason:

Opinion only (partial credit): Yes I would have helped the seal.
Opinion + reason (full credit): Yes I would have helped the seal because animals need people to rescue them when they are in danger.

Same opinion. One extra piece. Full credit.
Which answer would earn full credit on Q8?

THE CAPITAL-I FIX
One more small thing across Q6, Q7, and Q8: the word "I" is always a capital letter when it means you. "i would have helped" should be "I would have helped." Also, the first word of every sentence starts with a capital letter — so "yes it was a good idea" starts with "Yes".
