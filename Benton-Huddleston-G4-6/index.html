THE RULE: A REPAIRED FRAGMENT SAYS WHAT THE ORIGINAL WAS TRYING TO SAY
A fragment is a piece of a sentence from the passage. Fixing it means finding the sentence it belongs to and completing that idea, not inventing a new person or a new verb. The check: read the sentences on either side of the fragment. Does your repaired sentence say what they say? For Q6 to Q10, the check is simpler: read the question again and count what it asks for. One sentence has to hold all of it.
