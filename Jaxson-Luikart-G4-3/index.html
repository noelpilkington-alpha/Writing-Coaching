If you had read this sentence back after finishing the test, you would have immediately heard the problem. "Keep them inside so they CAN escape" makes no sense. Your ear catches logic flips that your eyes miss when you are writing fast.
Which sentence makes logical sense?
A One way to keep small animals safe is to keep them inside so they can escape and get hurt.
B One way to keep small animals safe is to keep them inside so they can't escape and get hurt.
THE TAKEAWAY
Logic flips are the most dangerous rushing error because the sentence still looks like a sentence. Your eyes glide past it. Only reading it back slowly reveals that it says the opposite of what you mean. This one missing word cost you 2 points.
