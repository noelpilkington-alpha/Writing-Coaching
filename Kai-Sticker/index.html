DEVELOPED VERSION (6 sentences)

TS: One effective way children learn new languages is through immersion classes.

E1: According to the passage, in immersion schools, students must use the new language all day — during lessons, group work, and even simple daily conversations like asking to go to the bathroom.

X1: This shows that students don't just study the language in one class period; instead, they are surrounded by it constantly, which makes learning feel natural rather than forced.

E2: The passage also explains that students learn new words because they hear the language in every part of class.

X2: As a result, vocabulary builds through daily exposure rather than memorization alone.

CS: Because of this constant practice, immersion is one of the most effective methods for language learning.

WHAT CHANGED (HIGHLIGHTED)
Specific evidence: The details now come straight from the passage — "lessons, group work, and daily conversations" instead of vague summaries.

Sentence strategies: X1 uses a semicolon to join two related ideas into one compound sentence, making it more sophisticated than two simple sentences back to back.

Cause-and-effect transitions: "As a result" and "Because of this" show why the evidence matters. These are stronger than "Also" or "They also" because they explain the connection between ideas.
