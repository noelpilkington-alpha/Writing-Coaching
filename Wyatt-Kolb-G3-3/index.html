Step 4: The Echo Trick

Turn fragments into real answers

These do not earn points because they are fragments or just copied words. They do not answer what the question asked. Here is the trick that fixes this:

THE ECHO TRICK
Step 1: Read the question.
Step 2: Start your answer by echoing (repeating) the question as a sentence.
Step 3: Finish the sentence with something from the passage.

Example:
Question: "Why did Sally swim to the other side?"
Echo start: "Sally swam to the other side because..."
Finish: "...she wanted to find food for her family."

Full answer: "Sally swam to the other side because she wanted to find food for her family."
The question asks: "Why did the seal go to the beach?" Which answer uses the Echo Trick?

THIS IS ALL IT TAKES
One real sentence that echoes the question and gives a reason. That is the difference between 0 points and 2 points on every single question. Ten questions at 2 points each = 20 points. From 7 to 20+ points just by using the Echo Trick.
