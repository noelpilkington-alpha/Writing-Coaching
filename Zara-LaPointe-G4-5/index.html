THE RULE
A sentence has ONE complete thought. When you feel yourself adding another full idea, you have three choices:

1. End the first sentence with a period and start a new one.
2. Keep them connected with a word like because, but, so, and, or which.
What does NOT work: a comma by itself, or nothing at all.
THE "READ IT OUT LOUD" TEST
Read your answer quietly to yourself. Every time you naturally pause, check if there is a period there. If you feel a pause but see only a comma (or nothing), that is a run-on. Add the period. One second of work, one full point saved.
Pick the sentence that is NOT a run-on.

B The powwow was fun. Everybody cheered loudly.

Question 5 Rewrites — Match the Tense, End With "?"

Your G4.5 Q5 rewrite was close, but two small misses cost you half credit

Zara, on G4.5 the prompt asked you to rewrite a sentence as a question. You picked a great question word ("Does") — but two small pieces tripped you up.

FROM G4.5 Q5 (Apr 2026) — 0.5/2.0
Prompt: "Rewrite the following sentence as a question: The shawl dance began when shawls replaced the blankets young women once wore."

You wrote: "Does the shawl dance begin when shawls replace the blankets young women once wore."
(Three small issues: wrong tense on "begin," wrong tense on "replace," and a period instead of a question mark at the end.)
Prompt: "Rewrite as a question: The shawl dance began when shawls replaced the blankets young women once wore." Which rewrite earns full credit?
A Does the shawl dance begin when shawls replace the blankets young women once wore.
B Did the shawl dance begin when shawls replaced the blankets young women once wore?
THE 3-STEP REWRITE CHECK
1. Match the time frame. If the original says "began" or "replaced" (past), use Did. If the original says "begins" or "replaces" (present), use Does.

2. Keep every original detail. Do not add words. Do not drop words.

3. End with "?" A question ALWAYS ends with a question mark. Never a period.
CORRECT REWRITE FOR G4.5 Q5
→ "Did the shawl dance begin when shawls replaced the blankets young women once wore?"

Past tense kept ("began" → "Did... begin"). Nothing added. Ends with "?".
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
This one question was worth 2 full points, and you got 1 of them. Catching the missing "?" is the easiest proofread fix there is — literally one character. Anytime you see a question word at the start of your answer (Did, Does, Is, Were, How, Why), the sentence MUST end with "?".
