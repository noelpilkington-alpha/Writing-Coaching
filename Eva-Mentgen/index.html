Plan Before You Write

An outline prevents circular paragraphs

Here's how a quick outline would have organized your essay's topics:

Essay Outline
INTRO Hook about food preferences → Thesis: People's food preferences develop through a flavor window in infancy, three types of learning, and gradual exposure over time.
BP1 Flavor window — E1: University of Birmingham, 4-6 months → E2: trying food 5-10 times helps toddlers accept it
BP2 Three learning types — E1: flavor-nutrient (cookie → energy) + taste-flavor (jelly on bread) → E2: social learning (watching family/friends)
BP3 Gradual change over time — E1: children start with sweets, adults prefer vegetables → E2: persistence and repeated exposure rewires preferences
CONC Restate 3 points → broader message about persistence and not giving up on new foods

REWRITTEN PARAGRAPH 4
[TS] Beyond specific learning methods, food preferences also change gradually as people grow older. [E1] The passage notes that young children naturally prefer sweet flavors, while adults tend to enjoy vegetables and other complex tastes. [X1] This shift doesn't happen overnight; it occurs because repeated exposure to new foods slowly rewires the brain's taste preferences over time. [E2] According to the passage, even people who once disliked a food can learn to enjoy it through persistence — trying it multiple times rather than giving up after the first bite. [X2] As a result, the key to developing a broader palate is patience and willingness to keep trying, which is why the passage emphasizes not giving up on foods like broccoli. [CS] Because taste is always changing, there is always an opportunity to learn to enjoy new foods at any age.

Notice what's different from your original:

  • E1 and E2 draw on specific passage details, not general claims
  • X1 and X2 explain why the evidence matters

Repeated misspellings: vegtables → vegetables (appeared twice), becuase → because (appeared twice), addapting → adapting. If you misspell a word once, you'll misspell it every time — fix it the first time.
