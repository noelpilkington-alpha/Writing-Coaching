Fix 1: The Prompt Is the Outline

Three ways in the prompt, three body paragraphs, a thesis that names them
THE RULE: THE PROMPT IS THE OUTLINE
The prompt tells you what the body paragraphs are. Three different ways the project changed the community means three body paragraphs, one way each, and a thesis that names the three. The check: before you write, copy the prompt’s question into the box and answer it in one sentence. Then read each topic sentence and ask: is this one of the ways? If a paragraph answers a different question, however well, it earns evidence points for nothing.
