Essay: Add Detail + Explanation

Your body paragraphs need more D+E to reach 18+

Cole, your essay has good structure and solid ideas, but the body paragraphs need more development. Specifically, each body paragraph should have at least one moment where you state a detail from the passage and then explain why it matters or what it shows.

💡 The D+E pattern: D = a specific detail, fact, or example. E = an explanation of why that detail matters, what it proves, or how it connects to your thesis. Every body paragraph needs at least one D+E pair.
D+E EXAMPLE
Detail: "According to the passage, the city planted over 500 trees along Main Street in 2019."

Explanation: "This matters because the trees provide shade that lowers the temperature by several degrees during summer, making the downtown area more comfortable for pedestrians and encouraging people to spend time outside."

The detail gives the evidence. The explanation shows the reader why it matters and connects it to the essay's argument.
YOUR ESSAY FIX
After writing each body paragraph, check: "Did I just state a fact, or did I also explain why it matters?" If you only stated the fact, add one more sentence that starts with:

• "This matters because..."
• "This shows that..."
• "As a result..."
• "This connects to [thesis] because..."
