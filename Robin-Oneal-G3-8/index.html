Q4 Fix — "Did + Base Verb"

When you use "did," the main verb drops its -ed

You got Q4 almost right. The only problem: when you use "did" to make a question, the main verb must stay in its base form (no -ed ending).

YOU WROTE
"Did Jordan asked his classmates to help organize?"

"Did" already shows the past tense, so the verb after it should be plain: ask, not "asked."

Tap to see the fixed version
Read the explanation above first
FIXED
"Did Jordan ask his classmates to help organize the shelves?"
THE RULE: DID + BASE VERB
When did starts the question, the next verb stays in base form — no -ed, no -s.

Did she walk to school? (not "walked")
Did they finish the project? (not "finished")
Did Jordan ask his classmates? (not "asked")

Spelling Spotlight — Compound Words

These common words are spelled as one word, not two

In several answers, you split compound words into two words. These are all single words:

"team work" → teamwork
"every thing" → everything
"some one" → someone
HOW TO REMEMBER
These words have been used together for so long that they became one word. Think of it like a team — the two parts joined up permanently.

Quick test: If you can say the word without pausing in the middle, it's probably one word. "Team...work" flows as one sound → teamwork.
