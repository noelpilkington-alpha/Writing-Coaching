Step 6: Topic Sentence Matching

Q7 (0/1) — you picked the enabler, not the result
🔎 Common trap on this passage: The solar panel passage has a paragraph where one sentence describes what made the project possible (the enabler) and another describes what the project achieved (the result). Many students pick the enabler. The topic sentence is the result because that is what the paragraph is mostly about.

Q7 (0/1): You chose the sentence about what enabled the solar panel installation. But the paragraph is about the impact of the panels on the school. The topic sentence should state the main outcome, not the process that led to it.

THE FIX: ASK "WHAT IS THIS PARAGRAPH MOSTLY ABOUT?"
Re-read the entire paragraph. Then ask: "Is this paragraph mostly about how the panels got installed, or about what they achieved?"

• The enabler answers: "How did it happen?"
• The result/topic sentence answers: "What is the main point?"

The topic sentence is always the one that states the main point the rest of the paragraph proves.
A paragraph discusses how solar panels reduced the school's energy bill by 40%. Which is the topic sentence?
A A community fundraiser raised enough money to cover the installation costs.
B The solar panels reduced the school's monthly energy costs by nearly 40 percent.
WHY THIS MATTERS
This 1 point seems small, but it is the same error type that many students make on this passage. At your level (9/10 on multiple choice), catching this one pattern is the difference between a perfect Q1-Q10 score and losing a point.

Step 4: Essay Development — The D+E Pattern

Q11 scored 15/20 — development is the unlock

Imara, your essay earned 15 out of 20. Your ideas are clear and your structure is solid. But the body paragraphs present evidence without fully explaining why it matters. At G7, the rubric expects that extra layer of analysis after each detail.

THE D+E PATTERN (DETAIL + ELABORATION)
After every piece of evidence, add one sentence that answers:

• Why does this matter?
• How does this connect to my main point?
• What does this show about the bigger idea?
YOUR PATTERN (EVIDENCE ONLY)
"The school installed solar panels on the roof. They saved money on eletricity bills every month."
DEVELOPED VERSION (EVIDENCE + ELABORATION)
"The school installed solar panels on the roof. They saved money on electricity bills every month. This means the school could redirect those savings toward new textbooks and after-school programs, directly benefiting students who had gone without updated materials for years."

The bolded sentence is the elaboration. It explains why the cost savings matter to the people in the passage. That is what turns 15/20 into 18+/20.

Which version shows proper development after the evidence?
A "The panels were installed in June. The school received a grant. The electricity bill went down."
B "After installation, the school's electricity bill dropped by 40%. This significant reduction meant that funds previously spent on energy could now support student programs that had been cut due to budget constraints."
YOUR SPELLING FIX
You wrote eletricity instead of electricity. Say it slowly: e-lec-tri-ci-ty. You hear the "c" after "ele." During your proofread (Step 5), read each word slowly and you will catch this.
