Detail + Explanation

The one move that pushes 14/20 toward 18/20

Jack, here is the exact pattern that earns the missing points. Each body paragraph needs passage evidence followed by a "This shows that..." sentence.

The Detail + Explanation Pattern
DETAIL A specific fact, number, or description pulled directly from the passage
EXPLAIN "This shows that..." — one sentence explaining what the detail proves about your thesis

Applied to the bridge/revitalization passage you wrote about on G8.2:

Example: Bridge Revitalization
DETAIL "The passage explains that after the bridge was cleaned up, local businesses reported a 30% increase in foot traffic."
EXPLAIN "This shows that even a small change like clearing weeds from a bridge can have a big economic effect on the surrounding community, proving that revitalization starts with one visible improvement."
A passage describes how volunteers repainted a bridge and foot traffic increased. Which body paragraph sentence earns more essay points?
A The volunteers repainted the bridge and more people started walking across it after that.
B After volunteers repainted the bridge, foot traffic doubled within a month. This shows that when people see a visible improvement in their neighborhood, it inspires confidence that the area is worth visiting again.
SENTENCE STARTERS THAT WORK
After every passage detail, use one of these to start your explanation:

• "This shows that..."
• "This matters because..."
• "This proves that..."
• "In other words, this means..."

Each one forces you to explain the significance of the detail rather than just restating it.

Build Each Body Paragraph

The outline: claim, evidence quote, explain what it proves

Jack, here is the full body paragraph formula. You already write a claim sentence. The upgrade is adding the evidence + explanation layer after it.

18/20 Body Paragraph Structure
CLAIM State the idea for this paragraph (you already do this well)
DETAIL A specific fact or quote from the passage that supports your claim
EXPLAIN "This shows that..." — what the detail proves about your thesis
DETAIL A second specific fact from the passage
EXPLAIN "This matters because..." — what the second detail proves
CLOSING Wrap back to your thesis: how this paragraph supports your main argument

Applied to your G8.2 essay about the bridge and community revitalization:

Example Body Paragraph (Bridge Passage)
CLAIM "One small change that led to a big effect was cleaning up the bridge itself."
DETAIL "The passage states that the bridge had been overrun by weeds and was considered an eyesore by residents for years."
EXPLAIN "This shows that the bridge had become a symbol of neglect, making people avoid the area entirely."
DETAIL "After volunteers cleared the weeds and repainted the railings, the passage notes that local shops reopened within months."
EXPLAIN "This matters because it proves that fixing one visible problem gave the whole community confidence that the neighborhood was worth investing in again."
CLOSING "The bridge cleanup shows that small, visible changes really can trigger big effects for an entire community."
THE DIFFERENCE BETWEEN 14 AND 18
Your current body paragraphs have the CLAIM and maybe one DETAIL. Adding the EXPLAIN sentence after each detail is the mechanical move that pushes you from 14 to 18. It is not about writing more words — it is about writing one sentence that answers "so what does this prove?"
