💡
The pattern: A "one sentence" answer has one subject + one main verb + one period at the end. If you find yourself writing "and she..." or "but she..." twice in the same answer, you probably have two sentences smashed together.
HOW TO WRITE A ONE-SENTENCE ANSWER
Build it in three steps:
1. Start with your main idea: "The most important moment was..."
2. Add ONE "because" or "when" or "that" to stretch it: "...when Layla saw the geode..."
3. Stop. Put a period. Re-read it to make sure it is one sentence with one period.
