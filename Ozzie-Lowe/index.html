Spelling Spotlight: "Because" & More

Q5, Q8, Q9, Q10 — The same misspelling keeps showing up

Ozzie, you misspelled "because" the same way five times across your test. Let's fix this pattern and your other common spelling errors.

The "because" pattern:

beacuse → because

You swapped the "a" and "u" every time. Remember: be-CAUSE. Think of the word "cause" inside it.

MEMORY TRICK
because = be + cause
The word "cause" is hiding inside "because." If you can spell "cause," just put "be" in front of it!

Other spelling errors in your test:

naem → name
amnimal → animal
attched → attached
strory → story
nevr gave → never gave up
thoought → thought
gaems → games
remberd → remembered
bug swith → bugs with
SPELLING CHECK STRATEGY
1. After you finish writing, go back and read each word one at a time.
2. Point to each word with your finger as you read it.
3. If a word looks wrong, try sounding it out slowly: be-cause, re-mem-bered, at-tached.
4. Watch out for switched letters (like "beacuse" and "naem") — your brain knows the right word, but your hand writes the letters out of order.
Practice: spell these words correctly
Try writing these five times each on a piece of paper:
because • animal • attached • remembered • thought
The more you write them correctly, the more your hand will remember the right order.

Issue 2: Informal tone

"Well he did" and "so where you catch bug swith balls" sound like talking, not writing. On a test, use a more formal, clear style.

Here's the G3 paragraph plan you should follow:

Paragraph Plan — TS → D1 → W → D2 → W → D3 → W → CS
TS = Topic Sentence  •  D = Detail from the article  •  W = Why it matters  •  CS = Concluding Sentence
TS Satoshi Tajiri's love for catching bugs as a kid led him to create Pokémon.
D1 As a child, Tajiri spent hours catching bugs — his friends called him "Dr. Bug."
W This shows how much he loved collecting creatures, which became the main idea behind Pokémon.
D2 When he grew up, he started playing video games but thought they were not fun enough.
W This explains why he decided to create his own game that combined bugs and video games.
D3 He remembered his childhood love of catching bugs and turned it into Pokémon.
W This shows that his childhood hobby inspired one of the most popular games in the world.
CS Tajiri's childhood love of nature led him to create a game that millions of people enjoy today.
KEY TAKEAWAYS
1. Fix spelling before you turn in your test — especially "because."
2. Read each sentence out loud. If two ideas run together, split them with a period.
3. After each detail, add a "why it matters" sentence.
4. Use a formal writing voice, not a speaking voice.
