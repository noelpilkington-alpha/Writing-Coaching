THE RULE: PROMISE, POINT, THEN THIS SHOWS
The TOPIC SENTENCE names what the facts will prove, in the question’s own words. Every supporting detail is a SHOW you can put your finger on in the passage, then a SO WHAT: This shows… what the fact reveals. Three checks: Does each fact prove what the topic sentence promised? Could you point to each fact in the passage, not in your memory? Does each fact have its This shows?
