If your answer only covers Part 1, you will lose points. Check both parts are there.

THE QUESTION MARK RULE
If a sentence starts with a question word (Did, What, How, Why, When, Where, Is, Are, Can, Will), it is a question and needs a question mark at the end. Not a period.

• "Did Layla find the geode?" (correct)
• "Did Layla find the geode." (incorrect)
