EXAMPLE 3 OF 3 — FROM G3.6 Q8 (scored 0.67/3)
Prompt: What do you think was the most important moment in the story? Explain in one sentence.
You wrote: "I think the most important moment in the story was when Layla took the geode to the guide."
This tells what happened but not why it was important. Every "explain" question needs a "because..." clause telling the reader the reason.
THE FIX: "FACT + BECAUSE"
When the prompt has the word "explain" or "why," your sentence must have two parts:

Part 1: The fact (what happens / what you think).
Part 2: A "because..." clause that gives the reason.

✗ "Something that changes is the flowers." (only Part 1)
✓ "The flowers change color and fall off because the pumpkin no longer needs to attract bees once it has been pollinated." (both parts)
From G3.7 Q10 — which answer earns full credit?
A The spring is the time with warm soil, so then the seeds can open.
B Someone might save pumpkin seeds to plant in spring because the warm spring soil helps the seeds sprout into new pumpkin plants.
