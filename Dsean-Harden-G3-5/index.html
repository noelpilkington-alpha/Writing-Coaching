Fix 2: Build the Paragraph

Topic sentence, two supporting details, concluding sentence. A box that is never empty again
THE RULE: TOPIC SENTENCE, SUPPORTING DETAILS, CONCLUDING SENTENCE
Six sentences. The topic sentence gives the answer (echo the question, like card 3). Then two supporting details. Each supporting detail is a SHOW sentence, one fact from the passage, followed by a SO WHAT sentence that says what the fact proves and starts with This shows. The concluding sentence says the answer again in new words. The check: count your facts. Fewer than two, and the paragraph is not finished.

The question you were asked: Explain why Jason chose to place his sculptures under the sea.
