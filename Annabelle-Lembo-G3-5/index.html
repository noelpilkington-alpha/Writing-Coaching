Error: Spelling Precision

This is a Step 5 skill: proofread before submitting

Annabelle, on Q8 you had two spelling errors that cost you a point:

Q8 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"The museum is better then other places becasue you can see real art underwater."

Fix 1: "Then" should be than. "Then" means "after that." "Than" is for comparisons ("better than").

Fix 2: "Becasue" should be because. The letters "a" and "u" are swapped.

REMEMBER: THEN vs. THAN
• Than = comparing things ("bigger than," "better than," "more than")
• Then = time/sequence ("first... then..." or "back then")

If you are saying one thing is MORE or BETTER, use than.
Which sentence uses "than" correctly?
A"Swimming in the museum is more exciting then swimming in a regular pool."
B"Swimming in the museum is more exciting than swimming in a regular pool."

Using the sunken museum passage
FULLY DEVELOPED PARAGRAPH (18-20/20 level)

The sunken museum is special because it combines art and ocean life in a way no other museum can. For example, divers can swim around statues that are covered in real coral and surrounded by colorful fish. This shows that the museum is not just a place to look at art, but also a living habitat where sea creatures have made a home. Another detail is that the sculptures were designed to attract ocean life on purpose. This means that the artists planned for nature to become part of the artwork over time. That is what makes the sunken museum unlike any museum on land.

Which paragraph is fully developed?
A"The sunken museum is cool. You can see statues underwater."
B"The sunken museum is special because it brings art and nature together. Divers can see statues covered in coral. This shows the museum is a living habitat. The artists designed it this way on purpose. This means nature is part of the art. That is why this museum is unlike any other."
