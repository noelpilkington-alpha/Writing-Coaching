Answer the Question Word: Why vs. How

Question 9 — 1 of 3. The fix is one second of reading.
🔍 Pattern alert: Your sentence was complete and correct — it just answered the wrong question. The prompt asked why the family works together, and your answer told how they do it.

Julian, Question 9 asked for the reason the Downwind family works together before each powwow. Here is what you wrote:

YOU WROTE (G4.5 Q9 — scored 1 of 3)
"The Downwind family works together before each powwow by making a hand made dress."

That sentence is clear and complete — but "by making a hand made dress" answers how they work together, not why. The question word was why, which wants a reason, usually joined with "because."

The question asks: Why does the family work together before the powwow? Which answer matches the question word?
A The family works together by making a handmade jingle dress.
B The family works together before the powwow because sewing the jingle dress is a tradition that keeps them close.
THE FIX: MATCH THE QUESTION WORD
Before you answer, read the question word and aim your sentence right at it.

• Why? → give a reason. Start the second half with because.
• How? → give a method or the steps. "by doing..." fits here.
• What? → name the thing exactly.
• Who? → name the person.

Your sentence on Q9 was a great how answer — it was just answering a why question. One second of reading the question word fixes it every time.
YOUR SENTENCE, AIMED AT "WHY" (full-credit version)
"The Downwind family works together before each powwow because sewing the jingle dress by hand is a tradition that brings the whole family closer."

• "This shows..." — what the detail proves
• Closing that wraps back to your main idea
