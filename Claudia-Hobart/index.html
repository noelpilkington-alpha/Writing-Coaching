The Body Paragraph Pattern

How to add explanation after every piece of evidence

Every body paragraph in a G7+ essay should follow this pattern:

Body Paragraph Formula
TS Topic Sentence — states the paragraph's main point and ties to the thesis
E1 Evidence 1 — a specific fact, quote, or example from the passage
X1 Explanation 1 — explain why that evidence shows connection
E2 Evidence 2 — a second fact or example from the passage
X2 Explanation 2 — explain why that evidence shows connection
CS Concluding Sentence — wraps up the paragraph by restating the connection

EXAMPLE: P3 WITH FULL PATTERN
[TS] El Silbo connects the people of La Gomera to one another because its unique whistling system allows messages to travel across the island's rugged valleys. [E1] According to the passage, each letter of the Spanish alphabet is replaced by a whistle with a different pitch or length, and these whistles can travel almost two miles. [X1] This means that people on opposite sides of a deep valley could send messages, invitations, and warnings without walking for hours — El Silbo turned the island's difficult terrain into a bridge instead of a barrier. [E2] The passage also explains that people used El Silbo to communicate messages, invitations, and warnings across the landscape. [X2] By sharing daily information through whistles, communities that might otherwise have been isolated stayed connected, working together even when mountains separated them. [CS] El Silbo's ability to cross distances that voices cannot is what made it such a powerful tool for keeping the people of La Gomera united.

CONC Restate 3 connections → broader message about language and identity

BP = Body Paragraph • E = Evidence • X = Explanation • TS = Topic Sentence • CS = Closing Sentence

THE KEY RULE
Every E (evidence) must be followed by an X (explanation) that connects back to the thesis. You already include good evidence — the missing piece is the X. After each piece of evidence, ask: "So what? How does this show that El Silbo connects people?" Answer that question in your next sentence.
