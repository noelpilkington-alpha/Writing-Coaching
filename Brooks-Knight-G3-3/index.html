Connectors: "Since" vs "But"

Step 2: identify whether the question asks for a reason or a contrast

Brooks, on Q5 you used "But" where the question needed "Since." This is about picking the right connector for the job.

Q5 (1/2) — YOUR ANSWER
You used "But" to connect two ideas, but the second idea gives a reason for the first — so the correct connector is "Since" or "Because."
THE CONNECTOR RULE
• "Because" / "Since" = the second idea explains why the first idea is true.
• "But" / "However" = the second idea contrasts with (is different from) the first idea.
• "So" / "Therefore" = the second idea is a result of the first idea.

Quick test: Ask "Does the second part explain why?" If yes, use "because" or "since."
Which connector fits? "_____ it was raining, we decided to stay inside."
A But it was raining, we decided to stay inside.
B Since it was raining, we decided to stay inside.
YOUR QUICK CHECK
Before you pick a connector, ask: "Does the second part explain why, show a contrast, or show a result?" Match the connector to the relationship.

Appositives and Comma Splices

Two different comma skills from Q3 and Q4

Brooks, Q3 asked for an appositive and Q4 had a comma splice. These are both about how commas work in sentences.

Q3 (1/2) — APPOSITIVE ISSUE
The question asked you to combine sentences using an appositive (a renaming phrase between commas). You used "because" instead. An appositive renames a noun — it does not explain why.
APPOSITIVE PATTERN
"Maya, a talented artist, painted a mural on the school wall."

The phrase "a talented artist" renames Maya. It sits between commas right after the noun it renames.
Q4 (1/2) — COMMA SPLICE
You joined two complete sentences with just a comma. Two full sentences need a period, or a connector word like "and" or "because."
FIXING A COMMA SPLICE
Wrong: "The dog ran fast, it caught the ball."
Right: "The dog ran fast, and it caught the ball."
Also right: "The dog ran fast. It caught the ball."
TWO DIFFERENT SKILLS
Appositives: A phrase between commas that renames the noun before it. Pattern: Noun, renaming phrase, rest of sentence.

Comma splices: Two complete sentences joined by only a comma = wrong. Fix by adding "and"/"because" or using a period.
