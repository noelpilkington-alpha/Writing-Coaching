Error #1: Appositive Structure

This is a Step 2 skill: the question is testing appositives

Alyan, both Q4 and Q5 tested your understanding of appositives. On Q4 you used "and" instead of an appositive. On Q5 you misidentified which part of the sentence was the appositive.

Q4 — YOUR ANSWER (1 out of 2 weighted points)
"Little Golden Books and affordable children's stories were popular with families."

The problem: Using "and" makes it sound like two different things. An appositive renames the noun, set off by commas, showing they are the same thing.

CORRECT APPOSITIVE
"Little Golden Books, affordable children's stories, were popular with families."

Now "affordable children's stories" renames "Little Golden Books." They are the same thing, explained with commas.

THE APPOSITIVE PATTERN
Noun, [renaming phrase], rest of sentence.

The renaming phrase comes AFTER the noun, between two commas. It tells the reader what the noun IS. If you use "and," you are listing two separate things, not renaming one thing.
Which sentence uses a correct appositive?
A "The sunken museum and an underwater attraction opened last year."
B "The sunken museum, an underwater attraction, opened last year for divers to explore."
WHY THIS MATTERS
Q4 and Q5 together are worth 4 weighted points. You earned 2 out of 4. The appositive pattern is the same every time: Noun, [renaming phrase], rest of sentence. Lock this pattern in and those points come back.

Error #3: Fragment Sentence

This is a Step 2 skill: the question is testing complete sentences

Alyan, on Q9 your answer started with "Because" and never completed the thought. A sentence that starts with "Because" needs a second part to be complete.

Q9 — YOUR ANSWER (2 out of 3 weighted points)
"Because the statues are covered in coral and sea creatures live around them."

The problem: This is a fragment. "Because..." introduces a reason, but you never stated what the reason leads to. It answers "why" but never says what happens as a result.

COMPLETE SENTENCE
"The museum is good for ocean life because the statues are covered in coral and sea creatures live around them."

Now the sentence has two parts: the main idea ("The museum is good for ocean life") and the reason ("because the statues are covered in coral...").

THE "BECAUSE" RULE
A sentence that starts with "Because" is only complete if it has a comma and a second part:

• Fragment: "Because the statues are covered in coral."
• Complete: "Because the statues are covered in coral, sea creatures have a new home."

OR put "Because" in the middle:
• Complete: "Sea creatures have a new home because the statues are covered in coral."
Which sentence is complete (not a fragment)?
A "Because the books were affordable and had colorful pictures."
B "Families bought many copies because the books were affordable and had colorful pictures."
