THE RULE: TOPIC SENTENCE, SUPPORTING DETAILS, CONCLUDING SENTENCE
Six sentences. The topic sentence gives the answer. Then two supporting details. Each supporting detail is a SHOW sentence, one fact from the passage, followed by a SO WHAT sentence that says what the fact proves and starts with This shows. The concluding sentence says the answer again in new words. The check: count your facts. Fewer than two, and the paragraph is not finished.
