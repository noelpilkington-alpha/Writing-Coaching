Add the Insight, Don't Restate

Q7: your answer must say something the question didn't already say

Beau, Question 7 was your lowest score — 1 out of 3 — and it is worth understanding exactly why. The question asked what the passage teaches us about working together. Here is your answer:

FROM G4.9 Q7 (1 out of 3)
"One thing it tells us is that the ancient Egyptions could acomplish most of their goals by working together."
See the problem? The question asked what the passage teaches about working together — and the answer says "they could accomplish goals by working together." That is the question's own words, handed back. It adds nothing new.

When a question asks "what does this teach us" or "what does this show," the grading is looking for a new idea from the passage — something specific that the question did not already say. The passage gave you the material: some workers cut the blocks, others moved them into place, and thousands of people built something no single person could.

Which answer would earn full credit?
A One thing it teaches us is that the ancient Egyptians could accomplish their goals by working together.
B It teaches that by splitting up the work, with some workers cutting stone blocks and others moving them into place, thousands of ordinary people could build something no one person could build alone.
THE RULE
Your answer must add something the question did not already say.

✗ Question asks about working together → answer says "they worked together." (Circle — nothing new.)
✓ Question asks about working together → answer names how the teamwork worked: splitting the jobs, the huge number of workers, building beyond any one person.

The insight lives in the passage — your job is to pull it out and name it.
A FULL-CREDIT VERSION OF YOUR Q7
"It teaches that by dividing the work, with some workers cutting blocks and others moving them, thousands of ordinary workers could build something no one could build alone."
One complete sentence — and it earns full credit because it adds the specific insight: division of labor at a massive scale.
QUICK CHECK
After you write a "what does this teach/show" answer, ask: did I add a fact or idea the question didn't already say? If your answer would still make sense with the question's words deleted from it, you probably restated. Go back to the passage and grab one specific detail.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Q7 is worth 3 points, and the gap between restating (1/3) and adding one specific insight (3/3) is two full points — on this test, that alone is the difference between 84% and 88%.
