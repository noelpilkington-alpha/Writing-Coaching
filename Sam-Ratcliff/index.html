The Body Paragraph Pattern

Make every paragraph as strong as your Paragraph 4

Your Paragraph 4 (citizen scientists) was your best — it had a topic sentence, multiple details, and stayed focused. Here's how to make all three body paragraphs that strong:

THE PATTERN

TS — Topic Sentence (one way people help)

E1 — Evidence from the passage ("The passage explains that...")

X1 — Explanation ("This helps because..." / "This shows that...")

E2 — More evidence ("The passage also says...")

X2 — Explanation ("This means that...")

CS — Concluding Sentence (why this way of helping matters)

TS → E1 → X1 → E2 → X2 → CS

Your Paragraph 3 (way stations) had the TS and E1 but was missing the X1, E2, X2, and CS — that's why it felt short. Your Paragraph 4 came closer to this pattern, which is why it was stronger.

PLANNING TRICK
Before writing, jot down:
Body 1: [way of helping] — 2 details from the passage
Body 2: [way of helping] — 2 details from the passage
Body 3: [way of helping] — 2 details from the passage

If you can't find 2 details for a paragraph, it might be background — not a body paragraph.
