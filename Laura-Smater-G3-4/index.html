Q11: all the facts, in order, and no answer to the question
THE RULE: SENTENCE ONE ANSWERS THE QUESTION AND NAMES TWO REASONS; EACH FACT GETS A THIS SHOWS
When the question asks how something developed and became popular, a retelling in date order lists the facts and never answers. The TOPIC SENTENCE answers with the two reasons the paragraph will prove. Each reason gets a DETAIL · SHOW, a fact from the article, and a DETAIL · SO WHAT: This shows plus what the fact proves. The CONCLUDING SENTENCE puts the two reasons together. The check: could a reader say your two reasons after sentence one? After each fact, does the next sentence say what it shows, or is it the next date?

THE RULE: COVER THE COMMA. IF BOTH SIDES ARE WHOLE SENTENCES, A PERIOD OR A JOINING WORD GOES THERE
Five of these on G3.3, three on G3.4. The check is the same: put your finger over the comma and read each side alone.
