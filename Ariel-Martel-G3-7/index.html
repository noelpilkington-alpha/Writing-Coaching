THE RULES — MEMORIZE THESE 3
there = a place. "Put it over there." Memory trick: "there" has "here" in it. Both are places.

their = they own it. "The kids left their shoes." Memory trick: "their" has "heir" (someone who inherits things) in it.

they're = they are. "They're coming home." The apostrophe replaces the missing "a."

are = a verb (plural “is”). "They are happy."
our = we own it. "That is our dog."
Which sentence uses "are/our" correctly?
A We play with are dogs at the park.
B We play with our dogs at the park.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
You have done this in 5 out of 7 tests. Once you learn the trick (there has "here," their has "heir"), you’ll never mix them up again. This one fix is worth 1–3 points per test — and it stays fixed forever.

Vary Your Sentence Openers

You start every Q6–Q10 answer the same way. Let's change that.

THE 3 UPGRADE OPENERS
Try these instead — they make your answers sound more confident:

✓ "The most important thing is..." (instead of "I think the most important is...")
✓ "One reason is that..." (instead of "I think because...")
✓ "[Claim]. This matters because..." (splits the thought into a real sentence + a real reason)
Which answer is stronger on a Q6–Q10 response?

BEFORE & AFTER — YOUR SAMPLE ANSWERS

Before: "I think that the lesen is follow your dream, because the man did that."

MODEL POLISHED PARAGRAPH (19–20/20)

Reading is important for many reasons. For example, people can read to learn new things about science, animals, and history. This shows how books help us understand the world without having to travel to see it. In addition, books can take you to new worlds and teach important lessons. This means even one book can change how a person thinks or feels. That is why reading is such a helpful part of life.

Your G3.7 Formula — Use It Every Time
TOPIC Name the main idea ("X is important" or "X has many ways")
DETAIL 1 "For example, + [fact from passage]"
SHOWS "This shows how..." or "This means..."
DETAIL 2 "In addition, + [second fact]"
SHOWS "This shows..." or "This means..."
CLOSE Restate the main idea in new words
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?
Your G3.7 paragraph already hit 17/20. Add 2 “this shows…” sentences using the formula above, and you’re at 19/20 easily — which is 2–3 points, on the biggest question of the whole test.
