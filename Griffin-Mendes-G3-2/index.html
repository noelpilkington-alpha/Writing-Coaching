Question 11 — Your Paragraph

"The Camel Mobile Library" — 12/20 points
Developing: 12/20 4 sentences Fragment issues
Which of these is a complete sentence?
A By helping people learn even if they are moving from one place to another.
B The Camel Mobile Library has affected the people in Garissa.

Griffin, here is what you wrote:

TOPIC (good start, but vague) The Camel Mobile Library has affected the people in Garissa.

FRAGMENT By helping people learn even if they are moving from one place to another.

DETAIL (lowercase start, underdeveloped) also the children love the books.

CLOSING (lowercase start, repeats topic) that is how the Camel Mobile Library affected people in Garissa.
THREE THINGS TO FIX
1. Fragment: "By helping people learn..." is not a complete sentence. Attach it to the sentence before it: "The Camel Mobile Library has affected the people in Garissa by helping people learn even when they are moving from one place to another."

2. Capitalize sentence starts: "also" and "that" both start new sentences and need capital letters: "Also" and "That."

3. Add specific details: "the children love the books" is vague. What books? Why do they love them? Use details from the passage to make this stronger.
SO WHAT? WHY DOES THIS MATTER?

Question 11 — Stronger Writing

How to push from 12/20 to 18/20 or higher
Which paragraph uses stronger evidence from the passage?
A The library has affected people. The children love the books. That is how the library affected people.
B The Camel Mobile Library has helped the people of Garissa by bringing books to families who move from place to place. According to the passage, camels carry boxes of books to nomadic communities so that children can read even when they are far from a school. When the camels arrive, children gather excitedly to pick out books. This shows that the library has made reading possible for people who would otherwise have no access to books.

Here is a model paragraph that shows how to improve your response:

Paragraph Blueprint
TOPIC The Camel Mobile Library has helped the people of Garissa by bringing books to families who move from place to place.
DETAIL 1 According to the passage, camels carry boxes of books to nomadic communities so that children can read even when they are far from a school.
DETAIL 2 When the camels arrive, children gather excitedly to pick out books they want to read.
CLOSING This shows that the library has made reading possible for people who would otherwise have no access to books.
THE EVIDENCE SANDWICH
1. Set it up: Write a sentence that introduces your evidence.
2. Evidence: Use specific details from the passage (quoted or paraphrased).
3. Explain: Tell the reader why this evidence matters or what it shows.
