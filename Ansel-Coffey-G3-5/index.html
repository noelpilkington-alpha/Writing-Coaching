Q11: Just Start Writing

A short paragraph earns far more than a blank answer

Ansel, Q11 is worth 20 points. Even a basic paragraph with a few sentences can earn 10 to 14 points. That is the difference between 50% and 75%+ on the whole test.

💡 Key insight: You do not need a perfect paragraph to earn most of the points. You just need to put something on the page. A paragraph with a topic sentence, two details, and a closing sentence earns strong marks even if it is short.

Here is what a quick attempt looks like using the Single Paragraph Outline:

Single Paragraph Outline (Step 6)
T.S. Answer the question in one sentence.
S.D. Give one reason or example from the passage.
S.D. Give a second reason or example.
C.S. Restate your answer in a different way.
EXAMPLE: A SIMPLE 4-SENTENCE PARAGRAPH (14–16/20 LEVEL)
T.S.: "Recycling helps the environment in many important ways."
S.D.: "For example, when people recycle plastic bottles, fewer bottles end up in the ocean where they can harm animals."
S.D.: "Another reason recycling helps is that it saves trees because recycled paper means fewer trees need to be cut down."
C.S.: "That is why recycling is an important habit for everyone to learn."
