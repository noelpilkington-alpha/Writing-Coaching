THE G3 PARAGRAPH FORMULA
A strong G3 paragraph has these parts:

1. Topic sentence – Tell the reader what the paragraph is about.
2. Detail + explanation – Give a fact from the passage, then explain what it shows.
3. Another detail + explanation – A second fact with another "because" or "this shows that."
4. Closing sentence – Wrap up by restating your main point in different words.

Right now your paragraph has the topic sentence and the details. What it needs is the explanation after each detail. Every time you write a fact, follow it with "This shows that..." or "This is important because..." That is the difference between 13/20 and 17 or 18 out of 20.

MODEL PARAGRAPH WITH EXPLANATIONS

Topic sentence: The Teddy Bear Toss is an exciting tradition that shows how much fans care about their team.

Detail + explanation: Every time The Bears score a goal, fans throw teddy bears onto the ice. This shows that the fans are so happy about the goal that they want to celebrate in a big, fun way that everyone in the arena can see.

Detail + explanation: After the game, the team collects all the teddy bears and donates them to children in need. This is important because it means the tradition is not just about having fun at the game, but also about helping the community.

Closing: The Teddy Bear Toss brings fans together and helps kids at the same time, which is why it has become one of the most popular traditions in hockey.
